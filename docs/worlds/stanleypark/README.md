# Stanley Park Seawall
The Seawall. A curving waterside race between cedars and sea, sprinting toward the green Lions Gate Bridge.

## 10 critical details
1. **Seawall path** — narrow paved path on a stone wall, painted lane line, sea right beside the rider.
2. **Lions Gate Bridge** — green suspension towers and cables grow ahead as the sprint target.
3. **Brockton Point totem poles** — cluster of painted totem poles in a clearing with visitors.
4. **Siwash Rock** — sea stack with a lone tree on top, standing just off the path.
5. **Cliffs and cedars** — basalt cliffs and tall western red cedars overhang the inland side.
6. **North Shore mountains** — dark forested peaks across Burrard Inlet fill the far horizon.
7. **Brockton Point lighthouse** — small red-and-white lighthouse at the point by the path.
8. **Rocky shoreline** — boulders, kelp and tide pools between wall and water.
9. **Ships and sailboats** — freighters anchored in English Bay and white sails on the water.
10. **Cool Pacific light** — soft blue sky, haze over the water, sun glints on waves.
