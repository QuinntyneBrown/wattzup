# Sixth Street, Austin
Sixth Street. A neon-lit night crit down a closed party street, crowds and live music pressing in on both sides.

## 10 critical details
1. **Victorian commercial blocks** — two- and three-storey brick and limestone fronts with arched windows and cornices.
2. **Neon bar signs** — vertical signs like the Ritz and Driskill glow above the street.
3. **Closed street crowds** — barricades and packed revellers spilling off sidewalks into the road at night.
4. **Live-music venues** — open doors and balconies with bands, stages visible through windows.
5. **Star-and-wire street decorations** — Texas-star lights strung across the street between lamp posts.
6. **Driskill Hotel** — ornate Romanesque hotel at Brazos Street as a corner landmark.
7. **Downtown towers** — glass high-rises and the Frost Bank tower rising behind the low old blocks.
8. **Light trails and street lamps** — warm streetlights and car-light streaks give speed at night.
9. **Murals and painted facades** — colourful painted walls on East Sixth.
10. **Festival kit** — food trucks, pedicabs, police barricades and SXSW banners along the curb.
