# Adelaide Hills
The Willunga road. A hot, aggressive summer stage race through vineyards and gum trees, ending up Willunga Hill.

## 10 critical details
1. **Old Willunga Hill** — steady 3 km climb with grassy verges, white edge lines and roadside fans.
2. **McLaren Vale vineyards** — neat rows of vines running to dry golden hills beside the road.
3. **Gum trees** — tall pale-trunked eucalypts shading road edges, bark peeling in strips.
4. **Dry summer grass** — golden paddocks and brown verges under hot January sun.
5. **Country roads** — smooth chip-seal with gravel shoulders, yellow diamond warning signs.
6. **Stone villages** — sandstone cottages, verandahs and churches in Willunga and Hahndorf.
7. **Tour Down Under kit** — inflatable giant bikes, orange expo tents and barriers on course.
8. **Roadside crowds** — fans in hats and costumes, camper vans and eskies on the hill.
9. **Gulf St Vincent views** — blue sea glimpsed over the vines from higher ground.
10. **Harsh bright light** — clear blue sky, heat haze and sharp shadows at midday.
