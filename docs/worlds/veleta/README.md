# Pico Veleta
The Sierra Nevada road. A long, thin-air grind up Europe's highest paved road, hairpins stacking toward a bare summit above Granada.

## 10 critical details
1. **Endless hairpins** — the A-395 and old Veleta road looping up the slopes in long switchbacks.
2. **Bare high mountains** — treeless grey-brown scree, slate and rock above 2,500 m, the summit pyramid ahead.
3. **Snow patches** — lingering snowfields and gullies beside the road even in summer.
4. **Pradollano ski resort** — the ski village, lifts and pylons passed on the way up.
5. **Thin-air sky** — deep, intense blue high-altitude sky and razor-sharp light.
6. **Road surface** — smooth tarmac lower down, broken narrow surface and barrier gates near the top.
7. **Views to Granada** — the city, Vega and distant Alhambra hazy far below.
8. **Observatories** — the IRAM radio telescope dish and the Sierra Nevada observatory domes on the ridge.
9. **Low pines lower down** — scattered pines and broom on the lower slopes thinning to nothing.
10. **Mulhacén and the ridge** — the neighbouring peaks and ridges, with Sierra Nevada's high glacial lakes.
