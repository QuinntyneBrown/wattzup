# Sea Point, Cape Town
Sea Point Promenade. Fast seaside laps on a wide promenade, the Atlantic on one side and the mountains on the other.

## 10 critical details
1. **Lion's Head** — the pointed granite peak rising directly above the apartment blocks inland.
2. **Table Mountain** — the flat-topped massif behind, sometimes with its tablecloth cloud.
3. **Atlantic waves** — rolling surf breaking on dark rocks beside the promenade.
4. **Wide promenade** — broad paved walkway with lawns, the road running alongside.
5. **Signal Hill** — the long green hill (the lion's rump) beside Lion's Head.
6. **Rocky shoreline** — black rocks and kelp at the water's edge, tidal pools.
7. **Palm trees and lawns** — tall palms and green grass strips along the promenade.
8. **Apartment blocks** — white mid-rise flats facing the sea along Beach Road.
9. **Walkers and joggers** — busy promenade with runners, dogs and families.
10. **Mouille Point lighthouse** — red-and-white striped lighthouse at the city end; plus the Sea Point pavilion pool.
