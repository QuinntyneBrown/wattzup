# Mulholland Drive
Mulholland Drive. Rolling ridge-top curves with Los Angeles spread out below at every bend.

## 10 critical details
1. **Winding ridge road** — narrow two-lane tarmac with yellow centre lines and blind curves.
2. **City views** — the LA basin and downtown skyline below.
3. **San Fernando Valley** — the grid of the valley on the other side.
4. **Hollywood Sign** — visible on Mount Lee.
5. **Overlooks** — scenic pull-outs with railings and viewers.
6. **Chaparral hills** — dry golden grass, sage and scrub oak.
7. **Hillside houses** — modernist homes perched on stilts and gated driveways.
8. **Palms and eucalyptus** — tall palms and gum trees along the road.
9. **Golden hour** — warm late-afternoon sun and haze over the city.
10. **Street furniture** — yellow curve signs, guardrails and wooden power poles.
