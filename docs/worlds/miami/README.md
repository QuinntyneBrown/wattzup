# Miami Neon Nights
The neon boulevard. A slick, glowing night sprint down a rain-wet beachfront strip, neon smeared across the asphalt at full speed.

## 10 critical details
1. **Art deco hotels in neon** — Ocean Drive facades (Colony, Breakwater, Carlyle) traced in pink, blue and green neon
2. **Wet road reflections** — rain-slick asphalt mirroring neon signs, headlights and palm silhouettes
3. **Royal and coconut palms** — tall palms lining the boulevard, uplit from below at night
4. **Moonlit Atlantic** — the dark ocean, white surf and moon glow across Lummus Park beach on one side
5. **Convertibles and classic cars** — slow-cruising convertibles and vintage cars parked along the kerb
6. **Lit skyline ahead** — Brickell and downtown towers glowing across Biscayne Bay
7. **Pastel by day, glow by night** — mint, peach and lavender facades with porthole windows and eyebrows
8. **Sidewalk cafes and crowds** — umbrellas, diners, strollers and music spilling onto the pavement
9. **Lifeguard towers** — the colourful South Beach lifeguard huts on the sand, glimpsed between palms
10. **Humid night atmosphere** — haze around streetlights, puddles, steam and a warm tropical glow
