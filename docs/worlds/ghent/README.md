# Ghent Six-Day
The Kuipke. Relentless indoor laps on steep wooden banking, the crowd and the music lifting the pace every lap.

## 10 critical details
1. **Wooden boards** — 166 m pine track, plank lines visible, with blue côte d'azur band and black/red lines.
2. **Steep banking** — the bends tilt sharply; the camera must lean with the riders through each turn.
3. **Tight, short oval** — a very small track: the next bend is always in view.
4. **Packed stands** — fans pressed to the rail on every side, rising steeply above the boards.
5. **Infield bars** — the track centre is full of bar tables, beer and spectators under lights.
6. **Roof and lights** — the low Kuipke roof with banks of floodlights; a warm, smoky indoor glow.
7. **Lap counter board** — big lap board and bell; the count drives each effort.
8. **Madison relays** — riders slinging teammates and resting high on the banking.
9. **Advertising hoardings** — sponsor boards ringing the rail and track edge.
10. **Night-time atmosphere** — late evening, beer and Belgian brass music; never daylight.
