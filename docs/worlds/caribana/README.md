# Caribana Parade
Lake Shore Boulevard. A hot, loud carnival sprint weaving between mas bands, steel pans and packed barricades toward the CN Tower.

## 10 critical details
1. **Lake Shore Boulevard closed to traffic** — wide asphalt lanes with painted lines, barricades lining both kerbs for the parade.
2. **Mas bands in feathered backpieces** — huge sequinned, feathered costumes in bold colours dancing up the lanes ahead of riders.
3. **Kings and Queens** — towering wheeled costume structures that dwarf the masqueraders and mark each band.
4. **Moko jumbies** — stilt walkers striding above the crowd, visible from far back along the road.
5. **Steel orchestra and music trucks** — pan players on a flatbed and DJ trucks stacked with speakers set the rhythm.
6. **Packed crowds waving island flags** — Trinidad, Jamaica, Guyana and other flags held up behind the barricades all day.
7. **CN Tower on the skyline** — rises ahead past the condos, the fixed landmark for every surge.
8. **Gardiner Expressway and Sunnyside Pavilion** — the elevated expressway alongside and the cream 1920s pavilion by the lake.
9. **Lake Ontario shoreline and trees** — lakefront parkland, shade trees and the water glimpsed beyond the crowds.
10. **Hot August afternoon light** — strong sun, hard shadows, glitter and feathers catching the light.
