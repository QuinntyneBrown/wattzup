# Las Vegas Strip
Las Vegas Boulevard. A neon-drenched midnight drag race, sprinting light to light past casinos that never sleep.

## 10 critical details
1. **Welcome to Fabulous Las Vegas sign** — the diamond-shaped sign on the median marks the start of the Strip
2. **Bellagio fountains** — dancing water jets lit white across the lake beside the boulevard
3. **Casino facades** — Caesars Palace, the Venetian's campanile, Paris's Eiffel Tower and New York-New York's skyline
4. **Neon and LED signage** — giant marquees and video boards washing the road in shifting colour
5. **Wide multi-lane boulevard** — six-plus lanes, painted medians, traffic lights at every intersection to sprint between
6. **Night sky and glow** — black desert sky above, road reflecting the light, the Luxor beam pointing up
7. **Pedestrian bridges and crowds** — elevated walkways over intersections, busy pavements of tourists at midnight
8. **Palms and median planting** — tall date palms lining the median and casino forecourts
9. **Traffic** — taxis, stretch limos, party buses and slow cruising cars in the lanes
10. **High Roller and Stratosphere** — the observation wheel and the Strat tower on the skyline as distance markers
