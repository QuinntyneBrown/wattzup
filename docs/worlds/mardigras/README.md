# Mardi Gras, New Orleans
St. Charles Avenue. A carnival ride down the parade route, pedalling to the brass-band beat between floats and roaring crowds.

## 10 critical details
1. **Parade floats** — towering, hand-painted papier-mâché floats in gold, purple and green, masked riders throwing beads.
2. **Live oaks** — huge spreading oaks arching over the avenue, branches hung with strings of beads.
3. **St. Charles streetcar** — olive-green Perley Thomas cars on the grassy neutral ground with overhead wires.
4. **Brass and marching bands** — high-school and HBCU bands in plumed hats, tubas and drumlines on the road.
5. **Packed crowds** — families on ladders and barricades, arms up for throws, both sides of the route.
6. **Purple, green and gold** — carnival colours on flags, costumes, banners and house decorations.
7. **Garden District mansions** — white-columned Greek Revival homes and iron fences behind the oaks.
8. **Dancers and flambeaux** — dance troupes, masked krewe members and torch carriers between floats.
9. **Throws on the street** — beads, cups and confetti littering the asphalt after each float.
10. **Iron-lace balconies** — wrought-iron galleries crowded with revellers on the downtown blocks.
