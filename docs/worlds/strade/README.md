# Strade Bianche
The white roads. A dusty, attacking gravel race over the Crete Senesi, surging up each white rise toward the towers of Siena.

## 10 critical details
1. **White gravel roads** — pale crushed-limestone strade bianche, rutted and dusty, winding over bare ridges.
2. **Cypress lines** — rows of tall dark Italian cypresses along drives and crests, the signature silhouette.
3. **Crete Senesi** — rolling clay hills, green in spring or golden stubble, with eroded white calanchi badlands.
4. **Torre del Mangia** — Siena's slender brick tower over the Piazza del Campo, the target on the ridge.
5. **Duomo campanile** — the black-and-white striped bell tower and dome of Siena Cathedral beside it.
6. **Stone farmhouses** — ochre poderi on hilltops ringed by cypress and olive groves.
7. **Dust clouds** — white dust kicked up behind riders and hanging in the low sun.
8. **Tifosi on the banks** — fans lining the grassy verges and steep gravel climbs.
9. **Sector banners** — race arches, numbered sector signs and barriers at gravel entries and the Siena finish.
10. **Tuscan light** — warm low sun, long shadows and big cumulus over the open hills.
