# Lisbon Tram Hills
The Alfama lanes. A grinding, twisting cobbled climb through the old quarter, dodging yellow trams on the way up to the castle over the Tagus.

## 10 critical details
1. **Yellow Tram 28** — the vintage Remodelado trams squeezing through the lanes, rails and overhead wires everywhere.
2. **Steep calçada cobbles** — polished limestone setts and tram rails on sharp, narrow gradients.
3. **Castelo de São Jorge** — the castle walls and battlements crowning the hill, the goal of every climb.
4. **Azulejo-tiled façades** — blue, green and patterned tiles on pastel houses lining the lanes.
5. **Miradouros over the Tagus** — Santa Luzia and Portas do Sol terraces opening onto rooftops and river.
6. **Terracotta rooftops and the river** — a sea of orange roofs falling to the wide blue Tagus.
7. **Sé Cathedral and São Vicente** — the fortress-like cathedral and white church towers on the route.
8. **Laundry, balconies and bougainvillea** — washing lines, iron balconies and flowers above the street.
9. **Stairs, alleys and little squares** — becos and escadinhas branching off, with café tables and locals.
10. **Bright Atlantic light** — clear sun on pale walls, deep shadows in the narrow lanes.
