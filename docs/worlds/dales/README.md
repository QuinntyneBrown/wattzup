# Yorkshire Dales
The Buttertubs. Racing should feel like a Tour de Yorkshire stage: a wild moorland climb between dry-stone walls, crowds roaring up the final ramps.

## 10 critical details
1. **Buttertubs Pass road** — the narrow single-lane road climbing from Hawes to Thwaite over the open moor.
2. **Dry-stone walls** — grey limestone walls criss-crossing green fields and lining the road.
3. **Stone field barns** — lone stone barns scattered across Swaledale and Wensleydale meadows.
4. **Open heather moor** — brown-purple moorland and rough grass at the top of the pass.
5. **Steep drop-off edge** — the road skirting a sheer fall to the valley with thin roadside posts.
6. **Race-day crowds** — fans lining the climb with flags, Yorkshire rose banners and painted road.
7. **Yorkshire rose and bunting** — white roses, yellow bikes and bunting in villages.
8. **Grey stone villages** — Hawes, Thwaite and Muker with stone cottages and pubs.
9. **Sheep everywhere** — Swaledale sheep grazing and crossing the unfenced road.
10. **Big changeable sky** — drifting cloud, bright sun bursts and squalls over the fells.
