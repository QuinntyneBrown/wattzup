# San Fermín, Pamplona
Calle Estafeta. Racing should feel like the encierro at dawn: a narrow, packed old-town sprint with the whole city in red and white.

## 10 critical details
1. **Calle Estafeta** — the long, slightly curving narrow street, tall balconied houses closing in on both sides.
2. **Red and white crowds** — white shirts, red pañuelos and sashes on every runner and spectator.
3. **Wooden barricades** — double timber fences along the route with medics and police behind them.
4. **Crowded balconies** — iron balconies hung with flags and packed with onlookers above the street.
5. **Wet cobbles and paving** — stone setts and slabs hosed down at dawn, glistening and slippery.
6. **Dawn light** — cool early morning blue in the street, golden sun only on the upper floors.
7. **Ayuntamiento square** — the baroque town hall facade in Plaza Consistorial where the run passes.
8. **Mercaderes corner** — the sharp turn into Estafeta where runners pile into the wall.
9. **Plaza de Toros** — the bullring entrance tunnel at the end of the run.
10. **Festival debris** — confetti, scarves and wine stains on the stones after the night's fiesta.
