# Bondi to Bronte
The coast walk. A sunny, surging clifftop dash, sea spray below and an ocean pool waiting at every headland.

## 10 critical details
1. **Bondi Beach crescent** — the golden curve of sand with surfers in the break and the promenade behind.
2. **Bondi Icebergs pool** — the white-walled ocean pool where waves crash over the lap lanes.
3. **Clifftop coastal path** — the paved and boardwalk path hugging sandstone cliffs with railings and steps.
4. **Honey sandstone cliffs** — weathered, honeycombed yellow rock headlands dropping into the Pacific.
5. **Tamarama and Bronte beaches** — small surf beaches tucked into coves along the route.
6. **Bronte ocean baths** — the rock pool at the southern end, finish line of the course.
7. **Breaking surf** — white Pacific swell bursting against the rocks below the path.
8. **Waverley Cemetery** — white headstones on the clifftop just beyond Bronte.
9. **Joggers and walkers** — busy Sydney runners, swimmers and families sharing the path.
10. **Bright Australian sun** — hard blue sky, turquoise water, Norfolk pines and coastal scrub.
