# Giza Plateau
Plateau Road. A sunrise desert circuit round the pyramids, efforts stacking and falling away like courses of stone.

## 10 critical details
1. **Great Pyramid of Khufu** — the largest pyramid looming beside the road, its stepped limestone courses readable up close.
2. **Pyramids of Khafre and Menkaure** — the three in a line; Khafre's polished casing cap catching first light.
3. **The Great Sphinx** — the crouching limestone Sphinx and its temple below the causeway, a sprint landmark.
4. **Sunrise light** — low orange sun and long pyramid shadows across pale sand, haze softening the distance.
5. **Plateau road surface** — sand-dusted asphalt with low kerbs and drifts of sand along its edges.
6. **Open desert dunes** — rolling tan sand and rock to the horizon west of the plateau.
7. **Queens' pyramids and mastabas** — small ruined satellite pyramids and tomb blocks lining the route.
8. **Camels and horses** — camel riders and horse carts with handlers waiting at the panorama point.
9. **Cairo skyline edge** — Giza's city blocks and minarets in the haze where desert meets town.
10. **Tourist touches** — stone barriers, guard posts and a few visitors in sunhats along the road.
