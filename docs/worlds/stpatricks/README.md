# St. Patrick's Day, Dublin
O'Connell Street. A raucous parade-day sprint down Dublin's grand boulevard, through a sea of green and the skirl of pipe bands.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Spire** — the 120 m stainless-steel needle at the centre of O'Connell Street
2. **General Post Office** — the GPO's Ionic portico and flagpoles along the west side
3. **O'Connell Monument and Bridge** — the bronze statue and the wide bridge over the Liffey
4. **Green-clad crowds** — tricolours, shamrock hats, face paint and green wigs packed behind barriers
5. **Pipe bands and floats** — marching bands in kilts, giant puppets and colourful parade floats
6. **Wide boulevard** — broad central median, plane trees, statues and Luas tram tracks
7. **Barriers and stands** — steel crowd barriers, grandstands and TV cameras along the route
8. **Georgian and Victorian facades** — shopfronts, Clerys clock and grand stone buildings
9. **March weather** — grey skies, bright spells, maybe drizzle and wet road reflections
10. **Bunting and flags** — green, white and orange flags and festival banners strung overhead
