# Champs-Élysées Finale
The Champs-Élysées. A final-day sprinters' circuit on rattling cobbles, the bunch fanning wide as the line approaches.

## 10 critical details
1. **Arc de Triomphe** — the arch crowning the top of the avenue at Place Charles de Gaulle, the turn point.
2. **Cobbled avenue** — the wide pavé surface, rough and pale, shaking the bunch on every lap.
3. **Tricolore flags** — rows of French flags on poles down the avenue, snapping in the wind.
4. **Plane-tree rows** — clipped London plane trees lining both sides above the wide pavements.
5. **Crowds behind barriers** — packed barriers and grandstands, waving flags and banners all the way up.
6. **Place de la Concorde** — the obelisk and fountains at the bottom of the avenue, the other turn.
7. **Haussmann façades** — cream stone buildings, flagship shop fronts and café terraces either side.
8. **Finish gantry** — the finish arch, sponsor boards and banners stretching the final 300 m.
9. **Evening light** — late-afternoon to dusk sun down the avenue, then streetlights and illuminated arch.
10. **Eiffel Tower glimpse** — the tower visible over the rooftops from the Concorde end and the Seine side.
