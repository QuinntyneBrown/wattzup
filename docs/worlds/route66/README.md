# Route 66 Desert
Route 66. A long, hot tempo pull across open Arizona desert, broken by flat-out sprints for each roadside sign.

## 10 critical details
1. **Straight two-lane highway** — sun-bleached asphalt with a yellow centre line running dead straight to the horizon.
2. **Red mesas and buttes** — Painted Desert bands and the Black Mountains above Sitgreaves Pass, glowing red at low sun.
3. **Route 66 shields** — the black-and-white shield painted on the road and on brown "Historic Route 66" signs.
4. **Chrome diners** — neon-trimmed diners like Mr. D'z in Kingman with red stools and pastel paint.
5. **Old gas stations** — Hackberry General Store with vintage pumps, Mobil Pegasus and rusting signs.
6. **Wigwam Motel** — white concrete teepees in Holbrook with classic cars parked outside.
7. **Classic cars** — 1950s Chevys, pickups and Corvettes parked along the main streets.
8. **Western main streets** — Oatman and Seligman boardwalks, false-front wooden shops and wandering burros.
9. **Red dirt and scrub** — rust-coloured sand, creosote and sage on wide shoulders with fence posts and power poles.
10. **Big desert sky** — deep blue with towering clouds by day, sunburst sunsets behind the mesas.
