The original diner, general store and western storefront artwork follows real 07–09 and 11–14. Wigwam proportions, the white cones, red bands and two-tone cars follow real 02 and 10. The road shield, brown signs, original grit, scrub, pump and donkey textures, wire fences and layered mesas draw on real 01–06, 09, 13 and 15. Game 01–05 informed the scale of the centre stripe, depth between roadside and mesas and forward sightlines. The route preserves the original in-game rhyme sequence and uses original course geometry and artwork.
