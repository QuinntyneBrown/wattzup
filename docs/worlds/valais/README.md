# Valais Ski Stations
The station road. Racing should feel like a relaxed summer spin between Swiss ski villages, meadows bright and glaciers above.

## 10 critical details
1. **Matterhorn and 4,000 m peaks** — snow-capped summits like the Matterhorn and Weisshorn always on the skyline.
2. **Summer alpine meadows** — green pastures full of wildflowers on both sides of the road.
3. **Wooden chalets and mazots** — dark larch chalets with flower boxes and stone-roofed granaries on stilts.
4. **Smooth hairpin roads** — well-kept Swiss tarmac with white edge lines and stone walls.
5. **Idle ski lifts** — chairlifts and gondola cables crossing the slopes, pylons on green pistes.
6. **Village churches** — white steeples in Verbier, Crans-Montana, Zinal and Saas-Fee style villages.
7. **Larch and pine forest** — conifers on the slopes between the villages.
8. **Hérens cows and bells** — black fighting cows grazing, their bells ringing.
9. **Rhône valley view** — vineyards and the wide valley floor far below.
10. **Yellow PostBus** — Swiss postal buses and yellow hiking signposts at junctions.
