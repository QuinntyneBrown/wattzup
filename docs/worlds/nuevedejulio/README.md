# Avenida 9 de Julio
Avenida 9 de Julio. An all-out drag sprint across one of the world's widest avenues, lane after lane, straight at the Obelisco.

## 10 critical details
1. **Obelisco** — the white 67 m obelisk at Plaza de la República, the sprint's target
2. **Extreme width** — up to 7 lanes each way plus side roads, a vast open expanse of asphalt
3. **Metrobus central lanes** — the red-roofed BRT stations and dedicated lanes down the median
4. **Evita murals** — the steel Eva Perón portraits on the Ministry of Health building
5. **Teatro Colón** — the grand opera house's facade just off the avenue
6. **Jacaranda and palo borracho trees** — purple jacarandas in bloom and green median plazas
7. **Traffic** — yellow-and-black taxis, colectivo buses and motorbikes filling the lanes
8. **Billboards and buildings** — rooftop ads, Beaux-Arts and modern towers lining both sides
9. **Pedestrian crossings** — long multi-stage zebra crossings with signals and crowds at each island
10. **Light** — warm golden evening or the Obelisco lit at night with streams of headlights
