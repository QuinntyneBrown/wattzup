# Copenhagen Cycle Superhighway
The Supercykelsti. A fast, flowing commuter rush: ride the green wave of lights in a river of bikes, past harbour baths and Nyhavn's colour.

## 10 critical details
1. **Kerb-separated bike lanes** — wide blue-edged cycle tracks raised from traffic, the race surface
2. **Commuter crowds** — streams of upright bikes, cargo bikes (Christiania, Bullitt) and riders in coats
3. **Green-wave signals** — bike traffic lights, LED speed markers and footrests at junctions
4. **Nyhavn** — the canal of red, yellow and blue gabled houses with wooden ships moored alongside
5. **Cykelslangen** — the orange snaking bike bridge winding over the harbour
6. **Harbour baths** — Islands Brygge's wooden decks and swimmers in the clean harbour water
7. **Inderhavnsbroen and harbour bridges** — sleek bike-and-walk bridges over the inner harbour
8. **Supercykelsti signs and service** — orange "C" route signs, air pumps and bike counters along the path
9. **Scandinavian light** — low northern sun, long shadows, pale blue sky or soft grey overcast
10. **Street life** — bike racks overflowing, cafes, linden trees and copper spires on the skyline
