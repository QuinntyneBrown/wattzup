# Montmartre
The lanes of the butte. A punchy, twisting cobbled climb through village Paris, kicking past cafés and painters to the white domes of Sacré-Cœur.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sacré-Cœur's white domes** — the travertine basilica crowning the hilltop, glimpsed ahead between rooftops.
2. **Paris pavés** — rounded granite setts on steep, narrow lanes with stone gutters.
3. **Steep ramps and stairs** — the rue Foyatier steps and railings beside the climbing streets.
4. **Cream and pink houses with green shutters** — low village houses like La Maison Rose on rue de l'Abreuvoir.
5. **Café awnings and terraces** — red and green awnings, wicker chairs and bistro tables spilling onto the pavement.
6. **Painters in the Place du Tertre** — easels, canvases and parasols crowding the square.
7. **Le Consulat and Au Lapin Agile** — signature corner bistro and cabaret on the route.
8. **Wallace fountains, Guimard Métro and lamp posts** — green Parisian street furniture at the corners.
9. **Montmartre vineyard and ivy walls** — the Clos Montmartre vines and climbing greenery on garden walls.
10. **Golden afternoon light** — warm low sun on pale façades, long shadows down the slopes.
