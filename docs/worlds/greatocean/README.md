# Great Ocean Road
The Great Ocean Road. A steady, wind-buffeted ride hugging spray-dashed sea cliffs and winding bends toward the Twelve Apostles.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Twelve Apostles** — limestone sea stacks standing in the surf, the signature landmark of the coast.
2. **Winding coastal road** — two-lane tarmac with yellow centre lines carved into the cliffside bends.
3. **Golden limestone cliffs** — sheer, layered sandstone cliffs dropping into the Southern Ocean.
4. **Southern Ocean surf and spray** — big rolling swells, white water and salt haze in the air.
5. **Loch Ard Gorge and London Bridge** — the gorge beach and the broken arch as route landmarks.
6. **Memorial Arch at Eastern View** — the timber arch over the road commemorating its builders.
7. **Coastal heath and scrub** — low, wind-sheared heath, tussock grass and banksia along the cliff tops.
8. **Otway rainforest sections** — tall eucalypts and tree ferns where the road turns inland.
9. **Road signs and lookouts** — yellow curve warnings, "Drive on left" signs and viewing-platform car parks.
10. **Changeable coastal light** — bright sun breaking through cloud, golden late light on the stacks.
