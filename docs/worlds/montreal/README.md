# Montreal Jazz Fest
Rue Sainte-Catherine. A buzzing summer street crit through festival crowds, punchy efforts between stages and easy spins between sets.

## 10 critical details
1. **Rue Sainte-Catherine** — the pedestrianised downtown street closed to cars, its asphalt painted and dotted with festival furniture.
2. **Outdoor stages** — big free-show stages with truss rigs, speaker stacks and coloured lights at Place des Festivals.
3. **Summer crowds** — dense, relaxed crowds in T-shirts and sun hats, dancing and filling the barriers.
4. **Quartier des spectacles** — the plaza with its fountain jets, Place des Arts and the red-dot lighting.
5. **Festival branding** — Jazz Fest banners, arches and kiosks in the festival's colours along the route.
6. **Downtown towers** — glass office towers and brick façades rising either side of the street.
7. **Summer evening light** — warm late sun turning to blue dusk as stage lights take over.
8. **Overhead décor** — strings of lights and hanging installations, like the pink-ball canopy of the Village.
9. **Street furniture** — food stands, terrasses, metro entrances and bike-share docks lining the kerbs.
10. **Mount Royal glimpse** — the cross-topped mountain visible up the cross streets to the north.
