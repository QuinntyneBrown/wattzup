# Marrakech Medina
Jemaa el-Fnaa. A smoky, lantern-lit dusk sprint across the great square and through the souks, dodging stalls, crowds and storytellers.

## 10 critical details
1. **Jemaa el-Fnaa at dusk** — the vast square filling with smoke, lights and noise as night falls.
2. **Koutoubia Mosque minaret** — the tall sandstone minaret silhouetted against the sunset sky.
3. **Food-stall smoke and lanterns** — rows of grill stalls under bare bulbs, plumes of smoke rising.
4. **Spice and orange-juice stalls** — pyramids of spices, carts of oranges, colourful awnings.
5. **Snake charmers and performers** — charmers with pipes, musicians, acrobats ringed by onlookers.
6. **Pierced brass lanterns** — metal lanterns casting patterned light across the souk lanes.
7. **Red ochre walls** — the pink-red rammed-earth ramparts and buildings of the medina.
8. **Souk lanes and reed roofs** — narrow covered lanes with slatted shade, carpets and slippers hung up.
9. **Scooters, donkey carts and crowds** — mopeds and carts weaving through the dense, busy throng.
10. **Rooftop café terraces** — terraces overlooking the square, with the Atlas glowing on the horizon.
