# Col du Tourmalet
The road to the col. Racing should feel like a Tour de France mountain stage: ramp after ramp, a devil at your shoulder and the summit cheering.

## 10 critical details
1. **The costumed devil** — El Diablo in red with a trident, sprinting beside riders up every ramp.
2. **Roadside fans** — Basque orange, polka-dot jerseys and flags packed on the verges near the top.
3. **Painted tarmac** — riders' names, slogans and chalk graffiti covering the climbing road.
4. **Le Géant statue** — the silver giant cyclist sculpture standing at the 2,115 m summit.
5. **Bare green slopes** — treeless pasture and scree sweeping up to grey Pyrenean peaks.
6. **Pic du Midi observatory** — the white domes on the summit ridge above the col.
7. **Km marker posts** — yellow-and-white col bornes showing distance and gradient to the top.
8. **La Mongie ski station** — concrete ski apartments and lifts on the eastern approach.
9. **Grazing livestock** — sheep, cows and horses wandering onto the road on the high slopes.
10. **Low cloud and sun** — mist rolling through the cols, then hard high sun on the switchbacks.
