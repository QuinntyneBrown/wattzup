# Dutch Tulip Fields
The bulb country. A pan-flat, wind-swept bunch race between blazing stripes of colour, windmills marking the sprints.

## 10 critical details
1. **Striped bulb fields** — dead-straight rows of red, yellow, pink, orange and white tulips running to the horizon.
2. **Flat horizon** — no hills at all; big sky dominates two-thirds of every frame.
3. **Narrow polder roads** — thin asphalt lanes with no centre line, grass edges, slightly raised above fields.
4. **Windmills** — tall Dutch smock mills (like Keukenhof's mill) as sprint landmarks along the route.
5. **Drainage ditches and canals** — water-filled sloten line fields and roads, reflecting the sky.
6. **Hyacinths and daffodils** — blue and yellow blocks between tulips break the pattern.
7. **Farmhouses and bulb sheds** — brick farms with steep roofs and low sheds sit along the field edges.
8. **Poplar and willow lines** — windbreak tree rows behind fields; pollarded willows by ditches.
9. **Cyclists and tourists** — upright Dutch bikes, tour groups, people photographing the fields at the verge.
10. **Spring light and wind** — bright, slightly hazy April sun with fast clouds and swaying flowers.
