# Mount Teide
The caldera road. A long, steady volcanic grind above a sea of clouds, then a fast, exposed run across a lunar plain.

## 10 critical details
1. **Pico del Teide** — the huge brown-and-ochre cone with snow streaks, always looming over the road
2. **Sea of clouds** — a white cloud layer far below, the road climbing out above it into clear sun
3. **Las Cañadas caldera** — a vast flat crater floor of black and tan lava, bare and otherworldly
4. **Roques de García** — the twisted rock towers beside the road near the Parador
5. **TF-21/TF-24 road** — smooth black asphalt, white edge lines, low stone walls and few barriers
6. **Canary pine forest** — tall Pinus canariensis on the lower slopes, black volcanic soil beneath
7. **Lava flows and colours** — black, red, ochre and green rock bands, frozen lava rivers along the road
8. **Red tajinaste and broom** — summer flower spires and retama shrubs dotting the lunar landscape
9. **Pro cyclists and observatory** — training riders, the white Izaña observatory domes on the ridge
10. **High-altitude light** — intense sun, deep blue sky, crisp shadows; thin air at over 2,000 m
