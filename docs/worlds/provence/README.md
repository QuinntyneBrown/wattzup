# Provence Lavender
The Valensole road. A sun-soaked summer roll across the plateau between endless purple rows, steady and fragrant.

## 10 critical details
1. **Lavender rows** — dense purple lavandin rows running in long converging lines to the horizon.
2. **Stone farmhouses** — honey-coloured mas and bories with terracotta roofs and blue shutters among the fields.
3. **Golden wheat beside lavender** — ripe wheat and sunflower fields contrasting with the purple.
4. **Narrow plateau road** — the D6 and D8 lanes, smooth tarmac with no kerbs and dusty verges.
5. **Lone trees** — isolated oaks and almond trees standing in the middle of fields.
6. **Alpine backdrop** — the hazy Alpes-de-Haute-Provence ridges and the Verdon edge on the skyline.
7. **High summer light** — blazing July sun, cicada heat and warm golden evening light.
8. **Valensole and Puimoisson villages** — hilltop villages with church towers and plane-tree squares.
9. **Bees and harvest** — honey bee hives at field edges and lavender harvest machines and bales.
10. **Roadside stalls** — lavender sellers, distillery signs and parked tourists photographing the fields.
