# Col du Galibier
The pass road. An epic high-alpine Tour climb, grinding up bare switchbacks between walls of snow to the summit at 2,642 m.

## 10 critical details
1. **Bare switchbacks** — narrow, patched asphalt snaking up open grey-green slopes, white edge lines and low walls.
2. **Snow walls** — cleared snow banks lining the road near the top in early summer.
3. **Summit sign** — the stone plinth with "Col du Galibier, altitude 2642 m" and Savoie/Hautes-Alpes signs.
4. **Henri Desgrange monument** — the granite memorial to the Tour's founder on the southern side below the summit.
5. **Summit tunnel** — the old stone-arched tunnel portal under the col.
6. **Écrins glaciers** — La Meije and the glaciated Écrins peaks across the valley from the top.
7. **Scree and tundra** — grey shale, scree slopes and thin alpine grass above the treeline.
8. **Tour road paint** — riders' names painted on the asphalt, chevron signs and kilometre markers.
9. **Plan Lachat and the valley** — the long road climbing up from Valloire past the chalets of Plan Lachat.
10. **Thin alpine light** — deep blue sky, sharp sun and fast-moving clouds over the peaks.
