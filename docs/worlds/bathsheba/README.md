# Bathsheba, Barbados
The east coast road. Racing should feel like an easy, sun-soaked Caribbean cruise, Atlantic surf booming beside the road.

## 10 critical details
1. **Mushroom rocks** — the huge wave-eroded boulders standing in the surf at Bathsheba's Soup Bowl.
2. **Atlantic surf** — rolling white breakers and turquoise reef pools along the windward shore.
3. **East Coast Road** — narrow tarmac following the shoreline, low sea walls and sandy verges.
4. **Coconut palms** — leaning palms and sea grape along the beach and roadside.
5. **Rum shacks** — small brightly painted wooden bars with hand-lettered signs and plastic chairs.
6. **White and golden sand** — wide empty beaches between rocky headlands.
7. **Chattel houses** — colourful wooden Bajan cottages with shutters and verandas.
8. **Scotland District hills** — green, eroded hills and cliffs rising inland from the coast.
9. **Fishing boats** — painted wooden boats pulled up on the sand.
10. **Bright tropical sun** — high sun, trade-wind clouds and sea spray haze.
