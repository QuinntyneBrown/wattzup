# Atlantic Ocean Road
The Storseisundet Bridge. Racing should feel like island-hopping on a ribbon of tarmac over open sea, waves breaking beside the wheels.

## 10 critical details
1. **Storseisundet Bridge** — the twisting cantilever bridge that appears to drop off into the sky from the approach.
2. **Low causeways** — road embankments on rock fill a few metres above the waves between islets.
3. **Breaking waves** — North Atlantic swell crashing on the rocks and spraying across the road edge.
4. **Bare skerries and islets** — smooth grey rock and low green islands scattered across the sea.
5. **Guardrails and road edge** — steel barriers, white edge lines and black tarmac sweeping in curves.
6. **Moody coastal sky** — fast clouds, squalls and shafts of sun; drama in every weather.
7. **Fishing spots and walkways** — anglers on the bridge walkways and rocky shore.
8. **Red rorbu cabins** — red and white wooden houses and boathouses on the islands.
9. **Distant mountains** — the Romsdal and Nordmøre peaks on the mainland horizon.
10. **Myrbærholm and Hulvågen bridges** — a chain of smaller arched bridges between islands.
