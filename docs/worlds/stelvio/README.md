# Stelvio Pass
Passo dello Stelvio. Racing should feel like a relentless high-altitude grind, hairpin after hairpin, the summit snow always above.

## 10 critical details
1. **Stacked hairpins** — the 48 numbered tornanti zig-zagging up the Trafoi wall, visible above and below the rider.
2. **Numbered hairpin signs** — "Tornante 48" countdown plaques at every bend.
3. **Stone retaining walls** — dry-stone walls and low parapets holding each switchback.
4. **Snowfields and glacier** — Ortler massif snow, roadside snowbanks near the 2,757 m top.
5. **Bare alpine scree** — grey rock and scree slopes above the treeline, sparse tundra grass.
6. **Larch and pine forest** — dense conifers on the lower ramps around Trafoi.
7. **Summit huts** — the pass-top hotels, sausage stands and souvenir shops at the col.
8. **Painted road** — riders' names and slogans scrawled on the narrow tarmac.
9. **Fellow cyclists and motorbikes** — streams of riders, motorcycles and camper vans sharing the road.
10. **Thin, bright light** — crisp high-altitude blue sky with cloud drifting below the peaks.
