# Mur de Huy
The Chemin des Chapelles. A short, brutal wall where every pedal stroke counts up to the finish line.

## 10 critical details
1. **Brutal gradient** — ramps up to 26%; the road must visibly tilt like a wall.
2. **Chemin des Chapelles** — narrow lane lined with small roadside chapels.
3. **Brick and stone houses** — Walloon terraced houses pressing close at the bottom.
4. **Wooded slope** — trees overhanging the lane on the upper climb.
5. **Painted names** — rider names painted on the tarmac.
6. **Barriers and crowds** — fans packed behind barriers on the steepest bend.
7. **Meuse valley** — the town of Huy and the river Meuse below.
8. **Huy citadel** — the fort on the rock above the town.
9. **Finish banner** — Flèche Wallonne finish arch at the top by the chapel of Notre-Dame de la Sarte.
10. **Spring Ardennes light** — April sun, fresh green leaves, damp road.
