# Times Square New Year's Eve
Broadway. Racing should feel like sprinting a canyon of light against the countdown, confetti in the air and a million people roaring.

## 10 critical details
1. **One Times Square and the ball** — the tower at the north end with the glowing crystal ball on its pole.
2. **Wall-to-wall billboards** — LED screens and signs wrapping every facade, lighting the road in shifting colour.
3. **Countdown screens** — giant clocks and numerals ticking toward midnight above the street.
4. **Confetti** — paper confetti drifting down through the light and carpeting the asphalt.
5. **Packed pens of revellers** — crowds in party hats and glasses behind barriers, filling side streets.
6. **NYPD barriers and officers** — blue police barricades and officers lining the cleared Broadway lanes.
7. **Night sky glow** — black sky above, the street lit brighter than day.
8. **"2027" numerals** — the glowing year sign atop One Times Square, the goal ahead.
9. **Bow-tie intersection** — Broadway crossing Seventh Avenue, the wide pedestrian plaza and red TKTS steps.
10. **Winter clothing and breath** — coats, hats and visible cold air on a December night.
