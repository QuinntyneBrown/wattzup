# Glastonbury Fields
The trackway. A muddy, flag-lined festival scramble, sprinting stage to stage through tents, crowds and music.

## 10 critical details
1. **Pyramid Stage** — the iconic steel pyramid with lights and screens, the course's centrepiece and finish
2. **Festival trackway** — metal and plastic trackway panels laid over grass and mud, the race surface
3. **Flags on poles** — hundreds of colourful flags waving above the crowd along every path
4. **Tent cities** — seas of multicoloured dome tents filling the fields up the valley sides
5. **Crowds** — wellies, raincoats and glitter; people walking the tracks and gathered in front of stages
6. **Other stages** — Other Stage, West Holts, the Park's ribbon tower and Arcadia's metal spider
7. **Somerset countryside** — green rolling hills, hedgerows and Glastonbury Tor visible on the horizon
8. **Mud and weather** — puddles, trodden grass, grey skies or golden evening sun
9. **Festival furniture** — steel security fences, signposts, bins, food stalls and lighting towers
10. **Evening lights** — stage lights and fairy lights coming on at dusk over the fields
