# Central Park Loop
Park Drive. An early-morning group lap of Manhattan's park, weaving past joggers and carriages under the skyline.

## 10 critical details
1. **Park Drive** — wide, car-free asphalt loop with painted bike and runner lanes and gentle rolling grades.
2. **Jacqueline Kennedy Onassis Reservoir** — the big still water with its fountain jet, mirroring the skyline beside the drive.
3. **Bethesda Fountain** — the Angel of the Waters on its red-brick terrace, glimpsed from the lake side.
4. **Skyline above the trees** — the San Remo towers and Billionaires' Row rising over the canopy on every straight.
5. **Horse carriages** — red and white hansom cabs sharing the drive near the southern end.
6. **Joggers and cyclists** — steady streams of runners, club riders and Citi Bikes in their own lanes.
7. **Mature elms and planes** — tall canopy shading the road, with spring blossom or autumn gold.
8. **Reservoir railing** — black iron fence and cinder bridle path wrapping the water.
9. **Park lamps and benches** — cast-iron lamp posts, timber benches and low stone walls along the edges.
10. **Morning light** — low sun and soft pink dawn reflecting on the water and the towers.
