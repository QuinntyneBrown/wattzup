# Alpe d'Huez
The 21 hairpins. A grinding stair of hairpins, each bend a wall of noise and colour, under a hot July sun.

## 10 critical details
1. **Numbered hairpin signs** — each bend's sign counts down from 21 to 1, with a winner's name.
2. **Stacked hairpins** — switchbacks visible above and below on the steep mountainside.
3. **Painted tarmac** — rider names and slogans in white and yellow paint across the road.
4. **Dutch Corner** — hairpin 7 by Huez church, a sea of orange fans and smoke.
5. **Church of Huez** — the little church and village beside the bend.
6. **Camper vans** — motorhomes parked on every verge, flags flying.
7. **Grandes Rousses peaks** — snowy summits and glaciers above, the Oisans valley below.
8. **Bourg-d'Oisans valley** — the flat valley floor and the steep first ramps out of town.
9. **Stone walls and barriers** — low walls and crash barriers on the outside of the bends.
10. **Ski station finish** — the resort of Alpe d'Huez with chalets and lifts at the top.
