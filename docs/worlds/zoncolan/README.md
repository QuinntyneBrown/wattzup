# Monte Zoncolan
The Ovaro road. A savage, grinding climb up a narrow ramp walled in by forest, then out onto bare summit slopes.

## 10 critical details
1. **Narrow steep ramp** — single-lane patched asphalt at 20%+ gradients; the road visibly tilts upward.
2. **Walled-in forest** — dense beech and spruce pressing both sides, blocking views low down.
3. **Painted road graffiti** — rider names and slogans painted on the tarmac by fans.
4. **Galleries/tunnels near the top** — short dark tunnels with mural art punctuating the final kilometres.
5. **Summit crowds** — packed fans on the grassy slopes at the top, flags and tents.
6. **Open alpine pastures** — bare green upper slopes with switchbacks visible above the treeline.
7. **Carnic Alps panorama** — jagged grey peaks across the valley once out of the trees.
8. **Ovaro and Liariis villages** — stone houses, church towers and balconies at the climb's foot.
9. **Guardrails and stone edges** — metal guardrails and crumbling verges on exposed bends.
10. **Grazing cows** — Friulian cattle on the high meadows near the summit.
