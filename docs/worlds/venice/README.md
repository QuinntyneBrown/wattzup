# Venice Beach Boardwalk
Ocean Front Walk. Twitchy, joyful ten-second sprints weaving between palms, skaters and street life, the pier wheel always ahead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Ocean Front Walk paving** — the wide concrete promenade with the Marvin Braude bike path running beside it.
2. **Towering skinny palms** — tall Mexican fan palms in long rows, the rhythm for each sprint.
3. **Mural-painted shopfronts** — graffiti and murals on the low buildings, T-shirt and souvenir stalls spilling out.
4. **Muscle Beach** — the open-air gym cage by the sand, an early landmark for the warm-up.
5. **Venice Skatepark and skaters** — bowls by the beach, skaters and rollerbladers gliding past the riders.
6. **Street performers and crowds** — buskers, vendors and strolling crowds give constant motion at the edges.
7. **Santa Monica Pier Ferris wheel** — Pacific Park's wheel turning on the horizon across the bay.
8. **Wide sand and the Pacific** — broad beach to the west, surf and lifeguard towers in blue.
9. **Venice graffiti walls** — the free-wall graffiti pit and painted concrete by the beach.
10. **Hazy California sun** — bright, slightly hazy light with the Santa Monica Mountains faint beyond.
