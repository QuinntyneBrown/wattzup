# Cinque Terre
The corniche. A sunny, twisting cliff-road race with the sea glittering below and a pastel village round every bend.

## 10 critical details
1. **Pastel tower-houses** — stacked pink, ochre and yellow houses clinging to the rock in Manarola and Vernazza.
2. **Ligurian Sea below** — deep blue-green water and white surf at the foot of the cliffs.
3. **The corniche road** — narrow, twisting SP370 high on the cliffs, low stone wall on the sea side.
4. **Terraced vineyards** — dry-stone terraces and vines stepping up the steep hillsides above the road.
5. **Vernazza harbour** — the tiny harbour, Doria castle tower and church by the water.
6. **Riomaggiore and Corniglia** — villages in ravines and on the headland, seen from bends above.
7. **Fishing boats** — bright wooden boats hauled up in village streets and slipways.
8. **Mediterranean scrub and olives** — olive groves, pines, agave and prickly pear along the edges.
9. **Coastal footpath and hikers** — the Sentiero Azzurro and walkers crossing the road on the hills.
10. **Golden Italian light** — bright sun, sharp shadows and warm evening glow on the façades.
