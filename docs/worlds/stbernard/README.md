# Great St Bernard Pass
The hospice road. A long, cold alpine grind up bare switchbacks to the border, the old hospice and its frozen lake at the top.

## 10 critical details
1. **Great St Bernard Hospice** — the massive stone hospice buildings straddling the road at the summit.
2. **Frozen summit lake** — the small high lake beside the hospice, icy or snow-covered for much of the year.
3. **Swiss–Italian border post** — customs buildings, flags and border signs at the col.
4. **Snow walls and snowfields** — banks of snow lining the road even in early summer.
5. **Bare switchbacks above the tree line** — narrow tarmac hairpins winding through rock and tundra.
6. **Avalanche galleries** — concrete snow sheds and tunnels on the approach roads.
7. **Saint Bernard dogs** — the famous dogs at the hospice kennels, a living landmark.
8. **Statue of Saint Bernard** — the bronze statue on the rocky knoll on the Italian side.
9. **Granite peaks** — jagged dark summits and scree slopes closing in around the pass.
10. **Cold, thin alpine light** — crisp blue sky or drifting cloud, chill wind and bright snow.
