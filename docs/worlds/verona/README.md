# Verona Arena Finale
The arena finale. A triumphant last stage through Verona's streets, finishing inside the Roman arena.

## 10 critical details
1. **Arena di Verona** — the Roman amphitheatre with pink stone arches; the finish.
2. **Piazza Bra** — the wide square with cafés and the Liston promenade beside the arena.
3. **Torricelle climb** — the hill road above the city with views over the rooftops.
4. **Adige river** — the river curling through town with stone bridges.
5. **Ponte Pietra** — the Roman stone bridge.
6. **Pink-marble paving** — polished stone streets and cobbles.
7. **Grand Tour crowds** — fans, pink banners and barriers lining the final kilometres.
8. **Portoni della Bra** — the twin-arched gate into Piazza Bra.
9. **Torre dei Lamberti** — the tall brick tower above Piazza delle Erbe.
10. **Cypresses and hills** — cypresses and vineyards on the hills around the city.
