# Indianapolis Brickyard
The front straight. A flat-out drafting duel on a vast oval, slingshotting off the last turn to the bricks.

## 10 critical details
1. **Yard of Bricks** — the strip of original bricks across the start/finish line, the finish to race for
2. **Pagoda** — the tall glass-and-steel timing tower beside the front straight
3. **Main grandstands** — huge multi-tier stands lining the straight, packed with fans on race day
4. **Banked turns** — gentle 9-degree banking at each corner, white walls and SAFER barriers
5. **Scoring pylon** — the tall numbered leaderboard tower showing positions
6. **Wide track surface** — smooth grey asphalt, rubber lines, painted pit-lane line and turn markers
7. **Catch fences** — tall wire fencing above the outside wall, cameras and flag stands
8. **Infield** — golf course, grass, trees and the museum within the oval
9. **Race-day atmosphere** — flags, balloons, pre-race crowds and the checkered flag at the line
10. **Midwest sky** — big flat horizon, summer sun and puffy clouds over the open stadium
