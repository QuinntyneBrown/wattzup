# Tel Aviv Promenade
The Tayelet. A bright, breezy seafront ride along the Mediterranean, spinning past beach courts and cafés toward old Jaffa.

## 10 critical details
1. **Tayelet promenade** — wide paved seafront walk with patterned stone circles and a red bike lane.
2. **Mediterranean beaches** — golden sand and turquoise surf along Gordon, Frishman and Banana beaches.
3. **Palm-lined boulevard** — date palms down the median and edges of Herbert Samuel Street.
4. **Beach volleyball and matkot** — sand courts, nets and paddle-ball players beside the path.
5. **Seafront cafés** — beach bars with umbrellas, sunbeds and timber shade pergolas.
6. **Hotel towers** — white high-rise hotels and apartment blocks lining the shore.
7. **Old Jaffa** — the stone old town, clock tower and St. Peter's Church on the headland to the south.
8. **Breakwaters and marina** — rock breakwaters, sailboats and the open-air Gordon Pool.
9. **Lifeguard huts and lamps** — raised lifeguard towers, tall promenade lamps and benches.
10. **Mediterranean sunset** — the sun dropping into the sea, golden light along the promenade.
