# Daytona Beach Sand
Tide Line. A flat-out drag race on hard wet sand, surf on one side, hotels on the other.

## 10 critical details
1. **Hard-packed tide-line sand** — wide, flat, wet-shiny surface with tyre tracks; the whole course is beach.
2. **Atlantic surf** — low breaking waves and foam running parallel to the racing line on the left.
3. **Main Street Pier** — long timber pier with the Joe's Crab Shack building; riders pass under or beside it.
4. **Beachfront high-rises** — Hilton and condo towers form a continuous wall along the dunes.
5. **Cars on the beach** — slow-moving cars and trucks parked in rows recall the beach-driving tradition.
6. **Boardwalk and Ferris wheel** — amusement strip with the Ferris wheel and Sky Coaster near the pier.
7. **Bandshell** — coquina-stone Daytona Bandshell as a recognisable landmark beside the boardwalk.
8. **Lifeguard towers and ramps** — coloured towers, beach-access ramps and speed-limit signage mark distance.
9. **Umbrellas and sunbathers** — rows of blue-yellow umbrellas and loungers fill the dry sand.
10. **Golden sunrise light** — low sun over the ocean, reflections on the wet sand.
