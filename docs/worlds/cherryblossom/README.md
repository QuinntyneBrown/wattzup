# Cherry Blossom Festival, D.C.
The Tidal Basin loop. A bright spring loop under pink-white blossom past monuments, kites and festival crowds.

## 10 critical details
1. **Yoshino cherry trees** — pale pink-white blossom arching over the Tidal Basin path.
2. **Tidal Basin** — calm water reflecting monuments and blossom right beside the loop.
3. **Jefferson Memorial** — white domed memorial across the water, a constant landmark.
4. **Washington Monument** — white obelisk rising over the trees on the Mall.
5. **Kites over the Mall** — Blossom Kite Festival colour filling the sky above the lawns.
6. **Festival crowds** — dense strolling visitors and photographers along the waterside path.
7. **Stone seawall path** — granite-edged walkway around the basin, narrow in places.
8. **White festival tents** — event marquees, pink banners and food stands on the grass.
9. **Paddle boats** — blue paddle boats out on the basin.
10. **Spring light** — fresh clear sky; soft pink sunrise as an option.
