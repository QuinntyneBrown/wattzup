# Alto de Letras
The Letras road. Racing should feel like an epic all-day ascent, from humid coffee slopes into cold, misty páramo high above the clouds.

## 10 critical details
1. **Endless switchbacks** — the Ruta 50 tarmac winding up green Andean ridges with no summit in sight.
2. **Coffee plantations** — glossy coffee bushes, plantain and shade trees on the steep lower slopes.
3. **Wax palms** — tall slender palma de cera on the ridges, Colombia's national tree.
4. **Páramo vegetation** — frailejones, tussock grass and moss at the cold, foggy top near 3,679 m.
5. **Cloud forest mist** — clouds drifting across the road and filling the valleys below.
6. **Roadside towns** — Fresno and Padua with painted colonial houses, balconies and bakeries.
7. **Colourful chivas and trucks** — painted buses and lorries crawling up the grade.
8. **Local cyclists** — Colombian amateur riders in bright kits climbing alongside.
9. **Roadside stalls** — fruit, arepa and aguapanela stands at bends and viewpoints.
10. **Andean light** — hot equatorial sun below, then cold grey light near the summit.
