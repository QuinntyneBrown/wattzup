# Comuna 13, Medellín
The escalator streets. A punchy hillside climb through a riot of murals, steep ramps stepping up beside the outdoor escalators.

## 10 critical details
1. **Outdoor escalators** — the orange-roofed public escalators climbing the hillside in stages, a landmark of renewal.
2. **Graffiti murals** — vivid street-art walls covering every surface with faces, birds and messages of peace.
3. **Stacked brick houses** — unrendered red-brick homes piled up the slope with tin roofs and rebar.
4. **Narrow steep lanes** — concrete alleys, steps and switchback ramps with painted kerbs.
5. **Hillside views** — panoramas across the Aburrá Valley to downtown Medellín's towers and the mountains.
6. **Street performers** — breakdancers, hip-hop crews and musicians performing on terraces.
7. **Colourful details** — painted stairs, bunting, umbrellas and hanging plants above the lanes.
8. **Visitors and locals** — tourist groups with guides mixing with residents, kids and vendors.
9. **Food and craft stalls** — mango, salpicón and arepa stands, souvenir shops and bars on terraces.
10. **Tropical greenery and light** — banana plants, bougainvillea and bright highland sun with afternoon clouds.
