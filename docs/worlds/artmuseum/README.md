# Philadelphia Art Museum Steps
Benjamin Franklin Parkway. Racing should feel like a triumphant boulevard charge toward the museum, every effort ending with a sprint up the steps, arms raised.

## 10 critical details
1. **Museum facade** — the Greek-temple Philadelphia Museum of Art with columns and golden-brown stone crowning the Parkway.
2. **The "Rocky Steps"** — the 72 wide granite steps up the east entrance, the finish of every effort.
3. **Benjamin Franklin Parkway** — the broad tree-lined diagonal boulevard with wide lanes and medians.
4. **Avenue of flags** — rows of international flags on poles lining the Parkway.
5. **Eakins Oval and Washington Monument** — the oval and fountain at the foot of the museum.
6. **Swann Memorial Fountain** — Logan Circle's fountain with bronze figures and spray.
7. **City Hall and skyline** — William Penn atop City Hall and Center City towers behind the rider.
8. **Rocky statue** — the bronze boxer statue by the steps with tourists posing.
9. **Plane trees and lawns** — leafy canopy and green verges along the boulevard.
10. **Runners and visitors** — joggers, tourists and fans running up the steps with fists in the air.
