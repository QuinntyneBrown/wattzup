# Old Québec
The Côte de la Montagne. A punchy, cobbled climb through North America's walled city, grinding up steep lanes to the ramparts.

## 10 critical details
1. **Côte de la Montagne** — the steep, curving street climbing from the Lower Town to the Upper Town.
2. **Château Frontenac** — the copper-roofed château towering over the cliff, visible from every bend.
3. **Stone ramparts** — grey fortification walls, grassy glacis and cannons ringing the Upper Town.
4. **City gates** — Porte Saint-Louis and Porte Kent, castellated stone arches the road passes through.
5. **Terrasse Dufferin** — the timber boardwalk with green-and-white gazebos above the river.
6. **St. Lawrence River** — the wide river, ferries and the far shore below the cliff.
7. **Stone houses** — 17th–18th-century fieldstone buildings with steep metal roofs and dormers.
8. **Petit-Champlain lanes** — narrow pedestrian lanes with shop signs, flower boxes and the Escalier Casse-Cou steps.
9. **Street furniture** — globe lamp posts, green iron railings, flags and café terraces.
10. **Seasonal light** — autumn maples in red and gold, or snow banks lit warm at dusk.
