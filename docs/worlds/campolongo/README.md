# Passo Campolongo
The road from Corvara. A short, steady Dolomites climb, attacking out of each hairpin toward the open grassy saddle.

## 10 critical details
1. **Hairpin road** — smooth grey asphalt with chevron signs and guard rails switching back through the trees.
2. **Sassongher** — the pale limestone peak towering over Corvara, visible from every opening in the climb.
3. **Sella massif** — the snow-streaked flat-topped wall of the Sella and Piz Boè ahead near the top.
4. **Larch and spruce forest** — dense dark conifers lining the lower bends, thinning toward the pass.
5. **Ski lifts** — chairlift and gondola cables, pylons and pistes crossing the meadows above the road.
6. **Grassy saddle** — wide green alpine pasture at 1875 m, with a rifugio and the pass sign.
7. **Corvara village** — timber-balconied Ladin hotels, chalets and the white church with its pointed spire.
8. **Alpine meadows and cows** — bright green slopes with grazing cattle, hay barns and wooden fences.
9. **Clear mountain light** — crisp blue sky and sharp shadows; summer cumulus building over the peaks.
10. **Ladin signage** — bilingual and Ladin place names on road signs and hotel fronts.
