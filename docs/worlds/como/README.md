# Lake Como
The shore road. A flowing, rolling run along the water, threading villages, tunnels and villa gardens.

## 10 critical details
1. **Lake Como** — deep blue water right beside the road, always in view.
2. **Steep wooded mountains** — pre-Alps rising straight from the far shore.
3. **Narrow shore road** — the twisting SS340 hugging stone walls, with short tunnels.
4. **Villas and gardens** — Villa Carlotta and Villa del Balbianello, terraced gardens and cypresses.
5. **Lakeside villages** — Bellagio, Varenna and Tremezzo with pastel houses stacked on the hillsides.
6. **Ferries** — white ferries and hydrofoils criss-crossing between villages.
7. **Church bell towers** — campaniles rising above each village.
8. **Stone walls and railings** — balustrades and walls between road and water.
9. **Lakeside promenades** — plane trees, cafés and moored boats at the water's edge.
10. **Hazy summer light** — warm afternoon haze softening each mountain layer.
