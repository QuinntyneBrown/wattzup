# Winnats Pass
The Winnats road. Racing should feel like a short, savage climb up a green limestone gorge, legs burning between the walls.

## 10 critical details
1. **Limestone gorge walls** — steep grassy slopes and grey crags rising sharply on both sides of the road.
2. **Narrow single-track road** — thin tarmac ribbon with no centre line, kinking up the gorge floor.
3. **20% ramps** — the steepest section near the top; the road visibly tilts toward the sky.
4. **Grazing sheep** — sheep on the slopes and wandering onto the unfenced road.
5. **Cattle grids and dry-stone walls** — grids at the gorge entrance and walls on the moorland approach.
6. **Mam Tor and the Great Ridge** — the shivering mountain and ridge seen from the top.
7. **Hope Valley view** — looking back down to Castleton and the green valley floor.
8. **Exposed rock outcrops** — limestone pinnacles and scree at the gorge's narrowest point.
9. **Cyclists and walkers** — riders grinding up, walkers on the verges, spectators at race time.
10. **Changeable Peak District sky** — grey cloud, bright breaks and wind across green slopes.
