# Cliffside Coast
Headland Road. Racing should feel like a sunset tempo duel on a cliff edge, the sea glittering on your right.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sea on the right** — open ocean fills the right side of the frame the whole way, sun glinting off it.
2. **Sunset light** — low golden sun ahead and to the right, long shadows and a warm orange sky.
3. **Headland climbs** — each surge rises over a grassy headland, the road crest silhouetted against the sky.
4. **Sheer cliffs** — pale rock faces drop to the surf; next headlands stack into the distance.
5. **Breaking surf** — white water at the cliff foot and in coves, visible from every crest.
6. **Narrow coast road** — two-lane tarmac, worn centre line, hugging the contour with sweeping bends.
7. **Low stone wall and guardrail** — seaward edge guarded by a dry-stone wall or steel barrier.
8. **Clifftop grass and gorse** — windswept turf, yellow gorse and heather on the landward bank.
9. **Lighthouse on a point** — a white lighthouse on a far headland as a goal for each climb.
10. **Lay-bys and walkers** — viewpoint lay-bys with parked cars and walkers watching the sunset.
