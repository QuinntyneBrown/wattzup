# Vienna Ringstrasse
The Ringstrasse. A stately, fast boulevard loop past palaces and monuments, racing the trams.

## 10 critical details
1. **Wiener Staatsoper** — the opera house on the Ring.
2. **Parliament** — the Greek-style Parliament with the Athena fountain.
3. **Rathaus** — the neo-Gothic city hall with its tall tower.
4. **Burgtheater and Hofburg** — the theatre and imperial palace buildings.
5. **Red-and-white trams** — Vienna trams running along the boulevard.
6. **Avenue of trees** — rows of plane and chestnut trees lining the Ring.
7. **Wide boulevard** — multi-lane road with tram tracks, cycle path and paved sidewalks.
8. **Museums** — the twin Kunsthistorisches and Naturhistorisches museums with Maria Theresa monument.
9. **Votivkirche** — twin white Gothic spires.
10. **Parks** — Volksgarten, Burggarten and Stadtpark greenery along the Ring.
