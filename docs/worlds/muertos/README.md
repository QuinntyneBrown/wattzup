# Día de Muertos Parade
Paseo de la Reforma. A festive evening parade race down a grand boulevard of marigolds, skeletons and roaring crowds.

## 10 critical details
1. **Paseo de la Reforma** — wide multi-lane boulevard with tree-lined medians, the parade's straight racing line.
2. **Ángel de la Independencia** — gilded angel on its column, a glowing roundabout landmark mid-course.
3. **Cempasúchil marigolds** — orange marigold beds and arches lining medians and floats.
4. **Giant skeletons and catrinas** — towering puppet skeletons and elegant catrina figures marching beside riders.
5. **Painted-face crowds** — sugar-skull face paint everywhere; packed spectators behind barriers.
6. **Monarch butterfly dancers** — orange-winged costumed dancers swirling across the road.
7. **Floats and alebrijes** — colourful floats, lowriders and fantastical alebrije creatures.
8. **Papel picado** — cut-paper bunting strung across the boulevard overhead.
9. **Glass towers** — modern Reforma skyscrapers framing the avenue and lighting the night.
10. **Dusk to night light** — warm sunset fading to streetlights, neon and float lights.
