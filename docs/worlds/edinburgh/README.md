# Edinburgh Fringe
The Royal Mile. A bumpy, crowded cobbled scramble between street performers and flyering crowds, climbing toward the Castle.

## 10 critical details
1. **Setts of the Royal Mile** — rough grey granite cobbles, slightly cambered, that rattle every rider.
2. **Edinburgh Castle on its rock** — the Castle Esplanade crowns the top of the climb.
3. **St Giles' Cathedral crown spire** — the open stone crown rising mid-route above the High Street.
4. **Tall grey stone tenements** — six-storey sandstone lands with crow-stepped gables and narrow closes.
5. **Street performers and circles of audience** — jugglers, acrobats and fire acts ringed by crowds on the cobbles.
6. **Fringe posters and flyers** — layered show posters on boards and railings, colourful against the grey stone.
7. **Dense August crowds** — tourists, flyerers in branded T-shirts and buskers spilling across the street.
8. **Festival stages and gazebos** — small temporary stages and Fringe-branded booths along the High Street.
9. **Tron Kirk and the Heart of Midlothian** — landmarks marking the route through the Old Town.
10. **Changeable Scottish light** — bright cloud and showers, wet setts reflecting the sky.
