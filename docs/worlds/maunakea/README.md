# Mauna Kea
The summit road. Racing should feel like a brutal, lonely climb from tropical cloud into a thin-aired Martian landscape crowned by gleaming observatories.

## 10 critical details
1. **Observatory domes** — the white and silver telescope domes (Keck, Subaru, Gemini) on the 4,207 m summit ridge.
2. **Red cinder cones** — rust-red and ochre volcanic cones and scree, bare of any plant life.
3. **Steep switchbacks** — the summit access road zig-zagging up loose cinder slopes, gravel then tarmac.
4. **Above the clouds** — a sea of white cloud far below the road, Mauna Loa rising beyond.
5. **Visitor station** — the Onizuka centre at 2,800 m with its stone buildings and silverswords.
6. **Saddle Road lava fields** — black lava flows and scrub on the lower approach.
7. **Māmane forest** — gnarled dryland trees on the lower slopes before the treeline.
8. **Thin blue sky** — deep cobalt high-altitude sky, harsh sun, sometimes snow on the summit.
9. **Warning signs** — altitude, 4WD and steep-grade signs along the road.
10. **Sunset glow** — orange sunset over the clouds lighting the domes.
