# Iceland Ring Road
Route 1. An epic, open-road ride through raw volcanic landscape under a sun that never sets.

## 10 critical details
1. **Route 1 road** — two-lane tarmac with yellow edge poles and no trees.
2. **Black sand** — black beaches and outwash plains at Vík and Skeiðarársandur.
3. **Glaciers** — Vatnajökull's outlet glaciers spilling down to the road.
4. **Seljalandsfoss and Skógafoss** — tall waterfalls dropping off the cliffs beside the road.
5. **Jökulsárlón icebergs** — the glacier lagoon with floating blue icebergs.
6. **Mossy lava fields** — green moss over lumpy lava, Eldhraun.
7. **One-lane bridges** — narrow steel bridges over braided glacial rivers.
8. **Icelandic horses and sheep** — grazing by the road.
9. **Midnight sun** — low golden sun near the horizon, long shadows.
10. **Red-roofed farms and churches** — small white churches with red roofs.
