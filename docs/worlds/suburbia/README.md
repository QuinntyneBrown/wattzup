# Suburban Sunrise
Maple Avenue. A quiet dawn street race past porches and lawns, the neighbourhood waking as you sprint.

## 10 critical details
1. **Tree-lined avenue** — mature maples arch over both sides, leaves catching low orange sunlight.
2. **Clapboard houses with porches** — two-storey wooden houses, columns and steps, set back behind lawns.
3. **Front lawns and sprinklers** — green mown lawns with ticking sprinklers throwing glittering arcs across sidewalks.
4. **Sidewalks and grass verges** — concrete sidewalks with grass strips, driveways cutting across every few metres.
5. **Parked cars and driveways** — family cars in driveways and along the curb narrow the street.
6. **Newspaper delivery** — rolled papers landing on porches and lawns as a paperboy cycles past.
7. **Neighbours waving** — residents on porches with coffee, dog walkers and joggers on the sidewalk.
8. **Street furniture** — mailboxes, fire hydrants, stop signs, basketball hoops and wooden utility poles.
9. **Cul-de-sacs and gentle curves** — wide asphalt corners and a cul-de-sac loop give the course shape.
10. **Sunrise light** — long shadows, pink-orange sky, dew on grass and soft mist in the distance.
