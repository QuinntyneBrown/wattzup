# Algarve Cliffs
The clifftop road. A sunny, rolling coastal race past golden cliffs and white fishing villages above turquoise coves.

## 10 critical details
1. **Golden sandstone cliffs** — ochre and orange cliffs, eroded and banded, dropping into the sea.
2. **Sea stacks and arches** — Ponta da Piedade's pillars and arches standing in the water.
3. **Turquoise coves** — small sandy beaches like Praia da Marinha tucked between headlands.
4. **Benagil sea cave** — the famous domed cave with its open roof onto the beach.
5. **Whitewashed fishing villages** — white houses with blue trim and terracotta roofs in Ferragudo and Carvoeiro.
6. **Colourful fishing boats** — painted wooden boats pulled up on the beaches.
7. **Clifftop road and paths** — narrow tarmac and red-earth tracks along the cliff edge.
8. **Mediterranean scrub** — umbrella pines, fan palms, agaves and wild herbs on the clifftops.
9. **Cape St Vincent lighthouse** — the lighthouse at Europe's south-western tip.
10. **Bright Atlantic sun** — intense sunlight, deep blue sky and golden late-afternoon glow.
