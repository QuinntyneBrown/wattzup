# Ibiza Sunset Strip
The sunset strip. A warm, pulsing waterfront ride where every effort drops with the bass and recoveries float toward the sunset.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sunset over the sea** — the sun sinking into the Mediterranean off Sant Antoni, sky blazing orange to violet.
2. **Conillera island** — the low rocky island silhouetted offshore, the sun setting beside it.
3. **Sant Antoni waterfront** — the Caló des Moro sunset strip of rocky coves and promenade along the bay.
4. **Whitewashed beach clubs** — white cube buildings with neon club names glowing as dusk falls.
5. **DJ terrace and light beams** — a DJ booth sweeping beams and lasers over the terrace crowd.
6. **Dancing crowds** — summer crowds in swimwear and linen on terraces and rocks, hands up at sunset.
7. **Palms and pergolas** — palms, wooden pergolas, cushions and parasols on the club terraces.
8. **Festoon lights** — warm bulb strings swinging over the promenade and terraces.
9. **Promenade surface** — smooth pale paving and low sea walls with the water right beside.
10. **Boats on the bay** — yachts, sailboats and sunset cruise boats anchored in golden water.
