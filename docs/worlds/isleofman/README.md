# Isle of Man Mountain Course
The Mountain Course. A flat-out, bare-moor charge over Snaefell on the legendary TT roads, walls inches away.

## 10 critical details
1. **A18 Mountain Road** — fast sweeping two-lane tarmac over open moor with white centre dashes.
2. **Stone walls and banks** — dry-stone walls and hedge banks tight to the road in lower sections.
3. **Snaefell** — the island's highest hill, rounded and grassy, dominating the skyline.
4. **Snaefell Mountain Railway** — tracks and tram crossing the road at the Bungalow.
5. **Black-and-white kerbs** — painted kerbs and straw bales on bends like Creg-ny-Baa.
6. **Footbridges and gantries** — Monster-branded footbridge spanning the course near the Bungalow.
7. **Creg-ny-Baa pub** — white hotel on the bend with spectator stands.
8. **Open moorland** — brown-green heather and grass with sheep, no trees above the treeline.
9. **Spectators and marshals** — fans on banks and walls, marshals in orange tabards.
10. **Changeable sky** — big grey-blue skies, wind and sudden sunbreaks over the Irish Sea.
