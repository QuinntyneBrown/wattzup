# La Mercè, Barcelona
Via Laietana. A wild festival-night race down Barcelona's grand avenue, weaving between giants, human towers and the sparks of the correfoc.

## 10 critical details
1. **Via Laietana** — the broad, straight avenue of grand stone office blocks linking the port to the Eixample.
2. **Correfoc fire runs** — devils and fire beasts spraying showers of sparks across the street at night.
3. **Gegants i capgrossos** — giant costumed figures and big-headed dancers parading through the crowd.
4. **Castells human towers** — castellers in coloured shirts building towers in Plaça Sant Jaume.
5. **Barcelona Cathedral and Gothic Quarter** — the gothic façade and narrow stone lanes just off the route.
6. **Ajuntament and Palau de la Generalitat** — the facing palaces of Plaça Sant Jaume.
7. **Dense festival crowds** — thousands lining barriers, kids on shoulders, phones raised.
8. **Catalan flags and bunting** — senyeres hanging from balconies over the street.
9. **Projection lights and fireworks** — light shows on façades and the piromusical glow over Montjuïc.
10. **Warm September night** — sodium streetlights, smoke haze and orange firelight on the stone.
