# Coney Island Boardwalk
The boardwalk. A sunny, rattling seaside ride on timber planks, pushing hard for the Wonder Wheel past the clam bars and arcades.

## 10 critical details
1. **Riegelmann Boardwalk planks** — weathered timber and concrete planks in herringbone, rumbling under the wheels.
2. **Wonder Wheel** — the 1920 steel Ferris wheel with swinging cars, the goal of every long effort.
3. **The Cyclone** — the white wooden-lattice roller coaster rising beside Surf Avenue.
4. **Parachute Jump** — the red steel tower, Brooklyn's "Eiffel Tower", visible from the whole route.
5. **Beach crowds** — wide sand packed with umbrellas, towels and swimmers below the railing.
6. **Boardwalk railing and lamps** — green iron railings and old lamp posts lining the sea side.
7. **Clam bars and arcades** — Ruby's, Paul's Daughter and arcade fronts with hand-painted signs and awnings.
8. **Stars-and-stripes bunting** — red-white-blue bunting and flags strung along the storefronts.
9. **Luna Park rides** — brightly coloured rides and the Thunderbolt coaster behind the boardwalk.
10. **Summer sun and sea** — bright high-summer light, Atlantic surf and the Steeplechase Pier reaching out.
