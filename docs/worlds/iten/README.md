# Iten, Kenya
The red-dirt road. A thin-air training ride on the roads of champions, holding rhythm over red murram above the Kerio Valley.

## 10 critical details
1. **Red murram roads** — rust-red compacted dirt tracks with ruts and grassy centres, the signature surface.
2. **Kerio Valley escarpment** — the sheer drop and vast valley view from the rim at the edge of Iten.
3. **Green highland farms** — patchwork maize plots, tea and pasture on rolling hills at 2,400 m.
4. **Runners in training** — groups of athletes striding along the roadside at dawn.
5. **Kamariny Stadium** — the dirt track with its simple wooden stand where champions do their intervals.
6. **Acacia and euphorbia** — flat-topped acacias and candelabra trees dotting the open grassland.
7. **Grazing livestock** — cattle, sheep and goats with herders on the verges.
8. **Iten town** — low tin-roofed shops, painted signs, matatus and boda-boda motorbikes on the main road.
9. **Fence lines and poles** — wooden fence posts, barbed wire and power poles along the tracks.
10. **Highland sky** — intense equatorial sun, deep blue sky and big cumulus over the hills.
