# Joshua Tree
Park Boulevard. A dry, sun-baked desert ride past spiky Joshua trees and giant golden boulder piles.

## 10 critical details
1. **Joshua trees** — twisted, shaggy yucca trees with spiky arms, scattered across the plain.
2. **Boulder piles** — huge rounded monzogranite boulders stacked into golden domes and towers.
3. **Park Boulevard** — the narrow two-lane park road with yellow lines winding between rock piles.
4. **Skull Rock** — the boulder with eye-socket hollows right beside the road.
5. **Jumbo Rocks and Hidden Valley** — climbing areas and camp spots among the rock formations.
6. **Cholla Cactus Garden** — dense fuzzy cholla glowing in backlight.
7. **Desert floor** — pale sand, gravel and sparse creosote, sage and yucca.
8. **Rock climbers** — climbers on the boulders and cars parked at pull-outs.
9. **Distant mountain ranges** — the Little San Bernardino Mountains on the horizon.
10. **Golden desert light** — warm low sun, long shadows, and a vivid sunset sky.
