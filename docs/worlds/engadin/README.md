# Engadin Lakes
The lakes road. A fast, flowing high-valley race along a chain of glittering alpine lakes from Maloja past Sils and Silvaplana to St. Moritz.

## 10 critical details
1. **Chain of lakes** — Lej da Segl, Silvaplana and St. Moritz lakes, deep blue beside the road.
2. **Valley road along the shore** — smooth two-lane Swiss tarmac with white lines and roadside bike lane.
3. **Piz Corvatsch and the Bernina peaks** — glaciated summits rising above the south side of the valley.
4. **Larch and stone-pine forests** — golden larches and Arolla pines on the slopes and lakeshores.
5. **St. Moritz town on the hillside** — hotels, the leaning tower and church above the lake.
6. **Engadin houses with sgraffito** — thick white walls, deep-set windows and etched decorations in Sils and Silvaplana.
7. **Kitesurfers and sails** — colourful kites on Silvaplana's windy lake in the afternoon Maloja wind.
8. **Open meadows and wooden fences** — wide green pastures with cattle and split-rail fences by the lakes.
9. **Rhaetian Railway** — the red trains running along the valley floor.
10. **Clear high-altitude light** — crisp blue sky, sharp reflections and autumn gold in the larches.
