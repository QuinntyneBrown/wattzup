# North Shore, Oahu
Kamehameha Highway. A sun-baked coastal cruise that turns into a rolling surf-town sprint between beaches and shrimp trucks.

## 10 critical details
1. **Kamehameha Highway** — narrow two-lane coast road, worn asphalt, sandy shoulders, no kerbs, double yellow line
2. **Big-wave surf** — huge winter swells breaking white at Waimea Bay, Pipeline and Sunset Beach
3. **Waimea Bay** — the curved bay, church bell tower and jumping rock seen from the highway bend
4. **Shrimp trucks** — Giovanni's and others, graffiti-covered trucks with picnic tables and queues in Kahuku
5. **Haleiwa town** — the Haleiwa sign, Rainbow Bridge over the Anahulu River, plantation-style wooden storefronts
6. **Palms and ironwoods** — coconut palms and ironwood trees leaning over the road along the beach
7. **Koolau mountains** — green, cloud-capped ridges rising inland on the right side of the road
8. **Surfers and beachgoers** — boards on car roofs, people crossing to the beach, parked trucks on the shoulder
9. **Tropical light** — bright sun, turquoise water, puffy trade-wind clouds and golden sunset over the ocean
10. **Roadside details** — fruit stands, surf shops, hibiscus, one-lane bridges and Hawaiian place-name signs
