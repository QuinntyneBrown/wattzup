# Golden Gate Park
JFK Drive. A cool, foggy sprint session through San Francisco's great park, bursting past the bison toward the sea.

## 10 critical details
1. **JFK Drive** — car-free promenade asphalt with painted lanes, curbs and lamp posts winding west through the park.
2. **Coastal fog** — grey marine layer drifting through the trees, softening distance and light.
3. **Eucalyptus and Monterey cypress** — tall peeling eucalyptus and wind-bent cypress framing the road.
4. **Bison paddock** — brown American bison grazing behind a fence on a sandy meadow; the sprint landmark.
5. **Dutch Windmill** — the white-sailed North Windmill above the Queen Wilhelmina tulip garden at the western end.
6. **Spreckels Lake** — still lake with model sailboats and ducks, ringed by cypress beside the drive.
7. **Conservatory of Flowers** — white Victorian glasshouse and palm-lined lawns near the eastern start.
8. **Roller skaters and walkers** — skaters, families and joggers sharing the closed road.
9. **Stow Lake stonework** — rustic Roman-style stone bridges and pedal boats on green water.
10. **Ocean Beach finish** — the Great Highway, dunes and Pacific surf beyond the windmill.
