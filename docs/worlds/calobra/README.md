# Sa Calobra, Mallorca
The Sa Calobra climb. A rhythmic hairpin-by-hairpin climb from the sea, out of the saddle through every switchback.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Knot (Nus de la Corbata)** — the road loops under itself in a 270-degree tie-knot bridge
2. **Endless hairpins** — 26 tight switchbacks stacked up the rock face, visible above and below
3. **Grey limestone cliffs** — sheer pale karst walls and rock fins the road squeezes between
4. **Narrow road with low walls** — smooth asphalt, waist-high stone parapets, no centre line on bends
5. **Mediterranean Sea** — deep blue cove and the port of Sa Calobra at the bottom of the climb
6. **Serra de Tramuntana peaks** — Puig Major and jagged ridges on the skyline
7. **Holm oaks and pines** — dark green scrub, wild olives and pines clinging to the slopes
8. **Training-camp cyclists** — packs of riders in team kit, plus tour coaches squeezing around bends
9. **Coll dels Reis** — the summit pass with its roadside cafe and riders regrouping
10. **Bright island sun** — hard light, strong shadows in the gorge, haze over the sea
