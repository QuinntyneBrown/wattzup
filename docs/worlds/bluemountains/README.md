# Blue Mountains, Jamaica
The coffee road. A lush, twisting mountain climb through coffee farms and cloud forest, bass lines rolling up from the bends.

## 10 critical details
1. **Hairpin switchbacks** — narrow, broken-edged road looping tightly up the mountainside with no centre line.
2. **Coffee terraces** — glossy-leaved Blue Mountain coffee bushes on steep terraced slopes, red cherries ripening.
3. **Blue Mountain Peak** — the misty blue-green ridgeline and peak, Jamaica's highest point, above the valleys.
4. **Cloud forest mist** — low clouds drifting through the trees and across the road in the afternoon.
5. **Tropical vegetation** — tree ferns, bamboo, banana plants and flame-red African tulip trees on the verges.
6. **Roadside sound systems** — speaker stacks at rum shops and yards playing reggae and dancehall.
7. **Rum shops and stalls** — painted wooden shacks in red, gold and green selling food and drinks.
8. **Coffee works and villages** — Mavis Bank and Hagley Gap's buildings, coffee drying barbecues and churches.
9. **Valley views to Kingston** — glimpses down the Yallahs valley to Kingston and the sea far below.
10. **Rough road surface** — potholes, gullies, drainage channels and landslip patches along the road.
