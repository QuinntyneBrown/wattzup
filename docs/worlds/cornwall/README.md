# Cornish Harbour
The harbour quay. A punchy seaside dash: drop down steep lanes, sprint flat along the quay, then kick back up between cottages.

## 10 critical details
1. **Harbour full of boats** — blue, red and white fishing boats moored or beached on the sand at low tide
2. **Granite quay walls** — massive stone harbour arms with iron bollards, ladders, lobster pots and nets
3. **Slate-roofed cottages** — whitewashed and pastel cottages stacked up the hillside above the harbour
4. **Steep narrow lanes** — tight, walled lanes with granite kerbs climbing out of the harbour
5. **Pasty shops and cafes** — painted shopfronts, chalkboards, ice cream signs and hanging baskets
6. **Seagulls** — herring gulls perched on roofs and walls, swooping over the quay
7. **Turquoise sea and tide** — clear green-blue water, wet sand and seaweed at low tide
8. **Cliffs and headlands** — green-topped cliffs, coast path and a lighthouse at the harbour mouth
9. **Visitors and locals** — fishermen, holidaymakers and dogs on the quay, cars squeezing through
10. **Changeable light** — bright sun between showers, sparkling water, glistening wet stone
