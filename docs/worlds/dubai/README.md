# Dubai Marina
The marina walk. A glittering dusk sprint race along the water, flat-out bursts past superyachts beneath glowing glass towers.

## 10 critical details
1. **Cayan Tower** — the twisting 90-degree helix tower glowing ahead against the dusk sky.
2. **Glass skyscraper canyon** — dense residential towers lining both banks, windows lighting up as dusk falls.
3. **Marina water** — the long man-made canal with towers mirrored in calm water.
4. **Superyachts at pontoons** — white yachts and dhow cruisers moored along the walk, deck lights on.
5. **Dusk sky** — orange to violet sky behind the towers, the city lights taking over.
6. **Marina Walk surface** — smooth pale stone promenade with metal railings above the water.
7. **Palms in planters** — date palms in square planters spaced along the walk, uplit at night.
8. **Crowds at barriers** — spectators in white kandura and bright summer colours along the race barriers.
9. **Lit cafés and podium** — waterfront terraces, shisha cafés and shops glowing at the podium level.
10. **Bridges and Ain Dubai** — pedestrian bridges over the marina and the giant wheel on Bluewaters beyond.
