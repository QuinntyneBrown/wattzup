# Seven Sisters Cliffs
The South Downs road. A rolling coastal ride over the chalk downs, one hard effort up each of the seven summits.

## 10 critical details
1. **White chalk cliffs** — the Seven Sisters' sheer white faces dipping and rising in seven summits along the sea.
2. **Coastguard Cottages** — the white row of cottages above Cuckmere Haven, the classic cliff view.
3. **Belle Tout lighthouse** — the squat white lighthouse on the clifftop toward Beachy Head.
4. **Downland roads** — narrow lanes and the A259 rolling over short-grazed green hills with white lines.
5. **Chalk tracks** — white chalk-and-flint paths cutting across the turf.
6. **Cuckmere meanders** — the river's looping bends across the flat valley floor to the sea.
7. **Shingle beach** — grey flint pebbles and surf at the foot of the cliffs at Birling Gap.
8. **Open grass and gorse** — sheep-grazed turf, gorse and hawthorn with few trees on the tops.
9. **English Channel** — wide grey-blue sea and big sky beside every climb.
10. **Field furniture** — wooden gates, fence posts, flint walls and National Trust signs.
