# Mount Tamalpais
The Panoramic Highway. A twisting, shaded climb through redwoods that bursts out onto open ridges with the whole bay below.

## 10 critical details
1. **Redwood groves** — towering coast redwoods shading the lower road near Muir Woods, cool and dim
2. **Panoramic Highway** — narrow two-lane road, double yellow line, tight bends cut into the hillside
3. **Golden Gate view** — the bridge's red towers poking through fog, San Francisco beyond
4. **Fog layer** — marine fog rolling in below the ridge, riders climbing into sunshine above it
5. **Golden grassland ridges** — summer-gold grass and oak-studded slopes on the upper mountain
6. **Pacific Ocean** — Stinson Beach and the ocean spread out far below Ridgecrest Boulevard
7. **Bay Area cyclists** — packs of road riders in team kit; a famous local training climb
8. **Chaparral and manzanita** — red-barked manzanita, coyote brush and serpentine rock on the verges
9. **East Peak and fire lookout** — the summit lookout cabin and rocky top at 784 m
10. **Wooden guard rails and turnouts** — rustic rails, gravel pull-outs and trailhead signs along the road
