# Teotihuacán
The Avenue of the Dead. A hot, straight, high-plateau drag race down the ancient avenue, sprinting between the Pyramids of the Sun and the Moon.

## 10 critical details
1. **Avenue of the Dead** — the dead-straight ceremonial avenue of packed earth and stone, kilometres long.
2. **Pyramid of the Sun** — the colossal stepped pyramid on the east side, dominating every view.
3. **Pyramid of the Moon** — the pyramid closing the north end of the avenue, the finishing target.
4. **Stepped platforms and stairways** — low talud-tablero platforms and stone stairs lining both sides.
5. **Temple of the Feathered Serpent** — carved serpent heads on the Ciudadela at the south end.
6. **Dry highland scrub** — yellow grass, agave, prickly pear and pepper trees around the ruins.
7. **Volcanic stone textures** — rough grey-brown tezontle masonry studded with small chinking stones.
8. **Hot-air balloons at dawn** — colourful balloons drifting over the pyramids in the morning.
9. **Visitors and vendors** — tourists climbing the steps, vendors with hats, jaguar whistles and crafts.
10. **Bright high-altitude sun** — intense light, deep blue sky with towering clouds, distant mountains.
