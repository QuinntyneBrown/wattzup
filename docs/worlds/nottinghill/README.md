# Notting Hill Carnival
Ladbroke Grove. Racing should feel like threading a bass-shaking street party, weaving past feathered mas bands under London terraces.

## 10 critical details
1. **Ladbroke Grove** — the wide, gently curving road framed by plane trees and white stucco terraces.
2. **Mas band costumes** — feathered headdresses, sequins and wings in bright colours dancing in the road ahead.
3. **Sound system stacks** — towering speaker walls on street corners and lorries, bass you feel in the frame.
4. **Steel pan floats** — pan sides on flatbed trucks rolling slowly with the procession.
5. **Flags of the Caribbean** — Trinidad, Jamaica, Barbados flags waved by the crowd and draped from windows.
6. **Packed pavements** — spectators lining both kerbs behind barriers, phones up, cheering riders through.
7. **Victorian terraces** — tall pastel and white houses with balconies full of onlookers.
8. **Westway flyover** — the concrete A40 crossing Ladbroke Grove, a dark arch to ride beneath.
9. **Food stalls and smoke** — jerk-chicken drum grills sending smoke across the street.
10. **Litter, whistles and paint** — whistles, horns, confetti and powder colour on the tarmac in August daylight.
