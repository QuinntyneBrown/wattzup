# Fish Hill, Cotswolds
The Broadway escarpment. A gritty English hill-climb out of a honey-stone village, up a steep wooded escarpment to the tower on the ridge.

## 10 critical details
1. **Broadway's honey-stone High Street** — golden Cotswold-stone houses, wide grass verges and shop fronts at the foot.
2. **The Fish Hill climb** — the steep A44 ramp up the escarpment with tight bends and a cutting.
3. **Broadway Tower** — the folly on the ridge top, visible from the climb and the finishing goal.
4. **Dry-stone walls** — mortarless limestone walls lining the lanes and fields.
5. **Beech woodland on the scarp** — tall beeches arching over the road on the steepest section.
6. **Sheep pastures and rolling wolds** — green fields, sheep and hedgerows on the open tops.
7. **Vale of Evesham view** — wide patchwork farmland stretching away below the escarpment.
8. **Stone-slate roofs and thatch** — mossy stone-tiled roofs, cottage gardens and wisteria.
9. **English road furniture** — white lines, red-bordered gradient signs, finger posts and red phone boxes.
10. **Soft English light** — mild overcast or watery sun, damp tarmac and lush green.
