# Andalusian White Villages
The white-village road. A sun-baked rolling race between hilltop white towns and silver olive groves.

## 10 critical details
1. **Hilltop white villages** — Zahara, Grazalema and Olvera stacked white on crags, crowned by castles.
2. **Moorish castle towers** — ruined stone towers above Zahara de la Sierra and Olvera.
3. **Olive groves** — endless silvery-green rows on rolling ochre hills.
4. **Winding mountain road** — narrow asphalt curves with white-painted edges and low stone walls.
5. **Whitewashed streets** — narrow lanes, iron window grilles, flower pots and terracotta roofs.
6. **Ronda's gorge and bridge** — Puente Nuevo spanning the deep El Tajo gorge.
7. **Sierra de Grazalema** — rugged limestone ridges with oak and pinsapo forest.
8. **Turquoise reservoir** — Zahara-El Gastor reservoir below the village.
9. **Church bell towers** — tall towers rising above the white houses.
10. **Intense southern sun** — bright white glare, blue sky, dry heat and hard shadows.
