# Utrecht Bike City
The canal wharves. A nimble, technical city race along sunken canal wharves and red bike paths, threading through a sea of bicycles.

## 10 critical details
1. **Oudegracht wharves** — the unique two-level canal: brick wharves at water level, cellars turned cafes
2. **Dom Tower** — the 112 m Gothic tower, the city's tallest, visible from everywhere
3. **Stationsplein bike park** — the world's largest bicycle garage, 12,500 racks under the station square
4. **Red asphalt bike paths** — dedicated red cycle lanes with white markings and shark-teeth
5. **Bikes everywhere** — racks packed with Dutch bikes, cargo bikes, crowds of cyclists at lights
6. **Canal bridges** — low brick arched bridges with iron railings over the Oudegracht and Nieuwegracht
7. **Gabled canal houses** — narrow brick houses, shuttered windows and old trading-house facades
8. **Trees and greenery** — linden and elm trees over the canals, flower boxes, ivy on walls
9. **Canal boats** — small open boats, canoes and tour boats on the dark green water
10. **Dutch weather and light** — soft grey sky, sudden sun, wet cobbles and brick reflections
