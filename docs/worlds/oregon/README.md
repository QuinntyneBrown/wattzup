# Oregon Coast
Highway 101. A cool, misty Pacific coast ride along headlands and beaches, rolling past sea stacks and lighthouses through dripping spruce.

## 10 critical details
1. **Highway 101 shoulders** — two-lane coastal highway with wide shoulders, guardrails and cliff-edge curves.
2. **Haystack Rock** — the giant basalt sea stack at Cannon Beach, the signature landmark.
3. **Sea stacks and arches** — dark rock islands and arches standing in the surf along the shore.
4. **Heceta Head and Yaquina lighthouses** — white towers with red-roofed keepers' houses on the headlands.
5. **Sitka spruce forest** — tall, mossy, wind-bent spruce lining the road and headlands.
6. **Coastal fog and drizzle** — low marine fog, damp tarmac and soft diffuse light.
7. **Wide grey-sand beaches** — flat beaches with driftwood logs piled at the high-tide line.
8. **Steel bridges** — Conde McCullough's arched bridges over the coastal river mouths.
9. **Pacific surf** — big rolling swells, white spray against black rocks.
10. **Viewpoints and coastal towns** — scenic pullouts, brown signs and small weathered-shingle towns.
