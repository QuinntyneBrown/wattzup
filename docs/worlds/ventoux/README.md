# Mont Ventoux
Chalet Reynard. A relentless, exposed grind up the Giant of Provence, fighting the mistral across bare white scree to the summit tower.

## 10 critical details
1. **Bare white limestone scree** — the moonscape above Chalet Reynard, blinding pale stone either side of the road.
2. **Summit weather station and red-white mast** — the observatory tower visible ahead for kilometres, the goal of every effort.
3. **Chalet Reynard** — the wooden mountain restaurant at the junction where the forest ends and the bare slopes begin.
4. **Narrow, grippy asphalt** — a single-lane-wide climbing road with painted rider names and white edge lines.
5. **Tom Simpson memorial** — the granite stele near the summit, decorated with bottles and caps left by riders.
6. **Kilometre markers** — the yellow-and-white roadside posts counting down distance and gradient.
7. **Mistral wind** — a strong, clear-sky wind; flags and jackets snapping, low stone walls as shelter.
8. **Cedar and pine forest below** — the wooded lower slopes giving way abruptly to scree.
9. **Provence panorama** — lavender plains, the Alps and Vaucluse valleys far below in clear light.
10. **Amateur cyclists and roadside fans** — riders standing on pedals, spectators and cars parked on the verges.
