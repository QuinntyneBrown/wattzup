# Westminster Rush Hour
Westminster Bridge. A frantic dusk dash through London traffic, elbow to elbow with cabs and buses as Big Ben looms.

## 10 critical details
1. **Elizabeth Tower (Big Ben)** — gilded clock face lit at dusk, dominating the view at the bridge's west end
2. **Palace of Westminster** — long Gothic riverfront facade along the Thames, floodlit gold against a blue sky
3. **Westminster Bridge** — green-painted cast-iron arches and parapets, Gothic lamp standards lining both pavements
4. **Red double-decker buses** — Routemaster-style and modern red buses filling lanes, overtaken and drafted by riders
5. **Black cabs** — TX-style taxis queueing and weaving, amber "TAXI" lights glowing
6. **London Eye and County Hall** — the giant wheel lit on the South Bank across the river
7. **Dusk light** — fading orange sky, sodium and LED streetlights, headlights and wet-looking tarmac reflections
8. **Pedestrians and tourists** — crowded pavements, phones raised, people crossing at zebras and signals
9. **Road markings and street furniture** — red bus lanes, zigzag lines, traffic lights, red phone boxes, bollards
10. **Parliament Square and Whitehall** — statues, lawn and grand stone buildings as the route turns off the bridge
