# Mount Fuji Road
The Subaru Line. A steady, ramping climb through dense forest, Fuji's cone growing ever bigger ahead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Fuji Subaru Line** — the smooth, wide toll road switching back up the mountain's north flank.
2. **Mount Fuji's cone** — the symmetrical, snow-capped volcano filling the windscreen near the top.
3. **Dense Aokigahara-style forest** — thick conifer and birch forest on the lower slopes.
4. **Larch and dwarf trees higher up** — trees thinning into stunted larch near the treeline.
5. **Volcanic scoria** — red-brown and black lava gravel on the upper slopes and verges.
6. **Fifth station** — the Kawaguchiko 5th Station shops, shrine and buses at the summit.
7. **Altitude markers and signs** — Japanese road signs, altitude boards and kilometre posts.
8. **Fuji Five Lakes below** — Lake Kawaguchi and the Yamanashi plain far below.
9. **Sea of clouds** — cloud layers below the road as the climb rises above them.
10. **Crisp clear light** — cool, clear mountain air with sharp blue sky.
