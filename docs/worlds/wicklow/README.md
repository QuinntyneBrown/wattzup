# Wicklow Gap
The Wicklow Gap road. A raw, rolling Irish road race over open bog and heather, wind in the face to the top of the gap.

## 10 critical details
1. **R756 over the gap** — the narrow, grippy mountain road climbing from Hollywood to Laragh through the pass.
2. **Blanket bog** — dark brown peat, tussocks and black bog pools stretching either side of the road.
3. **Purple heather and gorse** — heather on the slopes and yellow gorse flowering at the road edge.
4. **Bare rounded mountains** — Tonelagee and Camaderry, smooth, treeless and brown-green under big skies.
5. **Glendalough** — the glacial valley, upper lake and round tower on the descent toward Laragh.
6. **Turlough Hill** — the pumped-storage reservoir and its access road near the summit of the gap.
7. **Granite boulders and walls** — scattered granite erratics and low stone walls beside the tarmac.
8. **Conifer plantations** — dark Sitka spruce blocks on the lower valley sides.
9. **Wicklow sheep** — hardy blackface sheep loose on the open mountain and the road.
10. **Soft Irish weather** — low cloud, drizzle and sudden sun shafts over the hills.
