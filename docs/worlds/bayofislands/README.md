# Bay of Islands Group Ride
The bay road. A sociable bunch race along sheltered turquoise bays and green Northland hills.

## 10 critical details
1. **Coastal bay road** — two-lane road hugging the shore with yellow centre lines and grassy verges.
2. **Turquoise sheltered bays** — calm clear water and small sandy coves beside the road.
3. **Bush-covered islands** — rocky islets topped with native bush scattered across the bay.
4. **Paihia waterfront** — low shops, cafes and the green-roofed wharf along Marsden Road.
5. **Russell village** — historic white weatherboard buildings and the Strand under pohutukawa.
6. **Pohutukawa trees** — gnarled coastal trees overhanging road and beach.
7. **Waitangi Treaty Grounds** — carved meeting house, flagstaff lawn and the bridge over the inlet.
8. **Yachts and ferries** — sailboats, ferries and the tall-ship mast in the bay.
9. **Rolling green hills** — farmland and bush-clad hills behind the coast.
10. **Clear subtropical light** — bright sun, fluffy clouds, golden sunset over the water.
