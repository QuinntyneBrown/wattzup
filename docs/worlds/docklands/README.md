# Neon Docklands
Harbour Bridges Loop. A fast night crit through a glowing port, every bridge ramp a one-minute punch over black water.

## 10 critical details
1. **Bridge ramps** — cable-stayed and arched bridges (Erasmusbrug, Bolte, Webb style) carry every effort up over the docks.
2. **Night sky** — deep blue dusk to black; the whole loop is lit only by city and bridge light.
3. **Neon and LED lighting** — coloured cable lights and signage glow cyan, magenta and green along the route.
4. **Reflections on water** — still harbour water mirrors every light; ripples break the streaks beside the road.
5. **Brick warehouses** — Speicherstadt-style red-brick wharf buildings floodlit gold line the canal stretches.
6. **Glass towers** — a lit skyline of high-rise offices and apartments stands behind the quays.
7. **Quayside furniture** — mooring bollards, timber piles, railings and lamp posts edge the road at the water.
8. **Moored ships** — tall ships, cruise liners and ferries lit up alongside the wharves give scale.
9. **Wet tarmac** — dark, damp road surface catching light streaks, with crisp white lane markings.
10. **Waterfront life** — lit bars, restaurants and a few walkers on the promenade keep the docks alive.
