# Sydney Harbour Fireworks
The Harbour Bridge. A New Year's Eve night ride over the Coathanger, sprinting under bursts of fireworks above the harbour.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sydney Harbour Bridge** — the steel through-arch overhead, its granite pylons and lit girders framing the road.
2. **Midnight fireworks** — huge bursts and cascades fired from the bridge span and barges, lighting the sky.
3. **Sydney Opera House** — the white shell sails on Bennelong Point, lit and reflecting fireworks.
4. **Bridge deck road** — the wide Bradfield Highway lanes with the arch and cables either side.
5. **Harbour water** — dark harbour mirroring colour bursts, ferries and spectator boats with lights.
6. **CBD skyline** — the lit towers of Circular Quay and the city behind the bridge.
7. **Crowds at the foreshore** — packed crowds at the Opera House steps, Mrs Macquarie's Chair and the Rocks.
8. **Smoke and glow** — drifting firework smoke catching coloured light, haze over the water.
9. **Bridge effects** — the bridge-mounted pyrotechnics waterfall and the projected lighting on the arch.
10. **Luna Park and Milsons Point** — the north shore lights and Luna Park face at the bridge's end.
