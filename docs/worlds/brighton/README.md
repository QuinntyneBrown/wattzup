# Brighton Pride
Madeira Drive. A joyful, flag-waving seafront race alongside the Pride parade, the pier glittering ahead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Madeira Drive** — the long seafront road below the cliff, lined with barriers and crowds on Pride day.
2. **Rainbow flags everywhere** — Pride and progress flags on lamp posts, balconies, bunting and in hands.
3. **The Pride parade** — floats, dancers and marching groups in colour moving along the seafront.
4. **Brighton Palace Pier** — the white Victorian pier with its domes, rides and lights over the sea.
5. **Madeira Terraces** — the long green cast-iron arcade and lift tower under the cliff.
6. **Shingle beach and the sea** — pebble beach, groynes and the grey-green Channel beside the route.
7. **Volk's Electric Railway** — the little seafront railway running alongside Madeira Drive.
8. **Regency seafront** — cream stucco terraces and hotels on Marine Parade above.
9. **British Airways i360** — the slender observation tower rising along the seafront to the west.
10. **Festival crowds** — glitter, costumes and cheering spectators packed against the barriers.
