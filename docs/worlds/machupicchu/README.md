# Machu Picchu Road
The Hiram Bingham road. A humid, switchbacking climb through cloud forest, grinding up from the river to the Inca citadel on its ridge.

## 10 critical details
1. **Hiram Bingham switchbacks** — a narrow zigzag road of packed dirt and patched asphalt carved up the mountainside.
2. **Machu Picchu citadel** — the granite terraces, temples and walls on the saddle at the top.
3. **Huayna Picchu peak** — the steep green sugarloaf rising behind the ruins, the iconic backdrop.
4. **Drifting cloud and mist** — low cloud wrapping the peaks and spilling through the valley.
5. **Urubamba River gorge** — the roaring brown river deep in the canyon at the foot of the climb.
6. **Cloud-forest vegetation** — dense tropical green, bromeliads, ferns and orchids along the verges.
7. **Shuttle buses** — Consettur buses grinding up and down the hairpins, passing riders.
8. **Aguas Calientes** — the town and bridge at the river where the climb begins.
9. **Inca stone terraces and steps** — dry-stone agricultural terraces stepping down the slopes.
10. **Sheer jungle-clad peaks** — steep green Andean mountains on every side of the gorge.
