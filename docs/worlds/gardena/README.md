# Passo Gardena
The road from Selva. A car-free summer climb up hairpins between the Sella towers and the Cir spires.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sella towers** — sheer pale rock walls rising on the right throughout the climb.
2. **Cir spires** — jagged needle peaks on the left above the hairpins.
3. **Hairpins to the saddle** — stacked switchbacks visible above across green meadows.
4. **Car-free road** — Sellaronda Bike Day: the road full of cyclists, no traffic.
5. **Larch meadows** — bright alpine pastures dotted with larches and pines.
6. **Grazing cows** — cattle with bells on the lower meadows.
7. **Pass-top huts** — rifugi and hotels with car parks at the Passo Gardena saddle.
8. **Sassolungo backdrop** — the Langkofel massif looking back down toward Selva.
9. **Wooden hay barns** — dark timber huts scattered across the slopes.
10. **Bright summer morning** — clear blue sky, puffy clouds, crisp alpine light on the rock.
