# Knicks Championship Parade
Canyon of Heroes. A roaring float-to-float sprint up lower Broadway through a blizzard of ticker tape toward the Woolworth Building.

## 10 critical details
1. **Lower Broadway's Canyon of Heroes** — the straight avenue from Bowling Green to City Hall, walled by tall towers.
2. **Ticker tape and streamers** — paper and orange-and-blue streamers drifting down constantly and littering the asphalt.
3. **Woolworth Building's copper crown** — the neo-Gothic tower at the far end, the goal of the final surge.
4. **Parade floats and open buses** — Knicks floats and double-decker buses rolling ahead as sprint targets.
5. **Packed barricades** — blue NYPD barricades holding fans ten deep along both sidewalks.
6. **Fans in orange and blue** — jerseys, flags and signs, people leaning from office windows above.
7. **Canyon Of Heroes plaques and street furniture** — granite sidewalk plaques, lamp posts, traffic lights and street signs.
8. **Charging Bull and Bowling Green** — the bronze bull and the park at the parade's start.
9. **Trinity Church and St. Paul's Chapel** — the spire and churchyard glimpsed along the route.
10. **Shafts of sunlight between towers** — deep shade with bright slices of daylight across the road.
