# Furka Pass
The Furka hairpins. A high-alpine hairpin battle, swinging bend after bend beside a glacier toward a 2,429 m summit.

## 10 critical details
1. **Rhône Glacier** — the retreating ice tongue and its meltwater lake below the road, white blankets on the ice
2. **Hotel Belvédère** — the shuttered hotel perched on the famous hairpin right beside the glacier
3. **Hairpin stack** — tight switchbacks with low stone walls looping up the slope above Gletsch
4. **Gletsch** — the hamlet at the valley floor where Furka and Grimsel roads meet
5. **Grey granite and scree** — bare rock, boulders and scree slopes above the treeline
6. **Snow walls and patches** — lingering snow beside the road in early summer
7. **Swiss road furniture** — yellow postbus, white-red delineator posts, yellow pass signs, stone kerbs
8. **Alpine pasture** — short green turf, alpine flowers and grazing cows lower down
9. **Distant peaks** — Galenstock, Dammastock and the Bernese Alps on the skyline
10. **Mountain light** — crisp blue sky, strong sun, clouds boiling up from the valleys
