# Mount Etna
The Sapienza road. A relentless climb across black lava fields toward a smoking summit, heat shimmering off the tarmac.

## 10 critical details
1. **Black lava fields** — rough jet-black clinker flows either side of the road, edges sharp and broken.
2. **SP92 hairpins** — smooth dark asphalt switchbacks cutting through old lava flows with low stone kerbs.
3. **Smoking summit** — Etna's broad cone with a white plume trailing from the craters, always ahead.
4. **Silvestri craters** — red-brown cinder cones beside Rifugio Sapienza, people walking their rims.
5. **Rifugio Sapienza** — chalet-style refuge, souvenir shops and car parks at the road's top.
6. **Rust-red scoria** — reddish ash and oxidised rock contrasting the black slopes.
7. **Pioneer vegetation** — yellow broom, low shrubs and sparse birch and pine on older flows.
8. **Cable car** — red gondolas climbing the ash slope above the refuge.
9. **Sea and plain below** — Catania plain and Ionian coast in haze far down the mountain.
10. **Harsh Sicilian sun** — strong high light, deep shadows, occasional cloud drifting across the cone.
