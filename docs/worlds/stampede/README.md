# Calgary Stampede
The parade route. Racing should feel like riding a rowdy western parade: cowboy hats, chuckwagons thundering and the whole city cheering.

## 10 critical details
1. **White cowboy hats** — the crowd along the kerbs in white Smithbilts, plaid shirts, jeans and boots.
2. **Parade floats and marching bands** — decorated floats, brass bands and riders on horseback ahead.
3. **Chuckwagons** — canvas-topped wagons with four-horse teams racing round the half-mile track.
4. **Grandstand and rodeo arena** — the Stampede grandstand and dirt infield with bucking chutes.
5. **Downtown towers** — Calgary's glass skyscrapers and the Calgary Tower above the street.
6. **Midway** — Ferris wheel, rides and food stands at Stampede Park.
7. **Indigenous participants** — First Nations regalia and tipis of Elbow River Camp.
8. **Flags and bunting** — Canadian and Stampede flags, western storefront decorations.
9. **Hay bales and wooden fences** — rustic set dressing along barriers and storefronts.
10. **Prairie summer sun** — big blue July sky, bright light and the Rockies faint on the horizon.
