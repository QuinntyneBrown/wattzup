# Valparaíso Stairways
The hillside stairways. A zigzagging, colourful climb through the cerros, steep pitches and funicular-side ramps above the Pacific port.

## 10 critical details
1. **Painted houses** — corrugated-iron and timber houses in bright yellows, blues and reds stacked up the hills.
2. **Funiculars (ascensores)** — little wooden cars like Ascensor Reina Victoria and El Peral climbing steep rails.
3. **Street-art murals** — murals covering walls, stairs and doors across Cerro Alegre and Cerro Concepción.
4. **Steep stairways** — painted steps, including the piano-key Pasaje Piano and rainbow stairs.
5. **Narrow winding lanes** — cobbled and concrete streets hairpinning up the cerros with tight kerbs.
6. **Port and bay views** — the harbour cranes, container ships and Pacific bay beneath the hills.
7. **Overhead cables** — dense tangles of electricity and phone lines strung across every street.
8. **Trolleybuses and street life** — vintage trolleybuses below, dogs, cafés and artists on the hills.
9. **Balconies and miradores** — Paseo Gervasoni, Paseo Yugoslavo and other viewpoints with railings.
10. **Coastal light** — bright Pacific sun, morning sea fog (camanchaca) and pink evening light.
