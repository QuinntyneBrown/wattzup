# Mongolian Steppe
The steppe track. Racing should feel like flying across an endless green ocean of grass, horizon wide open, wild horses running alongside.

## 10 critical details
1. **Endless grassland** — rolling green steppe to every horizon, no fences, no trees, no road markings.
2. **Dirt double-track** — twin wheel ruts worn into the grass, branching and rejoining across the plain.
3. **White gers** — round felt yurts with orange doors standing alone or in small camps.
4. **Wild horses** — Przewalski's and herded Mongolian horses galloping in groups across the track.
5. **Huge sky** — deep blue sky with towering cumulus casting moving shadows on the land.
6. **Ovoo cairns** — stone cairns wrapped in blue khadag scarves on hilltops.
7. **Herders on horseback** — riders in deel robes driving sheep, goats and yaks.
8. **Low rolling hills** — smooth treeless ridges and distant mountains in haze.
9. **Wildflowers and gravel** — summer flowers in the grass and stony patches on the track.
10. **Golden evening light** — long low sun turning the grass gold toward sunset.
