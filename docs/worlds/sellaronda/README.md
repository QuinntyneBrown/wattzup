# Sella Ronda
The four passes. Racing should feel like a grand Dolomite loop, climbing and plunging over four passes around one colossal rock fortress.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sella massif** — the vertical grey-gold limestone walls of the Sella group always at the centre of the loop.
2. **Four passes** — Passo Pordoi, Sella, Gardena and Campolongo, each with its own summit sign and refuge.
3. **Hairpin ribbons** — tight numbered switchbacks visible snaking up green slopes below.
4. **Sassolungo and Marmolada** — Sassolungo's tower and Marmolada's glacier dominating the views.
5. **Alpine meadows** — lush green pasture dotted with wooden hay barns and wildflowers.
6. **Ski lift infrastructure** — cable cars, chairlift pylons and summer-quiet pistes.
7. **Rifugi and pass hotels** — stone-and-timber rifugi with terraces and flags at the cols.
8. **Valley villages** — Arabba, Canazei, Selva and Corvara with onion-dome churches.
9. **Cyclists and motorbikes** — many riders on the passes, especially on summer Sella Ronda Bike Day.
10. **Alpenglow light** — rose-coloured evening light on the pale dolomite rock.
