# Chicago Lakefront Trail
The Lakefront Trail. A fast, flat waterfront race between sand beaches and the skyline, wind off the lake.

## 10 critical details
1. **Lakefront Trail path** — wide paved trail with painted centre and lane markings, curving with the shore.
2. **Lake Michigan** — turquoise-blue water to the horizon, waves breaking on the revetment.
3. **Downtown skyline** — Willis Tower, John Hancock and tower clusters always ahead across the water.
4. **Sand beaches** — North Avenue and Oak Street beaches with people, umbrellas and lifeguard chairs.
5. **Concrete revetment steps** — stepped concrete sea wall where people sit by the water.
6. **North Avenue Beach House** — ship-shaped beach house as a landmark.
7. **Lincoln Park greenery** — lawns, mature trees and willows on the inland side.
8. **Harbours and sailboats** — marinas full of masts beside the trail.
9. **Trail users** — runners, cyclists, Divvy bikes and skaters filling the path.
10. **Clear summer light** — bright blue sky with crisp shadows; occasional lake fog rolling in.
