# Surfers Paradise
The Esplanade. A sunny, fast beachfront blast along the Gold Coast, sprinting beneath the towers with the surf beside you.

## 10 critical details
1. **High-rise wall** — a dense line of beachfront towers right behind the sand, glass and white concrete.
2. **Q1 tower** — the slender gold-topped spire that dominates the skyline as the landmark ahead.
3. **Golden beach** — wide, flat pale sand with wet reflective shoreline and swimmers.
4. **Pacific surf** — long rolling breakers, white foam and surfers out the back.
5. **Esplanade road** — two-lane beachfront street with palms, parked cars and the foreshore path beside it.
6. **Surfers Paradise arch** — the curved steel "Surfers Paradise" sign at the end of Cavill Avenue.
7. **Lifesaver flags** — red-and-yellow patrol flags, lifeguard towers and beach warning signs.
8. **Palms and pandanus** — tropical palms and coastal shrubs on the foreshore dunes.
9. **G:link tram** — the yellow light-rail trams on the Gold Coast Highway behind the beach.
10. **Subtropical sun** — clear blue sky, puffy cumulus and strong light; golden at sunrise.
