# Victoria Harbour
The harbourfront. A fast, high-cadence spin along the promenade with Hong Kong's skyline blazing across the water.

## 10 critical details
1. **Hong Kong Island skyline** — IFC, Bank of China and the Central towers lit up across the harbour.
2. **Tsim Sha Tsui promenade** — the wide waterfront walk on the Kowloon side with railings and lamps.
3. **Star Ferry** — green-and-white ferries crossing the harbour between piers.
4. **Symphony of Lights** — the nightly laser and light show beaming from the skyscrapers.
5. **Avenue of Stars** — the promenade's star plaques and Bruce Lee statue.
6. **Clock Tower** — the old Kowloon-Canton Railway clock tower at the start of the front.
7. **Victoria Peak backdrop** — dark green hills rising behind the island's towers.
8. **Harbour traffic** — junks with red sails, tugs and container ships moving on the water.
9. **Neon and LED façades** — huge glowing billboards and coloured building lighting on both shores.
10. **Night crowds** — tourists, joggers and photographers lining the rail as the show plays.
