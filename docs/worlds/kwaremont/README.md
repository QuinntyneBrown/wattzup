# Oude Kwaremont
The Kwaremont cobbles. Racing should feel like the Tour of Flanders decider: a long rattling cobbled drag through a roaring wall of beer-tent fans.

## 10 critical details
1. **Long cobbled drag** — 2.2 km of rough, uneven setts climbing gently, steepest in the village section.
2. **Kwaremont village** — brick houses and cafés crowding the cobbles near the top.
3. **Beer tents and VIP marquees** — white tents, sound systems and bars along the climb.
4. **Flanders lion flags** — black lion on yellow everywhere, plus club and rider banners.
5. **Deep crowds** — fans packed several rows deep behind barriers, bellowing at every rider.
6. **Gutter lines** — narrow smoother strips at the edge that riders fight for.
7. **Open farmland views** — rolling Flemish Ardennes fields and hedges beside the upper section.
8. **Bare spring trees** — budding trees and hedgerows under grey April skies.
9. **Painted names and signs** — rider names on the road, km banners and Ronde signage.
10. **Damp, grey light** — overcast April weather, wet stones and muddy verges.
