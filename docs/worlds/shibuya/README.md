# Shibuya Crossing
Dogenzaka. A rain-slick neon night sprint, high-cadence spinning through the scramble under giant video screens.

## 10 critical details
1. **The scramble crossing** — the famous all-way zebra crossing with crowds flooding every direction at once.
2. **Giant video screens** — QFRONT, 109 and station-front screens blazing colour over the junction.
3. **Neon rain** — wet asphalt mirroring signs and screens, rain streaks and clear umbrellas.
4. **Dogenzaka slope** — the climbing street of signs, karaoke bars and love hotels toward the hilltop.
5. **Shibuya 109** — the cylindrical silver tower marking the fork of Dogenzaka and Bunkamura-dori.
6. **Vertical signage** — stacked kanji neon and illuminated shop signs on every building face.
7. **Crowds with umbrellas** — commuters and shoppers under umbrellas, waiting at kerbs then surging.
8. **Shibuya Station and Hachikō** — the station front, Hachikō statue plaza and JR elevated tracks.
9. **Taxis and street markings** — Tokyo taxis, white road markings and pedestrian signals glowing green.
10. **New towers** — Shibuya Scramble Square and Hikarie lit up above the street.
