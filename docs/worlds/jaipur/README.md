# Holi in Jaipur
Johari Bazaar. A joyful sprint through crowded pink streets, bursting through clouds of festival colour.

## 10 critical details
1. **Pink City facades** — terracotta-pink buildings with white line patterns along the bazaar.
2. **Hawa Mahal** — the honeycomb pink palace facade with hundreds of small windows.
3. **Colour-powder clouds** — gulal bursts of magenta, yellow, green and blue hanging in the air.
4. **Holi crowds** — revellers covered in colour, dancing and throwing powder.
5. **Bazaar arcades** — shop arcades with awnings, signs and stalls under the pink colonnade.
6. **Gates of the walled city** — Sanganeri and Chandpole gates spanning the road.
7. **Street traffic** — auto-rickshaws, scooters and cows on the road.
8. **Colour-stained road** — tarmac splashed with powder and puddles of coloured water.
9. **Chhatris and domes** — small domed pavilions on rooftops; Nahargarh Fort on the ridge.
10. **Warm spring light** — bright March sun through haze and dust.
