# Winterlude, Ottawa
The Rideau Canal. A crisp race along the frozen canal skateway, weaving past skaters, ice sculptures and snowbanks.

## 10 critical details
1. **Rideau Canal Skateway** — wide groomed ice surface between snowbanks; the racing line itself.
2. **Skaters** — families and commuters on skates throughout, creating moving obstacles.
3. **Bridges overhead** — Pretoria Bridge stone towers and concrete overpasses crossing the canal.
4. **Snow sculptures** — large carved white snow figures along the banks.
5. **Ice sculptures** — clear carved ice pieces, lit blue and purple at night.
6. **Snowbanks and stone walls** — piled snow on stone canal walls and steps down to the ice.
7. **Festival tents and huts** — warming huts, BeaverTails stands and white festival marquees.
8. **Ottawa skyline** — Château Laurier and Parliament towers and office blocks beyond the trees.
9. **Bare trees** — leafless elms and maples lining the canal banks.
10. **Bright winter light** — cold blue sky, low sun and glare off snow and ice.
