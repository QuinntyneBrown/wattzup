# Havana Malecón
Malecón. A breezy, spray-soaked seafront blast, timing each surge between the waves breaking over the wall.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Malecón seawall** — the long low concrete wall curving 8 km along the bay, people sitting on it.
2. **Waves crashing over** — Atlantic surf bursting over the wall and sheeting across the road in surges.
3. **Six-lane seafront avenue** — wide, worn asphalt with puddles and salt-stained kerbs from the spray.
4. **Classic American cars** — 1950s Chevrolets and Buicks in candy colours, many as open-top taxis.
5. **Pastel colonnaded façades** — weathered, peeling neoclassical buildings with arcades and balconies facing the sea.
6. **Castillo del Morro** — the fortress and lighthouse across the harbour mouth at the eastern end.
7. **Hotel Nacional** — the twin-towered hotel on its hill above the Vedado stretch.
8. **Fishermen and strollers** — anglers on the wall, couples, musicians and kids dodging the spray.
9. **Coco-taxis and bici-taxis** — yellow egg-shaped scooters and pedal rickshaws sharing the lane.
10. **Golden late light** — warm low sun over the sea, haze and spray catching the light.
