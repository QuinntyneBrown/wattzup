# Big Sur
Highway 1. A dramatic, rolling coastal ride clinging to the Pacific cliffs, surging over Bixby Bridge high above the surf.

## 10 critical details
1. **Bixby Creek Bridge** — the pale concrete open-spandrel arch spanning the canyon, the signature landmark.
2. **Cliff-edge Highway 1** — narrow two-lane road with double yellow lines carved into steep hillsides.
3. **Pacific surf** — deep blue and turquoise water breaking white on rocks far below.
4. **Santa Lucia slopes** — steep golden-grass and chaparral mountainsides dropping straight to the sea.
5. **Marine fog** — banks of grey fog rolling in over the ridges and hiding the coast.
6. **Rocky Creek Bridge** — the smaller white arched bridge north of Bixby among the trees.
7. **McWay Falls** — the waterfall onto the turquoise cove beach in Julia Pfeiffer Burns.
8. **Sea stacks and coves** — dark rock pinnacles and pocket beaches along the shoreline.
9. **Roadside edges** — low guard rails, gravel turnouts and wildflowers on the shoulders.
10. **Cypress and redwood** — wind-shaped Monterey cypress on the headlands and redwoods in the canyons.
