# Old Town Criterium
Cathedral Circuit. Tight, elbow-to-elbow laps on rattling cobbles, with eight short sprints kicking up to the cathedral square.

## 10 critical details
1. **Gothic cathedral** — its spire and west front close every sprint; visible above the rooftops from most of the lap.
2. **Cobbled setts** — rounded, uneven stones with dark joints; the road must look and read as bumpy.
3. **Short steep kick** — each sprint ramps up a narrow cobbled rise into the cathedral square.
4. **Crowd barriers** — metal barriers with sponsor banners lining both sides of the course.
5. **Packed spectators** — fans three deep at the barriers, thickest on the climb and at the line.
6. **Tall old townhouses** — colourful plastered facades with shutters, crowding close to the road.
7. **Tight 90° corners** — narrow street corners with kerbs and bollards the bunch funnels through.
8. **Finish gantry** — inflatable or truss arch with a lap board and bell near the cathedral.
9. **Café terraces** — umbrellas, tables and shopfronts at street level where spectators gather.
10. **Race-day light** — bright midday sun casting hard facade shadows across the cobbles, flags and bunting overhead.
