# Blue Ridge Parkway
The Parkway. A long, flowing ride along an Appalachian ridgeline, every overlook blazing with autumn colour.

## 10 critical details
1. **Winding two-lane Parkway** — smooth, curving tarmac with no centre clutter, following the ridge crest.
2. **Blazing autumn foliage** — maples, oaks and hickories in red, orange and gold on every slope.
3. **Layered blue ridges** — hazy blue mountain ranges fading row after row into the distance.
4. **Stone overlooks** — pull-offs with low stone walls and wooden signs naming the view.
5. **Stone arch bridges and tunnels** — rough-cut stone bridges and rock tunnels on the route.
6. **Linn Cove Viaduct** — the curved concrete viaduct hugging Grandfather Mountain.
7. **Mabry Mill** — the wooden gristmill and waterwheel by its pond.
8. **Split-rail fences and meadows** — wooden fences, grassy balds and old farm fields along the road.
9. **Rhododendron and laurel** — dense evergreen thickets lining the road edges.
10. **Morning mist** — fog in the valleys below the ridge and soft autumn light.
