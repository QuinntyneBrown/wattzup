# Tremola, Gotthard Pass
The Tremola. A rhythmic, rattling cobbled climb, hairpin after hairpin stacked up the granite valley to the pass.

## 10 critical details
1. **Granite cobbles laid in fans** — hand-set grey setts in arcs, the whole road surface rattles under the wheels.
2. **Stacked hairpins** — dozens of tight switchbacks piled one above another up the Val Tremola.
3. **Dry-stone retaining walls** — massive granite walls holding each bend, with low stone parapets.
4. **Granite bollards and edge stones** — stone posts and kerbs lining the outside of every turn.
5. **Bare alpine valley** — steep rocky slopes, scree and tough grass above the tree line.
6. **Gotthard Pass summit** — the hospice, museum and lakes at the top, the goal of the climb.
7. **Airolo and the Leventina below** — the valley town and motorway viaducts far beneath the hairpins.
8. **Mountain streams and snow patches** — the Ticino torrent, waterfalls and lingering snow on the peaks.
9. **Swiss road signs and Tremola marker** — yellow pass signs, kilometre stones and the historic route plaque.
10. **Cyclists, postbus and classic cars** — riders grinding up, a yellow PostAuto and vintage cars on the cobbles.
