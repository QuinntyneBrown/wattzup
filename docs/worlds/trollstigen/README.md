# Trollstigen
The Troll’s Ladder. A grinding hairpin climb up a sheer valley wall, waterfalls roaring beside every bend.

## 10 critical details
1. **Eleven hairpins** — tight stacked switchbacks cut into the slope; the next bend always visible above.
2. **Stigfossen waterfall** — 320 m falls crashing down beside the road, crossed by a stone bridge.
3. **Sheer rock walls** — near-vertical grey granite faces boxing in the valley on both sides.
4. **Narrow road with stone parapets** — single lane-plus asphalt edged by grey stone wall blocks.
5. **Named peaks** — Kongen, Dronninga and Bispen silhouettes crown the skyline at the top.
6. **Isterdalen valley** — green U-shaped valley floor falling away behind the rider.
7. **Viewing platforms** — rusted-steel cantilevered viewpoints at the summit by the visitor centre.
8. **Snow patches** — lingering snow on high slopes even in summer.
9. **Tourist traffic** — camper vans, cars and motorbikes queuing on the bends.
10. **Moody Norwegian light** — low cloud and mist drifting over peaks, wet rock gleaming.
