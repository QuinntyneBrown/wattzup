# Independence Pass
Highway 82. A long, high-altitude grind over the Continental Divide, climbing from golden aspens to bare tundra at 12,095 feet.

## 10 critical details
1. **Highway 82 switchbacks** — narrow two-lane asphalt with yellow lines looping up the mountainside, guard rails on the drops.
2. **Continental Divide sign** — the brown "Independence Pass, Elevation 12,095 feet" sign at the summit, the finish.
3. **Aspen groves** — white-trunked aspens turning gold in autumn along the lower road.
4. **Alpine tundra** — treeless, rust-and-green tundra slopes and scree near the summit.
5. **Sawatch peaks** — big rounded 13,000–14,000 ft summits streaked with snow all around.
6. **Snow banks** — melting drifts and snow walls beside the road early in the season.
7. **Spruce and fir forest** — dark Engelmann spruce lining the climb below treeline.
8. **Roaring Fork River** — the clear, boulder-strewn river and its falls beside the Aspen side of the road.
9. **Independence ghost town** — weathered log mining cabins in the high meadow below the pass.
10. **Twin Lakes** — the deep blue lakes and valley at the eastern foot of the climb.
