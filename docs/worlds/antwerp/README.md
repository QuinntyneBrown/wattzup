# Antwerp Port Sprint
The quay road. A flat, windswept echelon battle along the docks that ends in a full-gas bunch sprint.

## 10 critical details
1. **Het Steen and the Scheldt quays** — the riverside castle and long concrete quay along the wide river
2. **MAS museum** — the red sandstone and glass stacked tower on the Eilandje docks
3. **Port Authority House** — Zaha Hadid's glass "diamond" perched on the old fire station
4. **Container cranes** — huge gantry cranes and stacked coloured containers on the horizon
5. **Flat, open quay roads** — wide concrete and asphalt, rail tracks set in the road, kerbless edges
6. **Crosswinds** — exposed docks with flags snapping and riders fanned out in echelons
7. **Bridges and locks** — lifting and swing bridges over dock basins, steel railings and barriers
8. **Warehouses and hangars** — brick 19th-century warehouses and the restored Red Star Line sheds
9. **Belgian cycling crowds** — fans along barriers, team buses, flags of Flanders' lion
10. **Grey North Sea sky** — low clouds, flat light, puddles; the cathedral spire on the skyline
