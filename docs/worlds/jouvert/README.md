# Trinidad J'Ouvert
Frederick Street. A pre-dawn street brawl of a race, punching through soca crowds splashed with paint and mud.

## 10 critical details
1. **Frederick Street** — narrow downtown street with shop fronts, balconies and signs, hills behind.
2. **Pre-dawn darkness** — streetlights and truck lights only, sky slowly turning blue toward sunrise.
3. **Paint and mud revellers** — bodies coated in mud, chocolate and bright paint, powder in the air.
4. **Blue devils and jab jabs** — traditional masqueraders in blue paint, horns and chains.
5. **Music trucks** — huge speaker trucks rolling slowly through the crowd, soca booming.
6. **Steelpan bands** — pan sides on wheeled racks, players in matching colours.
7. **Dense crowds** — revellers filling the road; each sprint breaks a gap through them.
8. **Woodford Square and Red House** — the red Parliament building and square as landmarks.
9. **Tropical trees** — royal palms, flamboyants and almond trees in squares and medians.
10. **Litter and colour** — flags, rags, splattered walls and trampled cups on the tarmac.
