# Strasbourg Christmas Market
The Grande Île. A twinkling winter evening crit through half-timbered lanes, surprise sprints bursting between the market squares.

## 10 critical details
1. **Strasbourg Cathedral** — the pink sandstone Gothic spire towering over Place de la Cathédrale, lit at night.
2. **Place Kléber Christmas tree** — the giant decorated tree, centrepiece of the Christkindelsmärik.
3. **Wooden market chalets** — rows of stalls with gingerbread, bredele, vin chaud and ornaments.
4. **Half-timbered houses** — leaning colombage façades of La Petite France and Place du Marché-aux-Cochons-de-Lait.
5. **Overhead light garlands** — strings of lights and illuminated motifs spanning every street.
6. **Façade decorations** — giant teddy bears, gift boxes and greenery on shop fronts like Rue Merciere.
7. **Cobbled lanes** — narrow sandstone-cobbled streets and squares, glistening with winter damp.
8. **Winter crowds** — shoppers in coats, hats and scarves clustering at stalls with steaming cups.
9. **River Ill and canals** — the river and Ponts Couverts towers reflecting the lights.
10. **Early winter dark** — deep blue twilight and warm gold lamplight; perhaps a dusting of snow.
