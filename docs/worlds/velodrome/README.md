# Lee Valley Velodrome
The boards. A fast, loud indoor track session, spinning high cadence round the pine banking under the arena lights.

## 10 critical details
1. **Siberian pine boards** — the pale timber track surface, plank seams running with the direction of travel.
2. **Steep 42° banking** — the bends sweep sharply up the wall, the rider tilted against the curve.
3. **Track lines** — the black measurement line, red sprinters' line, blue stayers' line and the pale-blue Côte d'Azur band.
4. **Curved timber roof** — the Pringle-shaped cedar-clad bowl and its lattice roof arcing overhead.
5. **Arena lighting** — bright, even floodlight from roof rigs, no shadows on the boards.
6. **Packed stands** — tiers of fans close above the safety fence, roaring on each lap.
7. **Infield** — team pens, rollers, bikes on stands and coaches waiting in the centre.
8. **Safety fence** — glass and rail barrier topping the banking, with sponsor boards along it.
9. **Riders in skinsuits** — team pursuit lines and sprinters in aero helmets on disc-wheeled track bikes.
10. **Lap board and screens** — lap counter, bell and big scoreboards above the straights.
