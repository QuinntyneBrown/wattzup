# Mount Washington Auto Road
The Auto Road. A relentless, grinding climb out of the forest and above the treeline into wind and cloud.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Auto Road** — narrow 7.6 mile road averaging 12%, winding up without guardrails on the drops.
2. **Gravel and pavement mix** — mostly paved with patched sections, rough edges and loose shoulders.
3. **Hardwood-to-spruce forest** — maple and birch below giving way to stunted spruce and fir krummholz.
4. **Treeline break** — the sudden emergence onto bare alpine tundra, rock fields and lichen.
5. **Clouds and fog** — cloud racing over the summit, fog banks swallowing the road, wind-blasted rime.
6. **Presidential Range views** — Mount Adams, Madison and the Great Gulf wilderness falling away below.
7. **Summit buildings** — Sherman Adams visitor centre, weather observatory tower and the Tip-Top House.
8. **Mile markers and signs** — Auto Road mile posts and "This car climbed Mt Washington" heritage feel.
9. **Cog Railway** — the steam cog train puffing up its trestle across the mountainside.
10. **Hill climb crowds** — spectators with cowbells lining the final steep 22% wall to the summit.
