# Atacama Desert
The salar road. A vast, empty high-desert time trial across salt flats past volcanoes and flamingo lagoons.

## 10 critical details
1. **Salar de Atacama** — white salt crust stretching flat to the horizon.
2. **Licancabur volcano** — the perfect cone towering over the desert.
3. **Flamingo lagoons** — Chaxa and Miscanti lagoons with pink flamingos.
4. **Straight desert road** — arrow-straight tarmac or gravel vanishing into heat shimmer.
5. **Valle de la Luna** — wind-eroded ridges and dunes near San Pedro.
6. **Altiplano colours** — red, ochre and violet mountains, yellow tufts of grass.
7. **Andean peaks** — snow-dusted volcanoes along the eastern skyline.
8. **Bare emptiness** — no trees, no buildings; scale must feel enormous.
9. **Deep blue sky** — dry, cloudless, intense high-altitude light.
10. **Vicuñas** — wild vicuñas grazing on the high plain.
