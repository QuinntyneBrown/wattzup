# Outback Highway
The Lasseter Highway. A long, hypnotic time-trial across red desert, buffeted by passing road trains on the way to Uluru.

## 10 critical details
1. **Uluru** — the great red monolith rising from the flat horizon, glowing orange at sunset
2. **Kata Tjuta** — the domed rock cluster on the skyline beyond Uluru
3. **Red earth verges** — bright rust-red sand right up to the tarmac edge, wheel ruts and tyre tracks
4. **Dead-straight bitumen** — narrow sealed two-lane road, white edge lines, shimmering heat haze
5. **Road trains** — triple-trailer trucks thundering past, dust plumes and a blast of wind
6. **Spinifex and desert oaks** — golden spinifex clumps, desert oaks, mulga and saltbush scrub
7. **Roadhouses** — Curtin Springs, Erldunda: fuel bowsers, corrugated sheds, "next fuel" signs
8. **Wildlife warnings** — kangaroo, camel and cattle signs, roadkill and wedge-tailed eagles
9. **Huge sky** — cloudless deep blue, harsh midday glare, then purple and pink dusk
10. **Mount Conner** — the flat-topped mesa often mistaken for Uluru beside the highway
