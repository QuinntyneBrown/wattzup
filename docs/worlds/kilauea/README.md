# Kīlauea Lava Road
Crater Rim Drive. Efforts across black lava fields toward the steaming crater rim, the ground glowing at dusk.

## 10 critical details
1. **Halemaʻumaʻu crater** — the huge collapse crater with steaming walls and glowing lava lake.
2. **Black lava fields** — ropy pāhoehoe and jagged ʻaʻā flows either side of the road.
3. **Steam vents** — white plumes rising from cracks beside the road.
4. **Crater Rim Drive** — dark tarmac with yellow lines, cracked and cut by lava flows.
5. **Lava glow** — orange-red glow on clouds and steam after sunset.
6. **ʻŌhiʻa trees** — red lehua blossoms and tree ferns at the rainforest edge.
7. **Sulphur banks** — yellow sulphur crystals and acid-stained ground.
8. **Thurston lava tube** — dense green fern rainforest around the tube entrance.
9. **Mauna Loa backdrop** — the vast gentle shield volcano on the horizon.
10. **Volcanic haze** — vog softening the light; sky shifts from blue to dusk.
