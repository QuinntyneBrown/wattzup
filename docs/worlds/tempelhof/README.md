# Tempelhof Field
The runway. A wide-open, wind-exposed blast down an old airport runway, kites overhead and Berliners all around.

## 10 critical details
1. **Concrete runways** — vast, slightly cracked runway concrete with faded white markings and centreline lights.
2. **Taxiways and the ring road** — the asphalt loop around the field used by skaters, cyclists and runners.
3. **The terminal building** — the huge curved 1930s Tempelhof Airport terminal and hangars along the field's edge.
4. **Kites and kite-surfers** — colourful kites and kite-skaters riding the wind over the open runway.
5. **Endless open sky** — a huge Berlin sky with fast clouds; the field is almost all horizon.
6. **Meadow grass** — long wild grass and fenced skylark meadows between the runways.
7. **Berliners at play** — skaters, cyclists, joggers, dog walkers and picnickers spread across the field.
8. **Community gardens** — the Allmende-Kontor raised beds and wooden shacks in the corner.
9. **Old airport furniture** — radar tower, windsock, signs and runway lights left from the airport.
10. **The Neukölln skyline** — apartment blocks, church spires and the Fernsehturm on the horizon.
