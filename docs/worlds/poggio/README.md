# The Poggio
The coast road to San Remo. Racing should feel like the last frantic kilometres of Milan–San Remo: seaside speed, a short twisting climb, a plunge to the Via Roma sprint.

## 10 critical details
1. **Aurelia coast road** — the SS1 hugging the Ligurian sea, waves and rocky shore on the left.
2. **Ligurian Sea** — deep blue Mediterranean glittering in spring sunshine below the road.
3. **Poggio hairpins** — narrow tarmac switchbacks climbing through terraced hillside above San Remo.
4. **Greenhouses and flower terraces** — glass greenhouses and stepped flower beds lining the climb.
5. **Poggio village and church** — pastel houses and the bell tower at the summit, crowds packed in.
6. **Twisting descent** — tight, fast bends dropping between stone walls back to the coast.
7. **Palm trees** — tall Canary palms along the seafront and villa gardens.
8. **Pastel Riviera towns** — ochre, pink and yellow facades with green shutters stacked on hillsides.
9. **Via Roma finish** — the narrow San Remo shopping street with barriers, banners and roaring fans.
10. **Capo headlands** — Capo Berta, Cervo and Mele tunnels and points jutting into the sea.
