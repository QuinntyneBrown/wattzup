# Marina Bay Night
The bayfront. A fast, glittering night loop where the skyline and Supertrees light every straight like a stage.

## 10 critical details
1. **Marina Bay Sands** — the three towers and SkyPark ship across the bay, always on the skyline.
2. **Supertree Grove** — Gardens by the Bay's glowing steel trees and the OCBC Skyway, colour-shifting at night.
3. **Downtown skyline reflections** — the CBD towers mirrored in the still black water of the bay.
4. **The Helix Bridge** — the curving steel DNA-spiral footbridge lit in blue and white.
5. **Merlion** — the water-spouting lion statue facing the bay at Merlion Park.
6. **ArtScience Museum** — the white lotus-shaped building beside Marina Bay Sands.
7. **Esplanade "durians"** — the spiky domes of the theatres on the bay's north shore.
8. **Smooth promenade surface** — wide paved waterfront walk with railings, lamp posts and palms.
9. **Night crowds and joggers** — strollers, cyclists and runners along the bayfront after dark.
10. **Singapore Flyer and light show** — the big wheel glowing and laser beams over the water.
