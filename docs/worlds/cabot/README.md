# Cabot Trail
The Cabot Trail. A rolling coastal epic over highland climbs with the Atlantic glittering below.

## 10 critical details
1. **Coastal switchbacks** — the road winding along cliffs above the Gulf of St Lawrence.
2. **French Mountain and MacKenzie** — steep climbs with sweeping descents and lookouts.
3. **Ocean views** — blue sea, rocky coves and headlands far below.
4. **Highland plateau** — boreal forest and bog on top of the highlands.
5. **Moose** — moose grazing by the road; yellow moose-crossing signs.
6. **Fishing villages** — Chéticamp and Pleasant Bay with wharves and boats.
7. **Acadian churches** — Saint-Pierre church in Chéticamp with its silver steeple.
8. **Autumn colour** — red and orange maples on the hillsides.
9. **Road edge** — guardrails, gravel shoulders and lookouts.
10. **Cape Breton Highlands** — rolling forested hills and deep river valleys.
