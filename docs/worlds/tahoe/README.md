# Lake Tahoe
The shore road. A rolling, scenic tempo ride round a crystal alpine lake, the road dipping between pines and granite.

## 10 critical details
1. **Shore highway** — two-lane asphalt with double yellow lines (SR 28 / SR 89) curving above the water.
2. **Clear blue water** — sapphire deep water fading to turquoise over the shallows, rocks visible beneath.
3. **Granite boulders** — rounded white granite piled along the shore at Sand Harbor and in the coves.
4. **Jeffrey and sugar pines** — tall pines and firs framing the road and every lake view.
5. **Emerald Bay** — the green bay and Fannette Island seen from the high road above.
6. **Sierra peaks** — snow-capped mountains ringing the far shore.
7. **Sandy beaches** — golden-sand coves with kayaks, piers and swimmers below the road.
8. **Mountain light** — crisp, high-altitude sun with deep blue sky and clear air.
9. **Roadside furniture** — curve warning signs, low guard rails and pine-needle shoulders.
10. **Lakeside lodges** — timber lodges, marinas and boat docks at the small towns.
