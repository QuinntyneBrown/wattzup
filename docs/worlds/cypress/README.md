# Cypress Mountain
Cypress Bowl Road. A steady, switchbacking climb through dark coastal forest, each lookout revealing more of the city and the sea.

## 10 critical details
1. **Cypress Bowl Road switchbacks** — wide two-lane road, smooth asphalt, long sweeping hairpins up the mountain
2. **Highview Lookout** — the roadside viewpoint over Vancouver's towers, Lions Gate Bridge and Burrard Inlet
3. **Coastal rainforest** — towering Douglas fir, western red cedar and hemlock walling the road
4. **Ocean and islands** — Howe Sound, the Strait of Georgia and Vancouver Island on the horizon
5. **City skyline** — downtown Vancouver, Stanley Park and the Lions Gate Bridge far below
6. **Mountain peaks** — Black Mountain, Mount Strachan and the Lions on the North Shore skyline
7. **Concrete barriers and guard rails** — jersey barriers, steel rails and chevron signs on the bends
8. **Road cyclists** — a classic Vancouver training climb, riders spread along the shoulder
9. **Ski area at the top** — lodge, chairlifts and runs of the 2010 Olympic venue at the summit
10. **West Coast weather** — mist in the trees, wet asphalt, then clear evening light over the ocean
