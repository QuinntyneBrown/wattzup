# Rome Colosseum Loop
Via dei Fori Imperiali. Racing should feel like a grand ride through two thousand years of history, rattling over cobbles toward the Colosseum.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Colosseum** — the vast travertine amphitheatre filling the end of the avenue, arches lit gold.
2. **Via dei Fori Imperiali** — the wide straight avenue from Piazza Venezia, lined with ruins on both sides.
3. **Sampietrini cobbles** — black basalt setts in fan patterns, bumpy and shiny underwheel.
4. **Imperial Forum ruins** — broken columns, brick walls and Trajan's Market beside the road.
5. **Umbrella pines** — flat-topped stone pines framing views across the Palatine Hill.
6. **Vittoriano monument** — the white marble "wedding cake" at Piazza Venezia behind the start.
7. **Arch of Constantine** — the triumphal arch beside the Colosseum on the loop.
8. **Crowds of tourists** — walkers, guides with umbrellas and street sellers on the pavements.
9. **Vespas and taxis** — scooters and white taxis weaving through traffic.
10. **Warm Roman light** — golden late-afternoon sun on ochre walls under a clear blue sky.
