# Oktoberfest, Munich
The Theresienwiese. A raucous, crowd-lined festival loop between giant beer tents, brass and fairground lights.

## 10 critical details
1. **Giant beer tents** — the Hacker, Schottenhamel and Paulaner halls with painted façades and towers.
2. **The Wiesn main street** — wide asphalt festival avenues packed with people between tents and stalls.
3. **Bavaria statue and Ruhmeshalle** — the bronze statue and colonnade watching from the hill above.
4. **Dirndls and Lederhosen** — crowds in traditional dress, arms linked, steins in hand.
5. **Brass bands** — oompah musicians in the parade and on tent bandstands.
6. **Fairground rides** — the Ferris wheel, Olympia Looping and carousels towering over the stalls.
7. **Gingerbread hearts and stalls** — Lebkuchen hearts, pretzels and roast-almond booths along the way.
8. **Horse-drawn brewery drays** — decorated beer wagons with draught horses in the opening parade.
9. **Blue-and-white bunting** — Bavarian lozenge flags and garlands strung everywhere.
10. **Golden autumn evening** — warm light turning to a glowing night of tent and ride lights.
