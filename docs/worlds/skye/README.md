# Isle of Skye Fog
The Glen Brittle road. A lonely single-track race through moorland mist, the Cuillin looming then vanishing.

## 10 critical details
1. **Single-track road** — narrow grey asphalt with grass centre-edge and white diamond passing-place signs.
2. **Black Cuillin** — jagged dark gabbro ridge that appears and disappears through cloud.
3. **Red Cuillin** — rounded rust-brown granite hills contrasting the black ridge.
4. **Drifting fog** — low cloud banks rolling across road and slopes, cutting visibility abruptly.
5. **Moorland and heather** — brown-green rough grass, bracken and purple heather on every side.
6. **Sea lochs** — Loch Harport and Loch Brittle grey water glimpsed below the road.
7. **Fairy Pools burns** — clear blue pools and small waterfalls in rocky streams beside the glen.
8. **White crofts and chapels** — whitewashed cottages and small churches scattered at Carbost and Glenbrittle.
9. **Sheep and highland cattle** — animals grazing at and on the unfenced road.
10. **Soft grey light** — overcast, damp and muted, with brief sunbreaks lighting the peaks.
