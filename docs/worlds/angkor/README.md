# Angkor Temples
The temple road. A misty dawn ride on jungle roads between Khmer temples, steady rhythm broken by surges along the moats.

## 10 critical details
1. **Angkor Wat silhouette** — the five lotus-bud towers reflected in the moat and pools at dawn.
2. **The great moat** — the wide still moat and laterite embankment running beside the road for kilometres.
3. **Dawn light and mist** — pink-orange sunrise, low mist over the water and humid haze in the trees.
4. **Jungle canopy** — towering dipterocarp trees and dense green lining the red-dirt edges of the road.
5. **Angkor Thom gates** — the stone face-towers and causeways of demons and gods at the South Gate.
6. **Bayon faces** — the serene smiling stone faces of the Bayon's towers.
7. **Ta Prohm roots** — silk-cotton and strangler-fig roots gripping crumbling temple walls.
8. **Temple road surface** — narrow tarmac with dusty red laterite verges and no kerbs.
9. **Tuk-tuks and bicycles** — tuk-tuks, cyclists and tourists on bikes sharing the road.
10. **Monks and vendors** — saffron-robed monks and roadside stalls with parasols near the temples.
