# Monaco Street Circuit
The harbour straight. A glamorous race-weekend sprint session between the barriers, superyachts on one side and packed grandstands on the other.

## 10 critical details
1. **Port Hercule** — the harbour packed with gleaming white superyachts moored stern-to along the quay.
2. **Red and white kerbs** — painted kerbs at every corner, with white lines on smooth dark race tarmac.
3. **Armco and catch fences** — grey steel barriers and tall debris fencing lining both sides of the road.
4. **Grandstands** — steep blue-seated temporary stands full of fans above the harbour straight.
5. **Casino de Monte-Carlo** — the Belle Époque casino with copper-green towers, palms and fountain gardens, ahead on the hill.
6. **Pastel town on the hill** — cream, ochre and pink apartment blocks stacked up the slope behind the port.
7. **Tunnel** — the curving, lit tunnel under the Fairmont hotel, with sponsor boards and a sudden flash of daylight.
8. **Race-weekend dressing** — sponsor banners, marshal posts, pit garages and safety cars along the circuit.
9. **Riviera sun** — bright Mediterranean light, deep blue sea and sky, sharp shadows.
10. **Rock and palms** — Monaco-Ville's rock, the Prince's Palace and palm trees framing the bay.
