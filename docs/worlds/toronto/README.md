# Toronto Waterfront Trail
Queens Quay. Racing should feel like a breezy lakeside spin, skyline overhead, ferries and boats sliding past on Lake Ontario.

## 10 critical details
1. **CN Tower** — the 553 m tower looming over the route, always in view above the condos.
2. **Lake Ontario** — wide blue water on the lakeward side, sailboats and whitecaps.
3. **Martin Goodman Trail** — the red-paved bike path with granite curbs and maple-leaf trail markers.
4. **Queens Quay streetcar** — red TTC streetcars running in their centre right-of-way beside the trail.
5. **Toronto Island ferries** — white ferries crossing to the islands from Jack Layton Ferry Terminal.
6. **Wavedecks and boardwalk** — curving wooden wavedecks and the long boardwalk along the water.
7. **Condo towers** — glassy residential towers lining the north side of Queens Quay.
8. **Rogers Centre and skyline** — the dome and downtown towers seen across the harbour.
9. **HTO Park umbrellas and Sugar Beach** — yellow and pink umbrellas on urban beaches.
10. **Joggers and cyclists** — busy trail with runners, commuters, strollers and rental bikes.
