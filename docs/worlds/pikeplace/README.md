# Pike Place at Dawn
The market street. A short, punchy city ride over brick and cobbles at sunrise, threading market crowds above Elliott Bay.

## 10 critical details
1. **Public Market Center sign** — the red neon sign and big clock over the market entrance, the course's centrepiece.
2. **Brick street** — Pike Place's red-brick paving with curbs, crosswalks and delivery trucks underfoot.
3. **Elliott Bay at sunrise** — orange dawn over the water, ferries and the Olympic Mountains beyond the market roofs.
4. **Fish market** — Pike Place Fish Co. stalls with ice-packed salmon, crab and handwritten price cards.
5. **Morning crowds** — shoppers, tourists and vendors spilling into the street around the arcade.
6. **Produce and flower stalls** — bright pyramids of fruit, vegetables and bouquets under the arcade lamps.
7. **Market buildings** — the Corner Market and Sanitary Market with green trim, "Meet the Producer" lettering and awnings.
8. **Post Alley** — narrow side lane with hanging signs and brick walls opening off the market.
9. **Coffee steam** — steam from cafes and street vents catching the low golden light.
10. **Downtown skyline** — glass towers, the Space Needle and the Great Wheel glimpsed downhill toward the waterfront.
