# Koppenberg Cobbles
The Koppenberg. Racing should feel like a misty Flemish spring classic: rolling farm lanes, then a brutal grind up wet cobbles between roaring banks.

## 10 critical details
1. **Koppenberg cobbles** — rough, rounded, mossy setts, slick and uneven, narrowing to a single line up the 22% ramp.
2. **Steep narrow chute** — a lane barely two bikes wide, sunken between grass banks and hedges.
3. **Flanders lion flags** — black lion on yellow waved everywhere along the banks and fences.
4. **Packed grass banks** — fans standing shoulder to shoulder on the slopes, leaning over the cobbles.
5. **Spring mist** — soft grey haze, damp light, wet sheen on stones and fields.
6. **Melden church tower** — the Sint-Martinus tower visible from the crest above the woods.
7. **Wooded crest** — bare-to-budding trees closing over the top of the climb.
8. **Flemish farm lanes** — narrow concrete and tarmac lanes between ploughed fields and brick farmhouses.
9. **Poplar rows** — tall lines of poplars along the lanes on the descents.
10. **Crowd barriers and race signs** — metal barriers, km banners and beer tents near the top.
