# Passo Sella
The Passo Sella road. A steady, rhythmic Dolomite climb through larch woods and switchbacks beneath the towering Sassolungo walls to the summit meadows.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sassolungo (Langkofel) walls** — the huge pale dolomite towers looming directly over the upper road.
2. **Sella group massif** — the flat-topped fortress of rock on the other side of the pass.
3. **Smooth Dolomite switchbacks** — well-surfaced tarmac with white edge lines, hairpins numbered on signs.
4. **Green alpine meadows** — rolling summit pastures dotted with wildflowers and grazing cows.
5. **Larch and spruce forests** — the lower slopes lined with conifers before the treeline opens up.
6. **Wooden hay barns and rifugi** — timber huts, mountain hotels and the summit rifugio at the pass.
7. **Pass sign and bilingual signage** — "Passo Sella / Sellajoch 2244 m" and Italian-German road signs.
8. **Stone boulder field** — the Città dei Sassi tumbled rocks near the top.
9. **Cyclists and motorbikes** — riders in the Sellaronda, motorbikes and cars on the bends.
10. **Bright high-altitude light** — clear sky, crisp shadows and pale rock glowing in the sun.
