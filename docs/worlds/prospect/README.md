# Prospect Park
The park drive. A fast, sociable weekend loop race through Brooklyn's leafy park, swapping pulls past the lake, the meadow and the market crowds.

## 10 critical details
1. **The park drive loop** — car-free asphalt with marked bike and runner lanes, gentle rollers and one hill.
2. **Weekend riders, runners and pace lines** — club cyclists, joggers and strollers sharing the lanes all morning.
3. **Long Meadow** — the vast open lawn with picnickers, dogs and ball games beside the drive.
4. **Prospect Park Lake** — the wide lake with ducks, swans, the boathouse and willows at the water's edge.
5. **Grand Army Plaza arch** — the Soldiers' and Sailors' Arch at the park's main entrance.
6. **Boathouse and Audubon Center** — the white terracotta boathouse reflected in the Lullwater.
7. **Smorgasburg and Greenmarket stalls** — tents, food trucks and crowds of the weekend markets.
8. **Mature hardwoods** — tall oaks, elms and London planes arching over the drive, dappled shade.
9. **Park lamp posts, benches and signs** — classic Olmsted-era lamps, green benches and NYC Parks signage.
10. **Brownstone edge of Park Slope** — townhouses and apartment blocks glimpsed beyond the trees.
