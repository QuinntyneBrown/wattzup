# Blackpool Illuminations
The Promenade. A fast, flashing seafront dash under miles of lights, weaving round trams and tram stops beside the sea.

## 10 critical details
1. **Blackpool Tower** — the red-brown lattice tower lit up ahead, the landmark every rider steers toward.
2. **The Illuminations** — six miles of lit arches, figures and festoons strung over and beside the road.
3. **Trams and tram stops** — heritage and Flexity trams on rails along the prom; stops to weave around.
4. **Promenade road surface** — wide tarmac with tram tracks, kerbs and painted lanes, wet-sheened under lights.
5. **North and Central Piers** — Victorian piers reaching into the dark Irish Sea, their lights reflected below.
6. **Sea wall and steps** — the stepped concrete sea defences and railings separating prom from beach.
7. **Night-time sky** — deep blue-to-black autumn evening with the lights as the main illumination.
8. **Seafront crowds** — families in coats strolling the prom and gathering under the brightest displays.
9. **Golden Mile frontage** — arcades, rock shops and fish-and-chip signs glowing along the landward side.
10. **Comedy Carpet and headlands** — the lettered Comedy Carpet at the tower foot and Gynn Square's lit features.
