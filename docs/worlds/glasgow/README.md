# Glasgow World Championships
The George Square circuit. A technical, rain-slick city circuit with constant corners and roaring crowds.

## 10 critical details
1. **George Square** — the square with the City Chambers and columns of statues.
2. **City Chambers** — the grand Victorian city hall facing the finish.
3. **Wet road** — rain-soaked tarmac reflecting lights; spray off wheels.
4. **Sandstone tenements** — red and blond sandstone blocks lining the streets.
5. **Barriers with branding** — rainbow-striped championship barriers and banners.
6. **Crowds under umbrellas** — fans packed along the course in rain jackets.
7. **Tight technical corners** — over forty corners through grid streets.
8. **Painted road lines** — slippery white lines and manholes.
9. **Grey Scottish sky** — overcast, drizzle, soft flat light.
10. **Glasgow landmarks** — Duke of Wellington statue with traffic cone, Merchant City streets.
