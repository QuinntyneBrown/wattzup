# Chicago Loop Crit
Wabash Avenue. A tight, twitchy city criterium: brake late, sprint out of every square corner under the rattling L.

## 10 critical details
1. **Elevated L tracks over Wabash** — steel girders and columns overhead, striped shadows across the road
2. **L trains** — silver CTA cars rumbling overhead; the noise and flicker as riders pass beneath
3. **Square right-angle corners** — the Loop's grid gives tight 90-degree turns at every block
4. **Steel and stone canyon** — tall Chicago School and modern towers walling in the street
5. **Steel support columns at kerbs** — green-painted L pillars lining the lanes, a hazard to dodge
6. **Crit barriers and crowds** — metal barriers, sponsor banners and spectators pressed against them
7. **Road surface** — patched asphalt, manhole covers and painted crosswalks under the tracks
8. **Landmarks** — Willis Tower, the Chicago Theatre sign and Marina City glimpsed down cross streets
9. **Chicago River bridges** — bascule bridges with steel grating where the course crosses the river
10. **Street furniture** — CTA station stairs, traffic signals, taxis, newspaper boxes and steam from grates
