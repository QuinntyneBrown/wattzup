# Kyoto Hanami
The Philosopher's Path. A quiet, petal-strewn canal-side race under arching sakura, slipping between strolling crowds.

## 10 critical details
1. **Stone canal** — narrow stone-walled Lake Biwa canal running beside the path the whole way.
2. **Sakura canopy** — somei-yoshino cherries arching over path and water in full pink-white bloom.
3. **Falling petals** — petals drifting through the air and floating on the canal surface.
4. **Gravel and stone path** — pale fine-gravel path with flagstone edges, narrow and gently undulating.
5. **Small stone bridges** — low granite bridges crossing the canal to homes and temples.
6. **Hanami crowds** — strollers, photographers and kimono-clad visitors sharing the path.
7. **Temples and gates** — Nanzen-ji and Ginkaku-ji gates, tiled roofs and wooden halls nearby.
8. **Higashiyama hills** — forested green hills rising directly behind the path.
9. **Wooden houses and cafes** — low wooden homes, tea shops and stone markers like the "哲学の道" post.
10. **Soft spring light** — gentle diffused sunshine, pastel colours, dappled shade under blossoms.
