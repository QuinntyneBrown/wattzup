# Overseas Highway
Seven Mile Bridge. A dead-flat, wind-exposed time-trial over open water, turquoise sea stretching to the horizon on both sides.

## 10 critical details
1. **Seven Mile Bridge deck** — the long concrete bridge running straight to the horizon, low rails either side.
2. **Turquoise water both sides** — shallow aqua and teal flats shading to deep blue Atlantic and Gulf water.
3. **Old Seven Mile Bridge** — the parallel 1912 railway bridge with its missing span, running alongside.
4. **Pigeon Key** — the tiny island with historic yellow-and-white buildings beside the old bridge.
5. **Bridge hump** — the high central arch for boats, the only climb, giving a sweeping view.
6. **Tropical sky** — big subtropical sky with towering white cumulus and bright sun.
7. **US 1 road furniture** — white edge lines, mile markers and green Overseas Highway signs.
8. **Mangrove keys** — low green mangrove islands and palm-lined keys at each end.
9. **Boats and wildlife** — fishing boats, pelicans and gulls skimming the water below.
10. **Sea breeze** — choppy water and leaning palms showing the constant crosswind.
