# South Beach Sunrise
Ocean Drive. Short, punchy sprints down a glowing pastel strip, chasing the rising sun toward the deco tower at the end.

## 10 critical details
1. **Pastel art deco hotels** — the Colony, Carlyle and Breakwater line the west side in mint, pink and lemon.
2. **Neon fins and signage** — vertical neon hotel names glow against the dawn before switching off.
3. **Sunrise over the Atlantic** — low orange sun to the east, long shadows thrown across the road.
4. **Royal palms of Lummus Park** — tall slender palms in a row between road and sand.
5. **Lifeguard towers** — the colourful, quirky South Beach towers dotted along the sand.
6. **Ocean Drive tarmac** — two-lane road with parked classic cars and a sidewalk café edge.
7. **Sidewalk cafés** — umbrellas, chairs and menus spilling onto the pavement, quiet at dawn.
8. **Beachwalk and dunes** — the paved path and sea-oat dunes between park and beach.
9. **Early-morning people** — joggers, rollerbladers, dog walkers and cleaners, sparse but alive.
10. **The deco tower at the end** — a stepped, finned tower closes the drive as the finish goal.
