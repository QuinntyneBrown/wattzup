# Han River Parks, Seoul
The riverside path. A smooth, fast night ride along Seoul's riverbank, sprinting beneath glowing bridges and the city lights.

## 10 critical details
1. **Banpo Bridge fountain** — the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain's coloured arcs of water pouring off the bridge deck.
2. **Lit bridges** — long concrete and steel bridges lined with blue, gold and violet light, mirrored in the river.
3. **Riverside bike path** — smooth red or grey asphalt lanes with yellow centre lines and lane markings.
4. **Wide dark river** — broad still water carrying long streaks of reflected light.
5. **Yeouido skyline** — the 63 Building, Parc1 and IFC towers glowing across the water.
6. **N Seoul Tower** — the lit tower on Namsan mountain above the city.
7. **Bridge underpasses** — the path ducking under massive concrete piers and deck lighting.
8. **Riverside parks** — lawns, trees, stepped banks and benches with picnickers and walkers.
9. **Rental bikes and riders** — green Ttareungyi bikes and evening cyclists sharing the path.
10. **Path lighting** — tall lamp posts and low glowing bollards edging the riverside promenade.
