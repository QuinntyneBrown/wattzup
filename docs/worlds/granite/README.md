# Col du Granit
North Face Ascent. A cold, quiet grind up a granite north face at first light, each steep ramp a fight to the next hairpin.

## 10 critical details
1. **Granite north face** — a sheer grey rock wall towers over the road, shadowed until the sun clips its rim.
2. **First light on the peaks** — summits glow pink and gold while the valley below still sits in blue shadow.
3. **Stacked hairpins** — tight switchbacks with stone retaining walls show the road above and below the rider.
4. **Steep ramps** — visibly tilted sections where the road kicks up; these carry the threshold blocks.
5. **Narrow worn tarmac** — single-lane grey asphalt, patched and cracked, with painted rider names on the surface.
6. **Kilometre markers** — the classic col bornes showing altitude and gradient for the next kilometre.
7. **Low stone parapets and posts** — granite blocks and white-red posts line the drop side of the road.
8. **Larch and pine giving way** — conifers on the lower slopes thin to scree, grass and boulders above the treeline.
9. **Snow patches and meltwater** — late snow in gullies and small streams spilling across rocky slopes.
10. **The col summit** — a sign, a cairn and a small refuge at the saddle mark the finish.
