# Playground Park Loop
The park loop. Quick, friendly laps on a park path, dodging strollers between the playgrounds.

## 10 critical details
1. **Playgrounds** — colourful climbing frames, slides and swings beside the loop.
2. **Splash pads** — water jets with kids running through them.
3. **Paved park path** — smooth asphalt loop with painted lines.
4. **Strollers and families** — parents pushing prams, kids on scooters.
5. **Mature shade trees** — big leafy trees over lawns and benches.
6. **Benches and lamp posts** — park furniture along the path.
7. **Lawns and picnics** — grass with blankets and people relaxing.
8. **Pond or fountain** — a small pond or fountain at the park centre.
9. **City edge** — houses or towers visible beyond the park trees.
10. **Sunny afternoon** — warm, cheerful light and clear sky.
