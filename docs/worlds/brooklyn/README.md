# Brooklyn Bridge Commute
The Brooklyn Bridge. A morning-rush push up and over the bridge, the Manhattan skyline growing ahead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Gothic stone towers** — the bridge's granite towers with twin pointed arches.
2. **Suspension cables** — the web of diagonal stays and vertical suspenders overhead.
3. **Raised promenade** — the elevated timber-plank walkway above the traffic lanes.
4. **Lower Manhattan skyline** — One World Trade Center and the Financial District ahead.
5. **East River** — water, ferries and the Manhattan Bridge alongside.
6. **Commuters** — walkers, runners and cyclists crowding the promenade.
7. **Traffic below** — cars and yellow cabs on the decks under the walkway.
8. **DUMBO** — brick warehouses and cobbled streets at the Brooklyn end.
9. **Lamp posts and railings** — vintage lamps and iron railings along the walkway.
10. **Morning light** — low sun behind the rider lighting Manhattan gold.
