# Torres del Paine
The Pehoé gravel. A raw, windblown gravel grind beneath granite towers and turquoise lakes.

## 10 critical details
1. **Cuernos del Paine** — the horned granite peaks with dark caps, dominating Lake Pehoé.
2. **Torres del Paine** — the three granite towers rising above the valley.
3. **Lake Pehoé** — vivid turquoise water whipped into whitecaps by wind.
4. **Gravel road** — pale, loose gravel with washboard, dust and ruts.
5. **Patagonian steppe** — golden tussock grass and low shrubs either side.
6. **Wind** — bent grass, wind-whipped water and lenticular clouds; the world must feel windy.
7. **Guanacos** — herds grazing near the road.
8. **Salto Grande** — the waterfall between Lake Nordenskjöld and Pehoé.
9. **Glaciers and snow** — glaciers hanging on the massif, snowfields on the peaks.
10. **Big Patagonian sky** — dramatic clouds, clear cold light and long shadows.
