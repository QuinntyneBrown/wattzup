# Songkran, Bangkok
Khao San Road. A soaked, chaotic street sprint through the world's biggest water fight.

## 10 critical details
1. **Khao San Road** — the narrow backpacker street with neon signs, hostels and bars on both sides.
2. **Water guns and buckets** — revellers blasting riders with super-soakers, hoses and buckets.
3. **Soaked wet street** — glistening, puddled tarmac and spray in the air everywhere.
4. **Packed crowds** — dense, cheering crowds in Hawaiian shirts dancing in the street.
5. **Talcum-powder faces** — white chalk paste smeared on cheeks, a Songkran tradition.
6. **Neon and hanging signs** — dense overhead signage and lights crossing the street.
7. **Street-food stalls** — pad thai carts, fruit and drink vendors along the edges.
8. **Tuk-tuks** — colourful three-wheel taxis parked and nosing through the crowd.
9. **Wat Chana Songkhram** — the temple at the end of the road with gold-trimmed roofs.
10. **Tropical heat and sun** — bright hot daylight turning into a neon-lit evening party.
