# Grand Canyon South Rim
Desert View Drive. A fast rolling rim road where the canyon drops away dizzyingly at every overlook bend.

## 10 critical details
1. **Desert View Drive** — two-lane asphalt with yellow centre lines through ponderosa forest along the rim.
2. **Canyon drop-offs** — the vast layered red, orange and cream canyon opening suddenly beside the road.
3. **Desert View Watchtower** — Mary Colter's stone tower at the route's end as the finish landmark.
4. **Ponderosa pine and juniper** — tall pines and twisted pinyon-juniper lining the road and rim.
5. **Overlooks** — Lipan, Moran, Grandview and Navajo Points with stone walls and railings.
6. **Colorado River glimpses** — thin green ribbon of the river far below at eastern viewpoints.
7. **Buttes and temples** — named rock formations rising inside the canyon, lit by low sun.
8. **Kaibab limestone rim** — pale rock edges and cliffs right at the overlooks.
9. **Tourists and shuttles** — visitors at viewpoints, cars and park shuttle buses.
10. **Golden-hour light** — long shadows deepening canyon layers; dramatic clouds over the plateau.
