# Els Àngels, Girona
Els Àngels. A steady, rhythmic pro climb on smooth tarmac through Mediterranean forest to the sanctuary.

## 10 critical details
1. **Santuari dels Àngels** — the stone sanctuary church at the summit, the finish.
2. **Winding GI-531** — smooth, narrow road with steady bends and low stone walls.
3. **Mediterranean forest** — cork oaks, holm oaks and pines lining the climb.
4. **Girona old town** — the cathedral, colourful Onyar river houses and city walls at the start.
5. **Views over the plain** — glimpses back over Girona and the Empordà towards the Pyrenees.
6. **Pro riders training** — club and pro cyclists climbing in small groups.
7. **Painted road markings** — kilometre markers and names on the tarmac.
8. **Stone farmhouses** — Catalan masies in the valley at the foot.
9. **Warm dry light** — clear Catalan sun, ochre earth and dust.
10. **Summit views** — the coast and Costa Brava hills from the top.
