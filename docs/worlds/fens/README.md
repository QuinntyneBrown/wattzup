# Fenland Time Trial
The drove road. A lonely, wind-battered effort on dead-flat roads stretching ruler-straight to the horizon.

## 10 critical details
1. **Dead-straight drove roads** — narrow, rough single-lane lanes running arrow-straight between fields.
2. **Flat horizon** — totally flat land with a huge sky taking up most of the view.
3. **Drainage dykes** — water-filled ditches and drains right beside the road edges.
4. **Black peat soil** — dark ploughed fields and crops of potatoes, sugar beet and wheat.
5. **Crosswinds** — wind-bent grass, leaning trees and dust blowing across the road.
6. **Raised river banks** — embanked rivers like the Great Ouse above the field level.
7. **Ely Cathedral** — the "Ship of the Fens" rising above the flat land in the distance.
8. **Windpumps and wind turbines** — old drainage windpumps and modern turbines on the skyline.
9. **Isolated farmsteads** — lone farms, barns and lines of poplars as windbreaks.
10. **Big grey East Anglian sky** — dramatic clouds, sun breaks and sweeping shadows on the fields.
