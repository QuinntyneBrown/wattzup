# Amsterdam Canals
Herengracht. Brisk, flat tempo along the canal, weaving among commuters and punching over each humpback bridge.

## 10 critical details
1. **Herengracht canal** — dark green water on one side of the road, the whole way.
2. **Gabled canal houses** — narrow brick houses with step, bell and neck gables and big white-framed windows.
3. **Humpback bridges** — brick arch bridges with iron railings and lights under the arches; the short kicks.
4. **Houseboats** — moored along the quay, some with plants and decks.
5. **Elm trees** — tall elms along the quay, dappling the road.
6. **Brick streets** — narrow red-brick clinker road and bike lanes, no kerb on the canal side.
7. **Parked bicycles** — rows of bikes chained to every railing and bridge.
8. **Commuter cyclists** — upright Dutch bikes everywhere, riders in everyday clothes.
9. **Canal boats** — glass-roofed tour boats and small open boats passing under bridges.
10. **Amsterdammertjes** — the red-brown bollards with three crosses edging the pavement.
