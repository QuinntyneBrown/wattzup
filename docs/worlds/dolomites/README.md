# Dolomites
Val Gardena Valley Road. A smooth rolling valley race under pale limestone walls, pace steady, views wide open.

## 10 critical details
1. **Sassolungo (Langkofel)** — the massive grey block must dominate the skyline ahead from Ortisei to Selva.
2. **Sella group** — flat-topped pale cliffs close the valley head; always visible beyond Selva.
3. **Geisler/Odle spires** — jagged serrated ridge on the north side for distant silhouette contrast.
4. **Valley road SS242** — two-lane asphalt with white edge lines, gentle bends and low stone retaining walls.
5. **Villages of Ortisei, Santa Cristina, Selva** — onion-domed church towers and balconied Tyrolean hotels line the road.
6. **Larch and spruce forest** — dark conifers on slopes, light-green larches at meadow edges.
7. **Alpine meadows** — bright green mown pastures with timber fences and hay barns beside the road.
8. **Wooden chalets and hay huts** — dark timber roofs scattered across slopes; flower boxes on balconies.
9. **Cable cars and lifts** — gondola lines crossing the valley sell the resort feel without dominating.
10. **Clear summer light** — crisp blue sky, puffy clouds, peaks glowing pale cream in sun.
