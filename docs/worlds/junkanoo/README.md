# Junkanoo, Nassau
Bay Street. A pre-dawn carnival rush through a thundering parade, weaving past giant costumes to the beat of drums and cowbells.

## 10 critical details
1. **Crepe-paper costumes** — towering, glittering costumes and headpieces of fringed crepe paper in vivid colour
2. **Goatskin drums and cowbells** — massed drummers, cowbell shakers and horn players in step
3. **Bay Street** — Nassau's narrow main street of colonial shopfronts with balconies and arcades
4. **Bleachers and crowds** — packed grandstands and spectators pressed along barriers, flags waving
5. **Night into dawn** — parade from 2 a.m.: floodlights, then pink sunrise over the harbour
6. **Rival groups** — Valley Boys, Saxons, Roots: themed troupes with banners and giant lead pieces
7. **Pastel colonial buildings** — pink Parliament Square buildings, Bahamian shutters and verandahs
8. **Rawson Square and Parliament** — Queen Victoria statue, pink government buildings at the route's heart
9. **Palms and tropical night** — royal palms, warm air, cruise ships at Prince George Wharf nearby
10. **Street litter of confetti** — scraps of crepe paper and confetti carpeting the road after troupes pass
