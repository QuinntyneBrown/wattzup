# Angliru
The Angliru. A brutal, grinding wall where every pedal stroke hurts and the roaring crowd parts only inches ahead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Cueña les Cabres ramp** — the 23.5% concrete wall near the top, the climb's infamous crux
2. **Concrete road surface** — narrow, grooved concrete strips on the steepest ramps, not asphalt
3. **Crowds pressing in** — fans lining both edges, running beside riders, flags waving inches away
4. **Painted road** — riders' names and slogans in white and yellow paint across the surface
5. **Asturian mountains** — steep green slopes, limestone crags and the Sierra del Aramo ridge
6. **Mist and cloud** — low cloud rolling over the summit, damp grey light, wet patches on the road
7. **Pastures and cattle** — open meadows, stone walls, grazing cows and horses beside the road
8. **Beech and chestnut woods** — dark green forest on the lower slopes, shaded ramps
9. **Asturian flags and camper vans** — the blue-and-gold Cross of Victory flag, vans parked on verges
10. **Kilometre markers and summit** — roadside gradient signs and the bare summit finish at 1,570 m
