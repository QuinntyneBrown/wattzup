# Lake Louise
The lakeshore road. An easy, scenic spin beside a turquoise glacial lake, framed by Rocky Mountain peaks and the Victoria Glacier.

## 10 critical details
1. **Turquoise glacial water** — the milky jade-blue lake, its colour from rock flour, always beside the road.
2. **Victoria Glacier and Mount Victoria** — the hanging glacier and snowy peak closing the far end of the lake.
3. **Fairmont Château Lake Louise** — the grand hotel on the shore, the landmark at the start.
4. **Fairview and Mount Temple peaks** — steep grey rock walls rising straight from the shoreline.
5. **Lodgepole pine and spruce forest** — dense dark conifers lining the road and the slopes.
6. **Red canoes on the lake** — rental canoes from the boathouse gliding across the water.
7. **Lakeshore trail and stone walls** — gravel path, low rock walls and benches along the water's edge.
8. **Bow Valley Parkway and Moraine Lake road** — narrow mountain roads with Parks Canada signs and pullouts.
9. **Visitors and hikers** — tourists on the shore, hikers with poles, cyclists on the road.
10. **Crisp clear mountain light** — bright morning sun, sharp reflections on calm water, clean blue sky.
