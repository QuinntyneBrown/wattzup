# San Francisco Hills
Filbert Street. A lung-busting roller-coaster of punchy city walls, cresting each block to a sudden view of the bay.

## 10 critical details
1. **Filbert Street's grade** — a 31.5% concrete wall with steps on the sidewalks, the steepest crest
2. **Lombard Street's crooked bends** — eight brick-paved hairpins lined with hydrangeas and flower beds
3. **Cable cars** — clanging red-and-cream cars on the Powell–Hyde line, slots and rails in the road
4. **Crest views** — each summit drops away to Alcatraz, the bay and the Bay Bridge or Golden Gate
5. **Victorian houses** — painted bay-windowed row houses stepping down the hills
6. **Coit Tower** — the white fluted tower atop Telegraph Hill above the route
7. **Parked cars at angles** — cars curbed nose-in on steep grades, wheels turned to the kerb
8. **Intersection flats** — level crossings at each block with stop signs, crosswalks and manholes
9. **Street trees and wires** — overhead trolley and power wires, street lamps, ficus and palms
10. **Fog and light** — Karl the fog rolling in, then bright sun and sharp shadows on pastel facades
