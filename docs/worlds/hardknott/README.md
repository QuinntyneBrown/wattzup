# Hardknott Pass
The lane over the fells. A brutal, lurching climb up England's steepest road, every 30% hairpin a stand-up fight.

## 10 critical details
1. **Single-track lane** — narrow, rough tarmac barely a car wide, with grass growing down the centre.
2. **30% hairpins** — tight, steep switchbacks that kink up the fellside, the hardest at the top.
3. **Gradient warning sign** — the red-triangle 30% sign and "unsuitable for caravans" boards at the foot.
4. **Bare Cumbrian fells** — rounded, grassy and bracken-brown fells with rock outcrops and screes.
5. **Hardknott Roman Fort** — the low stone walls of Mediobogdum on a spur above Eskdale.
6. **Dry-stone walls** — grey Lakeland stone walls running up the fellside and beside the lane.
7. **Herdwick sheep** — grey-white sheep grazing on the verges and wandering onto the road.
8. **Eskdale and Duddon valleys** — green valley floors far below with farms and the River Esk.
9. **Scafell range on the horizon** — England's highest peaks rising to the north-east from the top.
10. **Changeable Lakeland weather** — low cloud, drifting mist and bright breaks racing across the fells.
