# Prague Old Town
The Royal Way. A cobbled run through medieval lanes, over Charles Bridge and up the ramp to the castle.

## 10 critical details
1. **Charles Bridge** — the stone bridge lined with baroque statues and Gothic towers.
2. **Prague Castle** — St Vitus Cathedral's spires on the hill above the river.
3. **Old Town Square** — Týn Church's twin spires and the Astronomical Clock.
4. **Cobbles** — granite setts and mosaic pavement throughout.
5. **Powder Tower** — the Gothic gate where the Royal Way begins.
6. **Vltava river** — wide river with swans, boats and weirs.
7. **Nerudova Street** — the steep cobbled climb toward the castle.
8. **Red roofs** — terracotta rooftops and baroque domes seen from the climb.
9. **Tourist crowds** — dense crowds in the lanes and on the bridge.
10. **Baroque facades** — pastel facades with house signs and arcades.
