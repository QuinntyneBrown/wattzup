# Napa Vineyards
The Silverado Trail. A warm, rolling vineyard race on smooth valley tarmac, surging up short rises toward a mission-style bell tower.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Silverado Trail** — a smooth two-lane valley road with wide shoulders, yellow centre line and gentle rollers.
2. **Endless vine rows** — trellised grapevines in tidy rows running to the road edge on both sides.
3. **Golden summer hills** — dry, tawny grass hills of the Vaca and Mayacamas ranges framing the valley.
4. **Valley and live oaks** — big spreading oaks shading the road and the winery driveways.
5. **Stone wineries** — 19th-century fieldstone winery buildings with tasting terraces and umbrellas.
6. **Mission-style winery with bell tower** — white stucco, red tile roofs, an arched bell tower on a knoll ahead.
7. **Winery entrance signs and gates** — carved wooden signs, stone pillars and iron gates at each driveway.
8. **Warm Californian afternoon light** — low golden sun, long shadows across the rows, hazy blue sky.
9. **Roadside wildflowers and mustard** — yellow mustard and poppies between rows and along the verges.
10. **Cyclists and convertibles** — other riders on the shoulder, wine-country traffic and parked cars at tastings.
