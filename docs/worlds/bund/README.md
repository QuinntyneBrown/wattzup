# The Bund, Shanghai
The Bund promenade. Fast flat riverside laps between colonial facades and Pudong's glittering towers.

## 10 critical details
1. **Pudong skyline** — Oriental Pearl Tower, Shanghai Tower, World Financial Center and Jin Mao across the river.
2. **Huangpu River** — wide river with barges and illuminated cruise boats.
3. **Bund facades** — stone neoclassical and Art Deco buildings, Customs House clock tower.
4. **Peace Hotel** — the green pyramid roof of the Fairmont Peace Hotel.
5. **Riverside promenade** — wide raised paved promenade with railings.
6. **Zhongshan Road** — broad boulevard with traffic beside the promenade.
7. **Night lights** — buildings floodlit gold, towers lit with LEDs.
8. **Crowds** — tourists and locals packing the promenade.
9. **Waibaidu Bridge** — the steel truss bridge at the north end.
10. **Huangpu Park and monument** — trees and the People's Heroes monument at the north end.
