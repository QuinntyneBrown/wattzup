# Col d'Aubisque
The Cirque du Litor. A thrilling balcony ride along a cliff-cut Pyrenean road, cows and horses wandering into the race.

## 10 critical details
1. **Cirque du Litor corniche** — narrow road blasted into a sheer cliff face with a drop on one side.
2. **Stone parapet blocks** — low white-and-grey stone blocks lining the open cliff edge.
3. **Short rock tunnels** — rough-hewn tunnels piercing the cliff on the balcony section.
4. **Giant bike sculptures** — huge yellow, green and polka-dot bikes at the Aubisque summit.
5. **Free-roaming livestock** — horses, cows and sheep standing on the tarmac and verges.
6. **Summit col sign and café** — "Col d'Aubisque" sign, café and car park crowded with cyclists.
7. **Green Pyrenean pastures** — steep grassy slopes falling to the Ouzom valley.
8. **Limestone peaks** — grey jagged ridges including Pic de Ger with summer snow patches.
9. **Col du Soulor** — the linked pass with its chalet and signposts below.
10. **Shifting mountain weather** — bright sun alternating with cloud rolling up the cirque.
