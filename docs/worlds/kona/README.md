# Kona Ironman
The Queen K. A scorching, wind-blasted time-trial through black lava, alone with the heat until the crowds of Kona close in.

## 10 critical details
1. **Black lava fields** — jagged black and rust-brown aa and smooth pahoehoe lava stretching to the sea
2. **Queen Kaʻahumanu Highway** — wide shoulders, white lines, rolling straight asphalt shimmering in heat
3. **Coral graffiti** — white coral rocks spelling names and messages on the black lava verges
4. **Triathletes on TT bikes** — aero helmets, disc wheels, numbered riders spaced out on the shoulder
5. **Aid stations** — tents, volunteers, water bottles, cones and Ironman banners along the course
6. **Mumuku winds and heat** — shimmering haze, bent grass, riders tucked low against crosswinds
7. **Ocean views** — deep blue Pacific to the west, white surf against the lava coast
8. **Volcanoes inland** — Hualālai and Mauna Kea slopes, often cloud-capped, beyond the lava
9. **Aliʻi Drive finish** — palm-lined seafront street, banners, packed crowds and the finish arch
10. **Fountain grass and palms** — tufts of pale fountain grass and resort palms in the lava
