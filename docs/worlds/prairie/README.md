# Prairie Paceline
Route 9 Wind Farm. A wind-swept paceline on dead-straight farm roads, trading pulls at the front under turning blades.

## 10 critical details
1. **Wind turbines** — rows of white three-blade turbines along the ridge, blades turning; the world's signature skyline.
2. **Golden-hour light** — low sun raking across the fields, long shadows and warm amber haze.
3. **Straight two-lane road** — narrow asphalt running to a vanishing point with painted centre line and gravel shoulders.
4. **Wheat and canola fields** — gold wheat and yellow canola squares stretching to the horizon either side.
5. **Big open sky** — a huge sky filling most of the frame with high cirrus and drifting cumulus.
6. **Grain elevator** — a tall wooden or concrete grain elevator marking the small town beside the railway.
7. **Wind-bent grass** — verge grass and crops rippling in the crosswind so riders feel the wind.
8. **Power lines and fence posts** — wooden poles and barbed-wire fences running beside the road, giving speed.
9. **Farmsteads and round bales** — red barns, steel bins and hay bales dotted across the fields.
10. **Gentle rolling rises** — long low swells in the road revealing more turbines over each crest.
