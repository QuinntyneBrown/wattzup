# Santa Monica Pier
The beach path. A breezy, sun-soaked seaside race along the sand-lined bike path, sprinting under the palms for the pier.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Marvin Braude beach bike path** — a narrow concrete ribbon with a dashed yellow centre line winding across the sand.
2. **Wide golden sand** — huge flat beach either side, drifting sand on the path edges.
3. **Santa Monica Pier and its pilings** — the long wooden pier on timber piles, the sprint target ahead.
4. **Pacific Park Ferris wheel and roller coaster** — the solar-panelled wheel and coaster turning over the pier.
5. **Tall palm lines** — slender Mexican fan palms along Palisades Park and the beach edge.
6. **Painted lifeguard towers** — the colourful raised huts with ramps dotted along the sand.
7. **Surfers and Pacific breakers** — rolling surf, surfers in the line-up and wet sand at the waterline.
8. **Pier entrance sign and Route 66 end** — the blue arched "Santa Monica Yacht Harbor" sign and End of the Trail.
9. **Beach crowds, skaters and cruisers** — rollerbladers, beach cruisers and walkers sharing the path.
10. **Late afternoon golden sun** — low sun over the ocean, warm haze and long shadows on the sand.
