# Chichén Itzá
The sacbé. A hot, steady push down a white Maya causeway through the jungle, bursting out onto the plaza before El Castillo.

## 10 critical details
1. **El Castillo** — the stepped Temple of Kukulcán with its four stairways, rising over the grass plaza as the finish.
2. **White sacbé** — raised limestone causeway, pale and dusty, edged with low rubble kerbs.
3. **Dry tropical forest** — dense green Yucatán scrub and slender trees pressing in on both sides of the path.
4. **Temple of the Warriors** — terraced temple fronted by rows of carved stone columns.
5. **Group of a Thousand Columns** — long grey colonnades lining a wide path beside the plaza.
6. **Sacred Cenote** — sheer limestone sinkhole with green water and hanging vines at the causeway's end.
7. **Great plaza** — open, sun-baked lawn and bare earth around the pyramid, with distant ruins.
8. **Carved stonework** — serpent heads, skull racks and weathered relief panels on low platforms.
9. **Harsh sun and big clouds** — bright overhead light, deep shadows and towering cumulus.
10. **Visitors and vendors** — tourists in hats and craft sellers along the shaded path edges.
