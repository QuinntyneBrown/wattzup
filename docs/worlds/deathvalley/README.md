# Death Valley
Badwater Road. A shimmering, heat-hammered time trial below sea level, the road melting into mirage ahead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Badwater Road** — a lonely two-lane strip of sun-bleached asphalt with yellow centre lines, dead straight then gently curving.
2. **Badwater Basin salt flats** — glaring white salt polygons on the left, the lowest point in North America.
3. **Below-sea-level sign** — the "282 feet below sea level" sign on the cliff and boardwalk at Badwater.
4. **Panamint Range** — the long snow-tipped range across the valley, Telescope Peak above the salt.
5. **Black Mountains** — dark, layered, eroded cliffs rising steeply right beside the road on the right.
6. **Heat haze and mirage** — wavering air and false water on the asphalt and the flats ahead.
7. **Mesquite Flat Sand Dunes** — golden rippled dunes shimmering in the distance at the end.
8. **Alluvial fans and desert scrub** — gravel fans spilling from canyons, sparse creosote and desert holly.
9. **Furnace Creek thermometer** — the giant roadside temperature display for the cool-down.
10. **Blazing midday light** — white-hot sun overhead, almost no shadows, bleached colours and a pale sky.
