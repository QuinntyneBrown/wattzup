# Mont Royal
The Camillien-Houde climb. A punchy urban hill-climb up through Montreal's mountain park, grinding the switchback to the lookout over the city.

## 10 critical details
1. **Voie Camillien-Houde** — the wide, steady climbing road with painted bike lanes and a sweeping hairpin.
2. **Kondiaronk Belvedere** — the stone terrace and chalet at the summit overlooking downtown.
3. **Downtown skyline view** — the towers, the St. Lawrence and bridges spread out below the lookout.
4. **Mont-Royal Cross** — the illuminated steel cross on the summit, visible through the trees.
5. **Hardwood forest** — maples, oaks and birches lining the road, brilliant red and gold in autumn.
6. **Montreal club cyclists** — swarms of local riders doing repeats on the climb.
7. **Beaver Lake (Lac aux Castors)** — the small park lake and pavilion near the summit plateau.
8. **Olmsted Road and park paths** — gravel carriage paths, runners and walkers through the woods.
9. **Rock cuttings and guardrails** — exposed grey stone and metal railings along the climbing road.
10. **Bilingual Québec road signs** — French signage, bike symbols and the "Parc du Mont-Royal" markers.
