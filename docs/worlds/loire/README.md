# Loire Châteaux
The river lanes. A gentle, flowing riverside race along quiet lanes and levees, sprinting from one fairy-tale turreted château to the next.

## 10 critical details
1. **Château de Chambord** — the vast Renaissance château with its forest of turrets and chimneys.
2. **Château de Chenonceau** — the arched gallery spanning the River Cher, reflected in the water.
3. **Amboise and Chaumont on the cliffs** — châteaux high above the river towns along the route.
4. **The wide, sandy Loire** — slow braided river with sandbanks, islands and willows.
5. **Levee lanes and the Loire à Vélo** — narrow tarmac on the river embankment with green route signs.
6. **Vineyards of Vouvray and Chinon** — neat vine rows on gentle slopes beside the lanes.
7. **Tuffeau-stone villages** — creamy limestone houses, slate roofs and troglodyte cave dwellings.
8. **Plane and poplar avenues** — tree-lined roads and riverside poplars casting striped shadows.
9. **Formal gardens and moats** — geometric parterres, hedges and still moats around the châteaux.
10. **Soft summer light** — hazy blue sky, gentle sun and calm reflections on the water.
