# Moab Slickrock
The slickrock road. A hot, punchy ride through glowing red-rock canyons, arches framing the sky.

## 10 critical details
1. **Red sandstone cliffs** — towering Wingate and Navajo sandstone walls in deep red and orange.
2. **Slickrock domes** — smooth, rounded petrified-dune sandstone the road weaves between.
3. **Delicate Arch** — the free-standing arch on its slickrock bowl, the signature landmark.
4. **Balanced Rock and the Windows** — pinnacles and arches of Arches National Park beside the road.
5. **Winding desert road** — smooth tarmac with yellow lines snaking through red rock fins.
6. **Colorado River canyon** — the river corridor along Highway 128, cliffs straight up from the water.
7. **La Sal Mountains** — snow-capped peaks floating behind the red rock on the horizon.
8. **Desert scrub and junipers** — twisted Utah junipers, sagebrush and cryptobiotic soil.
9. **Fierce desert sun** — hard light, deep blue sky and strong red-orange glow at golden hour.
10. **Mountain bikers** — riders on the slickrock and trailheads, Moab's bike culture.
