# Yosemite Valley
Northside Drive. A flat, fast valley-floor ride dwarfed by granite walls, roaring waterfalls urging every surge.

## 10 critical details
1. **El Capitan** — the sheer 900 m granite monolith rising straight off the road, climbers' specks on its face.
2. **Half Dome** — the sliced granite dome at the valley's east end, glowing at sunset.
3. **Yosemite Falls** — the tiered falls plunging beside the drive, spray and roar near the base.
4. **Bridalveil Fall** — the wind-blown ribbon fall across the valley, seen from the road.
5. **Northside Drive** — narrow one-way two-lane road with yellow lines, granite boulders and no kerbs.
6. **Giant conifers** — ponderosa pine, incense cedar and black oak lining and shading the road.
7. **Merced River** — clear green river beside the road with sandy banks and Swinging Bridge.
8. **El Capitan Meadow** — open grass meadows with split-rail fences giving huge wall views.
9. **Dappled mountain light** — sun shafts through trees, bright granite against deep blue sky.
10. **Park life** — park shuttles, tourists with cameras and brown NPS signs at pullouts.
