# Bonneville Salt Flats
The speedway course. A flat-out paceline sprint across blinding white salt, nothing to hide behind but the wheel in front.

## 10 critical details
1. **Endless white salt plain** — dead-flat, cracked hexagonal salt crust stretching to the horizon in every direction.
2. **The black line** — the painted guide line down the centre of the speedway course, the rider's only lane.
3. **Mile marker boards** — numbered boards beside the course marking each mile for the turns.
4. **Silver Island Mountains** — the low brown range to the north-west, floating on the mirage.
5. **Shimmering mirage** — heat haze and false water lifting the horizon and the distant mountains.
6. **Speed Week pits** — motorhomes, trailers, canopies and race trucks clustered at the pit area.
7. **Streamliners and hot rods** — sleek land-speed cars and lakesters parked and being pushed to the line.
8. **Blazing desert sky** — huge deep-blue sky, hard overhead sun, almost no shadow.
9. **Tyre tracks and salt texture** — faint tracks, ridges and puddle-glazed patches in the crust.
10. **Interstate 80 and rest area** — the highway and its viewing area on the plain's southern edge.
