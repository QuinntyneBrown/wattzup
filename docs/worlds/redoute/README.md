# La Redoute
The Redoute. A brutal, all-in punch up an Ardennes wall between roaring Belgian fans and asphalt painted with riders' names.

## 10 critical details
1. **The steep ramp itself** — a short, straight wall of rough asphalt hitting double-digit gradients past Remouchamps.
2. **Painted fan names on the road** — white and coloured names, slogans and flags painted across the tarmac.
3. **Belgian fans and campervans** — packed verges, flags, beer tents and motorhomes parked along the climb.
4. **Village of Remouchamps** — slate-roofed stone houses and the church at the foot of the climb.
5. **Ardennes forest** — dense beech and spruce woods closing in around the upper slopes.
6. **Motorway bridge over the climb** — the E25 viaduct crossing near the top, a signature backdrop.
7. **Hedges, fences and pastures** — green fields, wire fences and grazing cattle along the road.
8. **Race furniture** — barriers, kilometre banners, Liège-Bastogne-Liège signs and team cars.
9. **Amblève valley** — the river and wooded hills below, seen from the top.
10. **Grey spring Belgian light** — soft overcast with damp tarmac and fresh green leaves.
