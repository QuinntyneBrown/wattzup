# Aguascalientes Velodrome
The pursuit line. A pure, flat-out pursuit on fast wooden boards at altitude, every lap a fight against the clock.

## 10 critical details
1. **Timber boards** — pale, polished pine track surface with visible plank seams catching the light.
2. **Steep bankings** — 42-degree banked bends that tilt the camera and riders through each turn.
3. **Track markings** — black measurement line, red sprinter's line, blue stayers' line and the blue côte d'azur.
4. **Pursuit start lines** — red starting marks on opposite straights; opposing rider visible across the infield.
5. **Infield** — flat coloured infield with team pens, bikes on stands and officials.
6. **Roof and lighting** — open-span roof with even, bright arena lighting and no hard shadows.
7. **Stands and spectators** — tiered seating close to the boards, spectators leaning on the barrier.
8. **Lap boards and timing** — lap counter, bell and scoreboard displaying pursuit times.
9. **UCI branding** — sponsor boards and rainbow-stripe banners around the barrier.
10. **Altitude atmosphere** — thin-air sense of speed: crisp light, empty curves, riders in skinsuits and aero helmets.
