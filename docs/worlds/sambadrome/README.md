# Rio Sambadrome
The Sambadrome runway. Racing should feel like charging down a 700-metre stage of light and drums, the samba schools parading around you.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Sambódromo runway** — the long straight concrete avenue between towering grandstands, lit like a stadium.
2. **Niemeyer's Apoteose arch** — the white M-shaped arch at the end of the runway, the finish line.
3. **Packed grandstands** — tiers of fans dancing, waving flags and phones, from street level to the sky.
4. **Giant allegorical floats** — multi-storey carros alegóricos with sculpted figures, lights and dancers on top.
5. **Feathered costumes** — alas of dancers in matching plumes, sequins and huge back-pieces.
6. **Bateria drum line** — hundreds of drummers in uniform, surdos and tamborims in tight ranks.
7. **Mestre-sala and porta-bandeira** — the flag-bearer couple twirling the school's banner.
8. **Stadium floodlights** — banks of white light and coloured spots against the night sky.
9. **Glitter and confetti** — sparkle in the air and on the tarmac under the lights.
10. **Rio backdrop** — glimpses of hills, favela lights and the city beyond the stands.
