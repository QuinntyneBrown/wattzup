# Diwali, Marine Drive
Marine Drive. Racing should feel like a glittering night sprint along Mumbai's seafront, fireworks bursting over the Arabian Sea.

## 10 critical details
1. **Queen's Necklace** — the curving arc of sodium streetlights along Marine Drive, glowing gold at night.
2. **Arabian Sea** — dark water and tetrapod sea wall on the left, reflections of fireworks.
3. **Fireworks** — bursts of colour over the bay and sparklers along the promenade.
4. **Diyas and lanterns** — clay oil lamps and paper akash kandil lanterns on balconies and railings.
5. **Art Deco facades** — the cream and pastel Art Deco apartment blocks lining the drive.
6. **Promenade crowds** — families in festive clothes strolling and sitting on the sea wall.
7. **Malabar Hill and Nariman Point** — skyline towers glittering at both ends of the bay.
8. **Rangoli** — colourful powder and flower patterns at doorways and on the pavement.
9. **Black-and-yellow taxis** — Mumbai's Premier Padmini kaali-peeli cabs on the wide road.
10. **Palm trees and string lights** — palms and fairy lights wrapping trees and buildings.
