# Hoover Dam
The dam road. A fast desert descent through switchbacks to the dam crest, then a hot, exposed grind along Lake Mead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Hoover Dam crest** — the curved concrete arch-gravity wall with the road running along its top.
2. **Art Deco intake towers** — the four concrete intake towers standing in the lake beside the dam.
3. **Mike O'Callaghan–Pat Tillman Bridge** — the soaring concrete arch bypass bridge spanning Black Canyon downstream.
4. **Black Canyon walls** — dark volcanic rock cliffs dropping sheer to the Colorado River.
5. **Desert switchbacks** — the old US 93 road winding down through rock cuts with stone parapets.
6. **Lake Mead** — deep blue lake with the white "bathtub ring" mineral line on rocky shores.
7. **Mojave desert light** — harsh bright sun, heat shimmer and a cloudless deep blue sky.
8. **Art Deco details** — the Winged Figures of the Republic, terrazzo plaza and Arizona/Nevada clock towers.
9. **Power infrastructure** — pylons and high-voltage lines climbing the canyon walls from the powerhouse.
10. **Creosote and rock verges** — sparse desert scrub, red-brown rock and gravel along the road edges.
