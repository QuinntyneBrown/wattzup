# Arenberg Forest
The Trouée d'Arenberg. A brutal, juddering dead-straight cobbled sector under the trees: hold the wheel or lose it.

## 10 critical details
1. **Arenberg pavé** — rough, gapped, cambered cobbles in a 2.4 km straight line, the hardest sector.
2. **Tunnel of trees** — tall oaks and birches of the Raismes-Saint-Amand forest closing over the road.
3. **Straight-line perspective** — the cobbles running dead straight to a vanishing point, no bends.
4. **Mine headframe** — the Wallers-Arenberg colliery winding tower at the sector entrance.
5. **Barriers and crowds** — metal barriers both sides, packed spectators with flags and Flandres banners.
6. **Muddy gutters** — dirt verges, puddles and mud along the edges riders try to ride.
7. **Old railway bridge** — the bridge over the entrance to the sector.
8. **Grey northern light** — flat, overcast spring light, often damp, with dust or spray rising.
9. **Sector signage** — the "Trouée d'Arenberg" sector sign and the Paris–Roubaix five-star marker.
10. **Commissaire motos and team cars** — motorbikes and cars squeezing through behind the bunch.
