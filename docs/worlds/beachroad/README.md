# Beach Road, Melbourne
Beach Road. A fast, flat bunch ride along Port Phillip Bay, rolling turns in the pack past beach boxes and piers.

## 10 critical details
1. **Brighton bathing boxes** — the row of brightly painted timber beach huts on the sand, the route's signature.
2. **Beach Road** — smooth bayside asphalt with painted bike lanes, gentle rises and sweeping bends.
3. **Port Phillip Bay** — calm blue water with sandy beaches right beside the road.
4. **St Kilda Pier** — the long pier with its pavilion kiosk and lamp posts reaching into the bay.
5. **City skyline** — Melbourne's CBD towers seen across the water from the bay curve.
6. **Clifftop paths** — shared paths with lamp posts winding along low sandstone bluffs and coastal scrub.
7. **Ricketts Point and rock shelves** — flat sandstone reef and rock pools at Beaumaris.
8. **Norfolk pines and tea-tree** — tall pines and twisted coastal tea-tree lining the foreshore.
9. **Bunch riders** — packs of club cyclists in kit sharing the road and lanes every morning.
10. **Bay sunsets** — warm orange skies over the water at the pier ends.
