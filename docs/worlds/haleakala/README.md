# Haleakalā
The summit road. A long, steady climb from tropical coast through cloud to a barren volcanic summit.

## 10 critical details
1. **Switchback road** — the summit road zig-zagging up the bare upper slopes.
2. **Above the clouds** — a sea of cloud below the road near the top.
3. **Summit crater** — the vast reddish crater with cinder cones.
4. **Silversword plants** — silvery spiky rosettes found only here.
5. **Volcanic cinder** — red, brown and black cinder slopes with no trees above the treeline.
6. **Ranch land** — green upcountry pastures, eucalyptus and jacaranda lower down.
7. **Coastal start** — Pacific coast, sugar-cane plain and palms at the bottom.
8. **Summit observatories** — white domes of the Haleakalā observatories on the ridge.
9. **Road edge** — narrow tarmac with yellow centre line and reflector posts, no barriers.
10. **Thin, bright light** — intense high-altitude sun and deep blue sky.
