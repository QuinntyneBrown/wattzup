# Okinawa Coast Road
The coast road. A warm, breezy island ride hugging coral shores, racing over long sea bridges above turquoise shallows.

## 10 critical details
1. **Turquoise shallows** — clear lagoon water shading from pale aqua over sand to deep blue over the reef.
2. **Kouri Bridge** — the long, low sea bridge to Kouri Island, riding straight across the lagoon.
3. **Coastal road** — narrow two-lane asphalt with orange centre lines, sea walls and Japanese road signs.
4. **Mushroom rocks** — undercut limestone islets standing in the shallows like giant stone mushrooms.
5. **Cape Manzamo** — the elephant-trunk limestone cliff over deep blue water.
6. **White coral beaches** — fine pale sand coves with parasols and swimmers below the road.
7. **Subtropical greenery** — pandanus, cycads, palms and dense green hills of the Yanbaru forest.
8. **Shisa guardians** — lion-dog statues on red-tiled roofs, gateposts and walls in the villages.
9. **Coral stone walls** — grey coral-rock walls and red-tile houses in the coastal villages.
10. **Big summer sky** — bright sun, tall white cumulus and humid haze over the East China Sea.
