# The Palouse
The wheat-hill road. A rolling, rhythmic ride over endless green-and-gold hills, every crest showing the next wave.

## 10 critical details
1. **Rolling loess hills** — smooth, dune-like hills covered edge to edge in crops, no flat ground.
2. **Green and gold wheat** — spring green and late-summer gold wheat, barley and lentil fields.
3. **Contour patterns** — curving stripes of harvest and ploughing tracing the hills' shapes.
4. **Quiet country roads** — narrow two-lane tarmac and gravel roads dipping and climbing over crests.
5. **Red barns and farmsteads** — weathered red barns, silos and windbreak trees in the folds.
6. **Steptoe Butte view** — the lone butte rising above the hills, the classic overview.
7. **Grain elevators** — tall white elevators and small railroad towns like Colfax.
8. **Old farm machinery** — rusting combines and trucks parked at field edges.
9. **Huge sky** — open blue sky with puffy clouds and long cloud shadows over the fields.
10. **Golden-hour light** — low sun raking across the hills, deep shadows in every fold.
