# Copacabana Promenade
Avenida Atlântica. A fast, flat beachfront sprint session at sunset, kicking between volleyball nets with Sugarloaf ahead.

## 10 critical details
1. **Wave-pattern calçadão** — Burle Marx's black-and-white wavy mosaic pavement along the whole promenade.
2. **Sugarloaf Mountain** — Pão de Açúcar's granite dome on the horizon at the Leme end of the bay.
3. **Crescent beach** — four kilometres of pale sand curving between Leme and the Copacabana Fort.
4. **Avenida Atlântica** — the multi-lane seafront avenue with its red ciclovia beside the mosaic.
5. **Beach volleyball nets** — nets, footvolley games and players on the sand all along the route.
6. **Beach kiosks** — round quiosques with yellow chairs and umbrellas on the promenade.
7. **Palms and almond trees** — coconut palms and shade trees lining the walkway.
8. **Seafront towers** — a continuous wall of apartment blocks, with the white Copacabana Palace.
9. **Sunset light** — warm orange sky behind the hills, the bay turning gold and pink.
10. **Atlantic surf** — rolling white waves breaking on the sand, with Morro Dois Irmãos and green hills beyond.
