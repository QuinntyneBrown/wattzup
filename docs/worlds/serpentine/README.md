# Serpentine, Hyde Park
Serpentine Road. Smooth park tempo along the lake, then thirty-second sprints for the bridge's arches.

## 10 critical details
1. **The Serpentine** — the long lake beside the road, calm and reflective.
2. **Serpentine Bridge** — five stone arches crossing the lake; the sprint target.
3. **Boathouse** — the Serpentine boathouse and rows of blue-and-white pedalos and rowing boats.
4. **White swans** — mute swans, geese and ducks gliding near the shore.
5. **Striped deckchairs** — green-and-white deckchairs on the lakeside lawns.
6. **London plane trees** — huge planes with mottled bark shading the road.
7. **Park road surface** — smooth tarmac with low railings and lamp posts.
8. **Park visitors** — walkers, joggers, families and other cyclists.
9. **Lido and café** — the Serpentine Lido and its café on the south bank.
10. **City skyline glimpses** — Knightsbridge and Park Lane towers above the trees.
