Ride trace

The ride trace draws what you have ridden over what the plan asked for: target blocks per interval, your power as a filled line, and a playhead at now. Beside it, a progress strip and a log of every resistance change sent to the bike.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

  • Trace (.ride-trace): an 1800 × 600 SVG (one unit per second of a 30-minute ride, one per watt up to 600 W) stretched to its box. Blocks and target lines in the kind colour, actual power as area plus line, playhead in --dashboard-playhead.
  • Progress strip (.ride-progress): an 8px strip of segments under the synced video with a head at now. It is the video view's stand-in for the trace; for a plain value bar use Progress bar.
  • Telemetry (.ride-telemetry): the custom ride's main column. Readouts and the interval, then the view (Video or Power vs. plan), the ticks and the metrics.
  • Log (.ride-log): "Sent to bike", the last commands with ride time, source and acknowledgement time. It is the rider's proof the bike is listening.

Use the trace only during a ride, against a plan. Before the ride the plan is a plan chart; after it, the results page charts the whole ride.

Anatomy

  1. Scale"600 W", 10px faint, top left; gridlines every 100 W in --color-raised.
  2. Target blockOne per interval, kind colour at 13% (7% once ridden).
  3. Target line2px in the kind colour at 80% (35% once ridden), at the target watts.
  4. Actual powerArea at 12% and a 2.5px line in --color-fg, drawn up to now.
  5. Playhead2px, --dashboard-playhead: lime in dark, ink in light. Non-scaling stroke.
  6. Ticks.ride-ticks every 5 minutes, 10px faint, aria-hidden.

Sizes

The trace has no fixed size: it fills the view (minimum 10rem tall, --dashboard-view-min for the whole view) and the SVG stretches with preserveAspectRatio="none". Strokes stay crisp because every line carries vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke". The progress strip is 8px tall (--space-2). The log is 12px mono with a 40px time column.

96px and 224px tall: the same data, the same 2px strokes.

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: telemetry stacks above the control aside; the view is the video at 16:9 with the strip; the trace, when picked, is at least 10rem tall. The legend wraps under the tabs. Ticks keep all seven labels (they fit at 10px).
  • 640px: readouts and interval sit side by side; nothing else changes.
  • 1024px: telemetry and the 340px aside sit side by side at full viewport height; the view takes the remaining height (flex: 1), so the trace grows with the window. The log pins to the bottom of the aside.

Theming

Kind colours come from .ride-kind--warmup|work|rest|steady|cooldown, which set --kind on the group; every block, target line, strip segment and legend line reads it. The playhead is --dashboard-playhead, the strip head --dashboard-progress-head; both flip between lime and ink so they stay visible on both grounds. Actual power is --color-fg, so it is light on dark and dark on paper.

/* A partner build with a heart-rate trace in place of power */
.ride-trace--heart .ride-trace__line { stroke: var(--color-heart-fg); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The view switcher is an APG tabs widget: role="tablist", two role="tab" buttons with aria-selected and aria-controls, and a role="tabpanel" each. The trace and strip are role="img" with a sentence label; the table in a disclosure (.ride-data) carries the detail. The log is a plain list.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabInto the tab list (the selected tab), then out to "Show power against the plan as a table".
← / →Move between Video and Power vs. plan and select it (automatic activation; both panels are already rendered).
Enter, SpaceOpens or closes the table.

Focus

Only the selected tab is in the Tab order (roving tabindex). The SVGs are not focusable. Switching tabs never moves focus into the panel.

Labelling

The trace's label is rebuilt every 10 seconds, not every second, so a screen reader that re-reads it gets a fresh but stable sentence: "Power against the plan: 107 watts now against a 107 watt target, 6:52 of 30:00 ridden". The table's caption says the same and adds the average; the current part is aria-current="step". Neither is a live region. The log's ack times are text ("ack 34ms").

Target lines in the kind colour at 80% are supplementary: the table and the interval panel give the target in text, so they are held to 3:1 where they can be and not relied on alone.

Motion

The line extends and the playhead steps once a second with no tween; the interval bar beside it eases over --duration-countdown (kept under reduced motion, as it tracks real time). Nothing else moves.

Touch

The tabs are 28px tall on a 32px track, inside a 16px-gapped bar: they pass 2.5.8 by spacing. They are used before or between efforts, not mid-sprint; the 48px rule applies to resistance.

Content

  • Tab names: "Video", "Power vs. plan". Legend: "Actual", "Work target", "Recover".
  • The sync line: "In sync · pauses with the ride".
  • Log lines: ride time, command ("RES 6"), source ("plan", "you"), "ack 34ms". The head names the link: "BLE · 0bf669f1". Empty: "Nothing sent — no bike connected."
  • Labels write watts and times out: "107 watts", "6:52 of 30:00 ridden".
