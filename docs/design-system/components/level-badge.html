Level badge

The rider’s level, drawn in gold because it was earned, always beside the XP that leads to the next one.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

The level badge is the gold disc with the rider’s level. Gold in Telo Ride means earned, so the badge appears only where a level is shown as an achievement: the app header, the Profile headline and the level-up moment on Results. It always travels with an XP bar (or the XP numbers) so the rider can see how far the next level is.

  • Header: 32px badge plus a 96px XP bar and “1,840 / 2,600 XP”, all inside one link to Profile. The bar hides under 640px.
  • Profile headline (.profile-level): 96px badge, the title “Sam · Level 7”, a large XP bar and the meta line.
  • Level up (.level-up): the large badge in a gold card at the top of “What you earned” on Results.
  • For a level requirement (“Opens at level 10”, “Lv 4” on the start grid) use text, not the badge: the rider has not earned it.

Anatomy

  1. Disc--color-gold circle, 32px (--space-8), full radius.
  2. NumeralBarlow Condensed 700, tabular, --color-on-fill (ink) in both themes.
  3. XP track.xp-bar: 6px raised track, full radius.
  4. XP fill.xp-bar__fill: gold, width from --value (0–100%).
  5. XP label11px muted “1,840 / 2,600 XP”, thousands separators, no spaces around the slash.

The level-up card is new: the design has XP, levels and an unlock card on Results but no frame for crossing a level. It reuses the unlock card’s gold idiom and timing so the two moments feel like one family; when both happen, the unlock card comes first and the level-up card sits under it.

Responsive behaviour

WidthBehaviour
360pxHeader: the badge alone (the XP bar and label hide under 640px); the link keeps its full name. Profile: the 96px badge wraps above the title when the row is narrower than badge + 12rem. Level up: badge left, text right, the bar fills the text column.
640pxHeader shows the 96px XP bar and label beside the badge.
1024pxNo further change. On Results the level-up card sits in the 400px aside under the unlock card.

Theming

The disc is --color-gold and the numeral --color-on-fill (ink) in both themes; the pair passes in both because gold is a light fill everywhere. The XP track is --color-raised (paper-raised in light). The level-up card uses the gold status tokens (--color-gold-subtle, --color-gold-border, --color-gold-fg) through --level-up-bg, --level-up-border and --level-up-title.

On paper the gold fill against its raised track is only 1.5:1; the bar is decorative there because the numbers beside it (and the bar’s aria-label) carry the value. Never show an XP bar without its numbers.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

In the header, one <a> to Profile named “Level 7, 1840 of 2600 XP”; the badge and the bar inside are aria-hidden (APG Link pattern). On Profile the level is in the <h1> and the bar is role="img" with “1,840 of 2,600 XP”. The level-up card is role="status" so it is announced once, politely, after the page title.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the header level link after the nav links and the streak.
EnterOpens Profile.

Focus

The ring follows the pill shape of .app-header__level (--radius-full). The Profile badge and the level-up card are not focusable.

Labelling

Write the numbers in the name, not “XP progress”. Keep the bar’s role="img" label identical to the visible text so speech and sight agree. Do not give the badge itself a name: it would repeat the level.

Motion

Only the level-up card moves: it fades and scales from 0.96 over --duration-unlock (250ms) after 200ms, and the badge settles from 0.6 with --ease-spring. Under reduced motion the durations are zero, so it simply appears.

Touch

The header link is 32px tall at its smallest (badge only) and about 140px wide with the bar.

Content

  • Level: “Level 7” in titles, “Lv 7” only in the dense start grid. Never “Lvl”.
  • XP: thousands separators and no spaces around the slash, “1,840 / 2,600 XP”; remaining XP as “760 XP to level 8”.
  • Level up: “You reached level 2”, then one line on why (“This race took you past 400 XP.”). No exclamation marks; gold already celebrates.
  • Leave out a zero streak: “0 rides · 0 km”, not “0-day streak” (the new-rider Profile mock prints it; see Sources).
