Menu

A menu gathers actions that do not each deserve a button. The ride's More menu folds Library, the workout, Diagnostics and Feedback into one control when the ride header runs out of room; the rider menu opens from the level badge.

Overview

The mocks have no menu yet; this one is designed for the place they run out of room. The custom ride header carries a lockup, Library, the bike status, the workout, Feedback, the clock, Pause, End and Full screen; under 640px it wraps to three rows. A More menu (.icon-btn with aria-haspopup="menu") collects the secondary ones, and the same component serves a rider menu behind the header's level badge.

  • Use a menu for three to eight actions or settings that apply to the current screen.
  • Do not put the primary action in a menu: Pause and End stay on the header.
  • Do not use a menu for site navigation (use the app header) or to pick a form value (use a select).
  • A destructive item opens a confirmation ("Leave this ride…" opens "Leave this ride?"); it never acts directly.

Anatomy

  1. Section heading12px semibold uppercase, --color-faint; labels a role="group" of radio items.
  2. Check slot16px; the dot (radio) or tick (checkbox) shows only when aria-checked="true", in volt.
  3. ItemIcon 16px muted, label 14px medium, optional 12px description; 44px tall on phones, 36px from 640px; --radius-md.
  4. MetaA value or shortcut, 12px --color-faint, right-aligned ("Connected").
  5. SeparatorHairline --color-line, full panel width.
  6. Danger item and panelHeart text, danger-bg hover. Panel: --color-panel, hairline border, --radius-xl, --shadow-4, 4px padding, 14–20rem wide.

Sizes

One size. Items are 44px tall below 640px (--target-comfortable, for touch) and 36px from 640px (--control-height-sm). The panel is 14rem to 20rem wide (--menu-min-width, --menu-max-width) and never wider than the viewport minus 32px.

Responsive behaviour

WidthRule
360 (base)The ride header shows lockup, clock, Pause, End and the More button; Library, the workout link, Feedback and Full screen move into the menu. Items are 44px tall. The panel aligns to the trigger's right edge and is capped at 100vw − 32px.
640 (sm)Library and Feedback return to the header; the menu keeps the rest. Items are 36px.
1024 (lg)Everything fits the header; the More button is removed rather than left with one item.

Near the bottom of the viewport use .menu--up; near the left edge use .menu--start.

Theming

The block declares --menu-bg, --menu-border, --menu-radius, --menu-shadow, --menu-pad, --menu-gap, --menu-min-width, --menu-max-width, --menu-item-height, --menu-item-radius, --menu-item-fg, --menu-item-bg-hover and --menu-item-icon. The danger item and the disabled state only re-point the item tokens. In dark the panel lifts off ink by fill and shadow; in light (white panel on paper) the hairline border does the work.

/* A menu on the ride HUD over the 3D world: a raised panel */
.ride-hud .menu { --menu-bg: var(--color-raised); --menu-item-bg-hover: var(--color-raised-hover); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Menu Button opening an APG Menu. The trigger is a <button> with aria-haspopup="menu", aria-expanded and aria-controls; the list is role="menu" labelled by the trigger; rows are menuitem, menuitemcheckbox or menuitemradio inside <li role="none">; sections are role="group"; separators role="separator".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / Space / ↓ on triggerOpens the menu and focuses the first enabled item.
↑ on triggerOpens the menu and focuses the last item.
↓ / ↑Next / previous item, wrapping; disabled items are skipped by the arrows in this implementation and stay reachable to the screen reader's virtual cursor.
Home / EndFirst / last item.
A letterNext item whose label starts with it (type-ahead).
Enter / Space on itemActivates it. Checkbox items toggle; radio items check and uncheck their siblings. The menu closes and focus returns to the trigger.
EscCloses the menu and returns focus to the trigger.
TabCloses the menu and moves on to the next focusable element.

Focus

Items take focus with tabindex="-1" and .focus() (roving focus, not aria-activedescendant). The focused item shows the raised fill and an inset 2px ring so the panel edge never clips it. Clicking outside closes without moving focus.

Labelling

The trigger names the menu ("More ride actions", "Level 7, 1840 of 2600 XP. Rider menu"). Items are named by their label; descriptions and meta text are part of the name, so keep them short. Icons are aria-hidden. An item that opens a dialog adds aria-haspopup="dialog"; one that opens a confirmation ends in an ellipsis.

Motion

The menu appears without animation; item fills ease over --duration-fast. A product that adds an opening fade uses --duration-base with --ease-enter, which reduced motion zeroes.

Touch

44px rows on phones, 36px from 640px (with pointer precision); the 36px trigger sits in a 56px header row. Nothing in the menu depends on hover.

Content

  • Items are verbs or destinations in sentence case: "Edit workout", "Send feedback", "Profile".
  • An item that asks a follow-up question ends in "…": "Leave this ride…".
  • Checkable items name the setting, not the act: "Full screen", not "Turn on full screen".
  • Put the destructive item last, after a separator.
  • Disabled items say why in the description: "Group rides can't pause".
