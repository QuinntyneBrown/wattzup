Avatar

A rider is a jersey colour: a dot beside the name in every grid and board, a ring around initials where there is room.

Overview

In Telo Ride a rider is a jersey colour. Every board, grid and session marks a rider with a dot in their --jersey-* colour (the lobby's .start-grid__dot, Class Start's .class-dot, Results' .standings__dot), and the same colour is the rider's dot in the 3D world. .avatar extends that idea to places with room for more: initials or a picture inside a jersey ring, an icon for bots and your ghost, a status dot, and a group that overlaps with a +N count.

  • Dot (.avatar-dot, and the area dots): in rows next to the name. Always the default.
  • Ring with initials: where riders appear without a row (a session summary, a group stack).
  • Icon: bots (Pacer Nova … Pacer Echo) and the gold "Your best" ghost.
  • Image: a channel or coach picture when one exists; riders have no photos in Telo Ride.

Your own colour is always --jersey-you (signal orange, the volt) and the ghost is always --jersey-ghost (gold), matching "volt is you, gold is earned".

Anatomy

  1. ContentInitials (two letters, uppercase, 12px semibold), an icon at 55%, or an <img> cropped to the circle.
  2. Jersey ring2px ring in var(--jersey), separated from the fill by a 2px gap in the surface colour (--avatar-surface).
  3. Status dot (optional)30% of the size, bottom right, ringed in the surface colour: ok connected, heart lost, steel idle.

Sizes

LKLKLKLK
Dot 10px / 12px; avatar sm 24px, md 32px, lg 48px, xl 64px.

Dots in rows; sm in dense boards and groups; md (default) in lists; lg in session summaries; xl for a rider's own headline. The ring is 1px on sm, 2px on md and lg, 4px on xl.

States

Avatars are pictures, not controls. When one links somewhere the link carries the states (ring on focus); the avatar does not change on hover. "Disabled" is an open seat; "current" is you.

Light colours (Ollie's #ffd166, Juno's #6be3ff) read faintly on paper; that is acceptable because the ring is decoration and the name is beside it.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: groups show at most four avatars plus +N; rows keep the dot at the start and truncate the name.
  • 640px: the start grid goes to two columns; dots do not change size.
  • 1024px: session summaries may show up to six before +N.
  • Avatars never scale with the viewport; pick a size per placement.

Theming

.avatar {
  --avatar-size: var(--space-8);
  --avatar-ring: var(--border-width-thick);
  --avatar-surface: var(--color-panel);   /* set to the surface it sits on */
  --avatar-bg: var(--color-raised);
}
/* On the page canvas */
.app__scroll .avatar { --avatar-surface: var(--color-ink); }

Jersey colours are content data (shared/game.ts), not theme tokens: they are the same in both themes and in the 3D world. Only the fill, initials and surface gap change with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <span>. Beside a visible name it is aria-hidden="true". Alone it is role="img" with the rider's name as aria-label; an <img> avatar uses alt. A group is role="group" labelled with the count and names. No APG widget applies.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips avatars; only reaches one wrapped in a link.
EnterFollows an avatar link.

Focus

A linked avatar shows the shared ring on the link, which is rounded to a circle.

Labelling

Name, not initials: aria-label="lena.k", never "LK". The +N count reads as part of the group label ("6 riders in this session: …"). Status dots need words elsewhere ("Lost the bike").

Motion

None. Avatars do not animate.

Touch

Not a target unless linked; a linked avatar smaller than 24px (sm) needs the link to include the name.

Content

  • Initials: first letters of the two parts of the display name ("Ollie R." → OR, "kenji.cyc" → KC); one part gives two letters ("jwatts" → JW).
  • Bots use the bot icon and their name ("Pacer Nova"); never initials, so they are never mistaken for people.
  • Your own avatar is labelled "You" in lists, the way every board does.
  • Overflow is "+N", never "N more".
