Toast

A short message that confirms an action or reports a background result, then gets out of the way. Toasts stack at the bottom of the screen and never block the ride.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

The mocks show no toasts: every result in Telo Ride is shown in place (the Copy link button turns its icon volt, Feedback shows "Feedback sent" in its own panel). That is the right default. A toast is for the few results that land somewhere else or after the rider has moved on:

  • "Ride saved" after Leave this ride → Save ride and open library (the rider is now in the library).
  • "Packet log downloaded" after the browser saves the file from Diagnostics.
  • "Couldn’t send feedback" when sending fails after the panel has closed.

Do not use a toast for errors the rider must fix (use an alert or inline message where the problem is), for anything during a ride interval (the HUD owns the screen), or as the only confirmation of something they will look for later.

Anatomy

  1. Icon20px, the tone's icon colour (--toast-accent); a shape per tone so colour is not the only signal.
  2. Title14px semibold fg: what happened.
  3. Text (optional)14px muted, figures in Barlow.
  4. Action (optional)One small button: View ride, Retry, Open.
  5. Dismiss32px .icon-btn. Panel: 384px max, 16px radius, line border (tone border for warning and danger), --shadow-3.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 640px: the region spans the screen less 16px gutters, toasts centred and full width, 16px above the bottom (plus the safe area).
  • From 640px: bottom right, 24px in, toasts 384px max and right-aligned.
  • On ride screens the region moves above the controls bar, and toasts wait for a recovery segment or the end of the ride.

Theming

The tone sets two tokens on the toast: --toast-accent (the icon, from --color-{info,success,warning,danger}-icon) and --toast-border (line, or the tone's border for warning and danger). The fill stays --color-panel in every tone so a stack reads as one family; the tone is carried by the icon shape and colour.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A live region (ARIA22 status messages, WCAG 4.1.3): the region holds a role="status" (polite) container; a danger toast goes into a role="alert" container. The live containers exist before content is added. Toasts are not dialogs and never take focus.

Keyboard

KeyAction
F6Moves focus to the notifications region (and back), so a toast's action can be reached without hunting.
TabInside the region: action, then Dismiss.
EscWith focus in a toast: dismisses it and returns focus to where it was.

Focus

Showing a toast never moves focus. If the rider tabs into it, the timer pauses; dismissing it returns focus to the previous element, or to main.

Labelling

The title is the announcement; keep it complete on its own ("Couldn’t send feedback"). The dismiss button is "Dismiss"; an action names its result ("Retry", "View ride").

Motion

Enters with a fade and an 8px rise over --duration-base, --ease-panel; leaves over --duration-fast. Reduced motion: appears and disappears without movement.

Touch

Dismiss and the action are 32–36px with 8px between; toasts never sit over the ride controls or the bottom sheet's buttons.

Content

  • Title: what happened, past tense, no full stop: "Ride saved", "Link copied", "Packet log downloaded".
  • Failure: "Couldn’t …" plus what is kept and what to do: "Your note is kept. Check your connection and try again."
  • Figures as figures: "6:52 · 2.24 km".
  • No "Success!", no exclamation marks, no emoji.
