Section head

The title of a preview section, with a link to the full list on the right and a hairline beneath.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A section head titles a block on a page and, when the block is a preview of something longer, offers a more link to the full list on the right. A hairline under it separates the head from what follows. Home uses it for its two doors, Race (“All maps”) and Classes (“All classes”).

  • Use it for a preview section whose full version lives on another page.
  • Without a link it is just a titled block; Profile and Results use their own heads with a count instead (.profile-section__head, .xp-earned__head), built on the same rule.
  • Not a page title (use .page__title) and not a tab bar.

Anatomy

  1. Title<h2>, 20px semibold, the section’s accessible name (aria-labelledby).
  2. More link.more-link: 14px muted text + 16px arrow, baseline-aligned with the title.
  3. Hairline1px --color-line under, 12px below the text.

Sizes

One size on Home (20px title). Profile’s section head is the 18px sibling. Do not make a section head larger than the page title (24px on phones, 30px from 640px).

States

Only the more link is interactive. Hover turns it from --color-muted to --color-fg; focus draws the ring with --radius-sm corners.

Responsive behaviour

WidthBehaviour
360pxTitle and link stay on one row (gap: --space-4); the link never wraps (nowrap). A title that would collide with the link is too long.
640pxUnchanged.
1024pxHome places the two doors side by side; each head spans its column.

Theming

Title in --color-fg, link in --color-muted → --color-fg on hover, hairline --color-line. The hairline is decorative (1.3:1), so it is fine below 3:1: the heading carries the structure.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The title is a real heading (<h2> on Home) and names its <section aria-labelledby>. The more link is a native <a> (APG Link pattern); its arrow is aria-hidden.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves to the more link.
EnterOpens the full list.

Focus

Shared ring, 2px offset, --radius-sm.

Labelling

Link text must make sense alone: “All maps”, “All classes”. Never “More” or “See all”: in a screen reader’s link list two “See all” links are indistinguishable.

Motion

The link’s colour change uses --duration-fast (150ms) and is removed under reduced motion.

Touch

The more link is 20px tall from its line height; pad the hit area to 24px with the 4px block gap above the hairline, and keep 16px from the title.

Content

  • Title: one noun, sentence case: “Race”, “Classes”, “Maps”, “Recent rides”.
  • Link: “All” + the plural noun of what the section previews: “All maps”, “All classes”. The arrow is always there.
  • No counts in the title; put them in the summary line below (“3 of 7 maps open · 5 of 21 stars”).
