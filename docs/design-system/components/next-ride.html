Next ride

Overview

The Next ride card is Home’s hero: the single goal that opens the next map, the map to ride for it, what it opens, the rider’s best there and one big button to go. It answers “what should I ride now?” before anything else on Home.

  • Next ride (.next-ride): one per Home, directly under the greeting.
  • Reward (.reward): the locked map the goal opens, a greyed thumb with “Opens Neon Docklands” in gold.
  • Unlock banner (.unlock-banner, a gold banner): on the Home visit after a map opens, above the card, with “Ride it”.
  • Unlock card (.unlock-card): on Results, the moment the goal is met, with “Ride Neon Docklands”.
  • When every map is open the card’s goal becomes a star goal (“Earn 3★ on Dolomites”); it is never empty.

Anatomy

  1. PhotoThe goal’s map, 16:7 (min 192px tall) above, the left 3fr column from 1024px.
  2. Photo tagMap · distance · difficulty.
  3. Eyebrow“Your next unlock”, 14px muted.
  4. Goal<h2>, 24px → 30px semibold: the card’s name.
  5. RewardRaised well: greyed 80 × 48 thumb, gold “Opens …”, the place in one muted line.
  6. Best“Your best here: 27:10”; left out on a first ride.
  7. Call to actionPrimary, 56px, full width: “Race Old Town Criterium →”. Pinned to the bottom of the body from 1024px.

Responsive behaviour

WidthBehaviour
360pxOne column: the photo (16:7, at least 192px tall) above the body. The grid column is minmax(0, 1fr) so the photo cannot force the card wider than the phone (the design overflowed at 390px).
640pxBody padding 28px; goal 30px.
1024pxTwo columns, photo 3fr and body 2fr; the photo fills the height and the CTA drops to the bottom of the body.

Theming

The card is a --color-panel surface with --radius-2xl; the reward is a --color-raised well with --color-gold-fg title. The banner uses the gold status tokens (--color-gold-subtle, --color-gold-border). The unlock card mixes gold at 10% and 50% (border); on paper it is a cream card with deep-gold text.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section aria-labelledby> named by its goal heading. The CTA is a link (APG Link pattern) styled as a button; the banner is role="status" so “Neon Docklands is open.” is announced once on arrival. Photo and reward thumb are decorative.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBanner’s Ride it (when present), then the CTA. The reward is not interactive.
EnterFollows the link to Race Setup.

Focus

The CTA takes the shared ring, offset 2px from its --radius-xl corners; on the panel the ring is ≥ 3:1.

Labelling

The CTA names the map: “Race Old Town Criterium”, never “Go” or “Start”. The arrow is decorative. The reward’s lock icon is decorative; “Opens” carries the meaning.

Motion

The CTA scales to 0.98 when pressed; the unlock card on Results fades and scales from 0.96 over 250ms after 200ms, then its photo fades from grey; reduced motion keeps opacity only.

Touch

The CTA is 56px tall and full width: the biggest target on Home.

Content

  • Greeting above it: “Evening, Sam.”; for a new rider, “Welcome, Sam. Your first race opens a new map.”
  • Goal: an imperative sentence without a full stop: “Finish top 3 vs Bots on Old Town Criterium”.
  • Reward: “Opens Neon Docklands”, then the map’s one-line description.
  • CTA: “Race” + map name. Banner: “Neon Docklands is open.” + description and distance; action “Ride it”. Unlock card action: “Ride Neon Docklands”.
