Form layout

Every Telo Ride form is one column of labelled fields, grouped into short sections, with its actions at the end. When a submit fails, an error summary at the top links to each field; while it sends, the fields lock and the button says what is happening.

Overview

Use .form for any set of fields the rider fills and then confirms: Custom Setup ("Set up your ride", confirmed by "Start ride"), Send feedback (confirmed by "Report bug"). A form is a real <form> so Enter submits and the browser can validate.

  • One column. Fields stack in reading order, capped at 36rem (--form-max-width). Two short related fields may share a .form__row from 768px.
  • Sections (.form__section) group three to five fields under a title, ruled from each other.
  • Actions last (.form__actions): the primary at the end of the row on desktop, on top of the stack on phones; one primary per form.
  • Errors: on submit, an .error-summary at the top takes focus and links to each invalid field, and each field shows its own .field__error.
  • Submitting: fields go into a disabled <fieldset> and the submit button carries aria-busy with its label kept.

Anatomy

  1. Title.form__title (or the page h1), optional .form__lead under it.
  2. Fields.fields, 24px apart (--form-gap; 16px in --compact).
  3. Note.form__note, 12px faint: what happens on submit.
  4. Actions.form__actions; --bar rules it off at the bottom of a panel, --split puts the note at the start.

Sizes

Forms have no sizes: width comes from --form-max-width (36rem, or none with --full) and density from --form-gap (24px, or 16px with --compact). Field sizes are set on the fields.

Responsive behaviour

WidthRule
360 (base)One column at full width with a 16px gutter. Actions stack full width, primary on top (column-reverse). Long forms may use --sticky so the primary stays above the keyboard. --split keeps a note and one button on one row.
640 (sm)Actions become a row aligned to the end, primary last. Steppers and training modes go two-up inside their sections.
768 (md).form__row puts two short related fields side by side.
1024 (lg)The form stays at 36rem; the extra width goes to an aside (Custom Setup's plan preview), never to longer fields.

Theming

.form declares --form-max-width and --form-gap; sections rule off with --color-line. .error-summary declares --error-summary-bg, --error-summary-border and --error-summary-accent from the danger status tokens, which are tuned per theme (a pale tint on paper, a deep tint on ink).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <form> named by its title (aria-labelledby). Sections that group one question use <fieldset> + <legend>. The error summary follows the APG Alert pattern: role="alert", tabindex="-1", and focus moves to it after a failed submit. Errors meet 3.3.1 (identified in text) and 3.3.3 (each says how to fix it).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabThrough the fields in visual order, then the actions.
EnterIn a single-line field: submits. In a textarea: new line.
Enter on a summary linkMoves focus to that field (the link targets the field's id).
EscIn a panel form (Send feedback): closes the panel and keeps the draft.

Focus

After a failed submit, focus goes to the summary; after success, to whatever opened the form (the Feedback button). While sending, focus stays on the busy button. Never move focus on blur.

Labelling

Every field has a visible label (see form field). The submit names the result ("Report bug", "Start ride"), never "Submit". The status line uses role="status" so "Sending your report…" is read without stealing focus.

Motion

No movement of its own. Scroll to the summary uses smooth scroll only without reduced motion.

Touch

Full-width stacked actions on phones; 8px between them. The xl "Start ride" (56px) is the one big target on Custom Setup.

Content

  • Summary titles count and point at the goal: "Fix 2 things to start your ride".
  • Summary links name the problem in a few words; the field error says how to fix it: "That isn't a YouTube link. Paste a link that starts with youtube.com or youtu.be."
  • Notes say what happens on submit: "Context is sent with your note."
  • Status while sending: "Sending your report…"; the button keeps its label.

Code

form.addEventListener('submit', async (e) => {
  e.preventDefault();
  const errors = validate(form);
  if (errors.length) { renderSummary(errors); summary.focus(); return; }
  fieldset.disabled = true; submit.disabled = true; submit.setAttribute('aria-busy', 'true');
  status.textContent = 'Sending your report…';
  await send(form);
});
