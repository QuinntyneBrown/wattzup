Popover

A small panel attached to its button, for a choice or a few facts that belong to that button. The page stays visible around it; Telo Ride uses two, Leave this ride and Send feedback.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

  • Leave this ride? (.ride-leave): under Library in the ride header; three choices, the ride paused while it is open.
  • Send feedback (.feedback-popover): under Feedback in the ride header from 640px; a bottom sheet below.
  • Generic (.popover): the same surface for new uses, such as the bike status in the ride header opening its connection facts instead of a full Diagnostics dialog.

Use a popover when the content is short (one title, a sentence, up to three actions or a small form) and clearly belongs to the button. Use a tooltip for a label with nothing to act on, a menu for a list of commands, a dialog when it needs the whole screen's attention.

Anatomy

  1. TriggerThe button it belongs to; aria-expanded and aria-haspopup="dialog", raised while open.
  2. Title16px semibold; names the panel (aria-labelledby).
  3. Body14px muted with Barlow figures.
  4. ActionsStacked full width, primary first, the way back last and quiet. Panel: 320px, 16px radius, line border, --shadow-4, 8px below the trigger.

States

Library
  • Open: aria-expanded="true" raises the trigger (the same fill as hover) so the popover visibly hangs from it.
  • Disabled: Library is never disabled mid-ride; a trigger that cannot open hides instead.
  • Busy (Send feedback sending) and done (Feedback sent) follow Dialog states; the popover stays open until Done.

Responsive behaviour

  • From 640px: anchored under its trigger, start- or end-aligned so it stays on screen.
  • Under 640px: Leave this ride pins inside the 20px gutters just under the header (it would run off the right edge from Library's position); Send feedback becomes a bottom sheet over a scrim. A new popover picks one of these two; it never floats half off-screen.

Theming

Block tokens on .popover: --popover-width, --popover-bg (--color-panel), --popover-border (--color-line). It sits on --z-popover, above sticky headers and below toasts. In light the border and --shadow-4 do the separating, since the panel and page are both pale.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A disclosure that opens a non-modal role="dialog" (APG Dialog, with the trigger as an APG Disclosure button: aria-expanded, aria-controls, aria-haspopup="dialog"). Leave this ride and Send feedback are modal in the mocks (aria-modal="true", the page inert) because the ride is paused or a form is open; a facts popover is not modal.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / SpaceOn the trigger: opens and moves focus in; again: closes.
TabThrough the popover's controls. Modal: wraps inside. Non-modal: leaving the popover closes it.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger (Leave: the same as Keep riding).

Focus

  • On open: the first action (Save ride and open library has autofocus in the mock), or the form's field (the feedback message).
  • On close by any means (Esc, the quiet action, click-away on the transparent scrim): back to the trigger.
  • The trigger keeps its raised fill while open, so the origin of the panel stays visible to sighted keyboard users.

Labelling

The popover is named by its title. The trigger's own name stays the same open or closed ("Library"); aria-expanded carries the state.

Contrast

Open trigger

Motion

Fades in with a 2% scale over --duration-base, --ease-panel; closes instantly. None with reduced motion.

Touch

Stacked 40px actions; the quiet "Keep riding" is 36px. Tapping outside closes only a popover where nothing would be lost (Leave this ride keeps the ride paused and is closed by Keep riding or the transparent scrim, which resumes nothing on its own).

Content

  • Title states the question or the thing: "Leave this ride?", "Echelon EX-5s".
  • One sentence of facts with figures; then actions named for their result.
  • The way back is the last, quiet action and says what it keeps: "Keep riding".
