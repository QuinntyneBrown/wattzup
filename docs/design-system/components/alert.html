Alert

A banner that sits in the page and says something about the whole screen: a map just opened, Bluetooth cannot work in this browser, the bike stopped answering. It stays until the situation changes or the rider dismisses it.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

  • Banner (.banner + tone): inside the content column. The product's one is the gold unlock banner on Home ("Neon Docklands is open.").
  • Browser notice (.browser-notice): full width under the header, the page still usable, when Bluetooth to the bike cannot work here (not Chrome or Edge, or plain http).
  • Browser panel (.browser-panel): stands in for a whole screen that cannot work at all (the custom ride in Safari).

Use an alert for a condition that affects the screen and lasts. Use a toast for a passing confirmation, an inline message for one field or block, a dialog only when the rider must decide before going on.

Anatomy

  1. Icon20px in the tone's accent; a shape that matches the message (unlock, alert, monitor-x).
  2. Title14px semibold in the accent: the fact.
  3. Detail14px muted on the same line, wrapping under it.
  4. Action (optional)One 36px button in the tone (.btn--gold) or raised.
  5. Dismiss (optional)32px .icon-btn, only when the condition is not still true. Panel: 16px radius, tinted fill, tone border, 12px × 16px padding.

Sizes

One size: 14px text, 12px × 16px padding (banner) or 12px × 20px, 32px from 1024px (browser notice). Width comes from the column it sits in.

States

PartDefaultHoverFocusActiveDoneDisabled
Copy link
Dismiss removed never
  • Done: Copy link swaps to "Copied" with a volt check (data-copied) for two seconds, the in-place confirmation the product uses instead of a toast.
  • Dismissed: the banner is removed and stays gone for that unlock (remember it per map); focus moves to the next heading.
  • Resolved: a warning or danger banner disappears on its own when the condition clears (Bluetooth turned on, bike reconnected), announced politely: "Echelon EX-5s reconnected."
  • Disabled: a banner action is never disabled; if it cannot work, say why in the banner.

Responsive behaviour

  • Banner: wraps. Below about 480px the action drops under the text (flex-wrap, 12px row gap); it never shrinks the text to one word per line.
  • Browser notice: stacked below 640px (no icon, action under the text, left-aligned); one row with the icon from 640px; 32px side padding from 1024px to match the header.

Theming

A tone sets --banner-bg, --banner-border and --banner-accent from the status tokens (--color-{warning,danger,info,success}-{bg,border,fg}, and --color-gold-subtle / --color-gold-border / --color-gold-fg for gold). Title and icon read the accent; detail stays --color-muted. In dark the fills are deep tints, in light pale ones; both keep the accent at 4.5:1 or more.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Alert for conditions that block riding (role="alert": browser notice, Lost the bike, Bluetooth is off); role="status" for news that can wait (the unlock banner, "MarcoVelo left."). An alert present when the page loads is not announced by every screen reader, so put it first after the header, where reading starts.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabTo the action, then Dismiss, in reading order.
Enter / SpaceActivates the action or dismisses.

Focus

A banner never takes focus when it appears. After Dismiss, focus moves to the next heading or the main region, never to the top of the page.

Labelling

The title is a full sentence, so it reads alone. Dismiss names what it dismisses ("Dismiss: Neon Docklands is open"). Copy link announces "Link copied" in a polite region when pressed.

Motion

None. Banners appear in place; a dismissed one is removed without animation so the page below does not slide.

Touch

Actions are 36px; Dismiss is 32px with 8px from the action. The browser notice's action is full width under the text on phones.

Content

  • Title: the fact, as a sentence with a full stop: "Neon Docklands is open.", "Lost the bike."
  • Detail: why it matters or what to do, with figures: "A harbour city after dark, 21.6 km."
  • Blocking notices name the fix, not the error: "Open Telo Ride in Chrome or Edge.", "Open the secure version of this page."
  • The action is the fix: "Copy link", "Open over HTTPS", "Reconnect", "Ride it".
