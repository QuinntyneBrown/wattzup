Route profile

The route profile tells a racer where they are on the map: the grade under their wheels, how much they have climbed, how far they have ridden, and the whole road ahead drawn as an elevation profile with the part behind them filled in.

Overview

On a world ride the 3D scene shows the next hundred metres; the route profile shows the rest. It has two parts that always appear together on the Race screen:

  • Route panel (.race-route): a glass panel in the bottom HUD, between the interval and resistance panels. Grade now, metres climbed, kilometres ridden, then the profile: the terrain silhouette, the ridden part filled brighter, a strip of the plan's segments along the bottom and a volt line where you are.
  • Race bar (.race-bar): the top bar over the world. Lockup, the map and route ("Old Town Criterium · Cathedral Circuit · vs Bots, medium"), the GPU backend, the bike, the clock and the ride's actions.

A class or custom ride has no road, so it has no route profile: it shows the plan chart (.class-profile), which draws resistance, not elevation. Both are "profiles" in the code; this page covers the road, the Plan chart page covers the plan. Other riders are not drawn on the profile in the design: positions live on the leaderboard, gaps in the power panel.

Anatomy

  1. GradeBarlow 36px, signed ("+0.5", "−3.2") with a muted "%"; coloured by steepness.
  2. ClimbedMountain icon, metres climbed so far, muted label.
  3. DistanceKilometres ridden, two decimals, right-aligned.
  4. TerrainThe route's elevation in a 0 0 1800 100 viewBox stretched to the panel; --race-terrain at 16% (foreground at 16% on paper).
  5. Ridden partThe same path clipped to your position, at 42%.
  6. Segment strip6 units tall along the bottom; each segment in its --kind, efforts at full strength, the rest at 55%.
  7. You are hereA 2px --color-volt line from the road down to the strip (vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke).

Variants

Grade

The grade numeral is the only part that changes colour, so the rider knows what the road is doing without reading the number. Downhill is blue (recovery), a climb is amber, a steep climb is the heart red. Flat stays in the foreground colour.

Resistance profile (no road)

Classes and custom rides use .class-profile (20px) and .class-profile--lg (80px) in the same place a race uses the route profile. They share the stretch-to-fit viewBox and the segment colours; the y-axis is resistance level, not metres. Full documentation on Plan chart.

30 Minute HIIT Cardio at both heights.

Sizes

The route panel has one size: the profile is 56px tall (--space-14) and as wide as the panel, which takes whatever the HUD grid leaves between the 300px interval panel and the 220px resistance panel (--race-hud-interval-width, --race-hud-resistance-width). The race bar is one row (36px controls plus 10px padding) and wraps to two on narrow screens.

States

The profile is a picture that redraws once a second; its states are the ride's. The race bar's buttons carry the pointer and focus states.

Light theme draws the glass as paper at 70% and the terrain from the foreground colour (ride.css extends the root-only rule to any light wrapper).

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: the route panel is hidden (display: none below 640px): the phone HUD keeps only the interval panel, because the world already shows the road and space goes to power. Distance and grade are read in the text alternative and announced at milestones (each kilometre). The race bar shows the lockup, the clock and icon-only buttons, 48px tall (ride.css).
  • 640px: the route panel appears full width under the interval panel; the bar shows "/ 30:00" and button labels.
  • 768px: the bar shows the map, route and backend.
  • 1024px: the HUD becomes three columns (300px · 1fr · 220px) and the profile takes the middle; the bar shows the bike.

Theming

The panel reads --color-ink through .race-glass (70% plus a 12px blur), --race-terrain for the silhouette in dark, --color-fg in light, --color-volt for the here line, the segment kind colours and the grade text colours (--color-rest-fg, --color-warn-fg, --color-heart-fg). Hairlines and wells over the world are written as color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-fg) 10%, transparent) so both themes keep them visible on any photo.

/* A brighter silhouette for a night map (Neon Docklands) */
.race-route { --race-terrain: var(--palette-bone); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The profile SVG is role="img" named with the route's relief ("Route profile, 43 m of elevation"). The live values beside it are plain text. The race bar is a <header>; its clock is role="timer", which is not a live region by default, so it never chatters. Long description: a .ride-data table of route facts (W3C complex images, APG disclosure).

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves through the race bar in visual order: Pause or Resume, Finish, Leave or End, Full screen. The profile is skipped.
Enter, SpaceActivates a bar button; toggles the route table.
EscLeaves full screen (browser behaviour). It does not leave the race.

Focus

Bar buttons take the shared ring. Over a bright sky the ring sits on the bar's own ink glass (75%), never on the photo, so it keeps 3:1.

Labelling

Bar buttons are icon plus a label that hides below 640px; the accessible name is always the aria-label ("Leave race", "End ride") and the visible label is aria-hidden so it is not read twice. The bike's state is in a visually hidden word ("connected", "disconnected"), not only the icon colour. Units are words in the table ("km", "m"), and the minus in a downhill grade is a true minus sign (U+2212).

The glass is checked against solid ink, the colour it tends to as the photo darkens; on a bright sky the 70% glass plus blur is what keeps it there, so never lower the glass opacity.

Motion

The ridden part and the here line advance once a second with no transition; there is nothing to reduce.

Touch

The profile is not a target. Bar buttons are 36px in the design; under 640px ride.css raises them to 48px (--target-ride), the plan's size for anything pressed mid-ride.

Content

  • The bar's route line is route name · mode: "Cathedral Circuit · vs Bots, medium", "Cathedral Circuit · vs your best", "Cathedral Circuit · Live race".
  • Grades are signed with one decimal ("+0.5", "−2.1"); distance two decimals in km ("1.62 km"); climbing whole metres ("10 m climbed").
  • The clock is elapsed over planned: "5:01 / 30:00"; the total hides on phones.
  • Actions are one word: Pause, Resume, Finish, Leave, End.
