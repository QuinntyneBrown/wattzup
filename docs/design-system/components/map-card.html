Map card

A map’s photo, name and progress, or the goal that opens it, on Home and along the unlock path in Map Select.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

A map card shows one map as a door: its photo, its name and how well the rider has ridden it (stars, best time) or, when it is locked, the one goal that opens it. Maps open in a fixed order, so the cards always appear in that order.

  • Map tile (.map-tile in .map-tiles): Home’s Race door, four maps in a 2- or 4-column grid. A locked tile still links, to Map Select, where its goal is.
  • Map row (.map-row on a .map-path): Map Select’s full list, one row per map on a vertical path of dots, with facts, stars and best time, or the unlock goal.
  • Three map states: open (volt dot, a link to Race Setup), next (gold dot, gold ring, the goal in gold: the map the current goal opens) and locked (hollow dot, greyed photo, the goal in faint text).
  • Not for classes: use the class card. The Profile maps table lists the same facts as a table.

Anatomy

  1. Path dot20px circle: volt (open), gold (next), hollow 2px line (locked). Decorative.
  2. Thumb16:9 photo in a raised well, --radius-lg; 96px wide, 176px from 640px.
  3. Name16px semibold; turns volt on hover; muted when locked.
  4. FactsDistance · climbing · difficulty, 14px muted, one line with ellipsis.
  5. Stars and best16px stars and “Best 27:10” (tabular numerals); or the unlock goal with a lock icon.
  6. Chevron20px faint, from 640px only; open rows only.

Sizes

Cards take the width of their column; they have no size modifiers.

WidthBehaviour
Map tilePhoto 4:3, name 14px medium, stars 14px. Column width = (container − 3 × 12px) / 4 from 640px, / 2 below.
Map rowThumb 96px → 176px at 640px, 16:9; 8px padding inside a --radius-xl row; dot 20px, line 2px.

  • Hover on an open card turns the name volt; a map row also gets a panel fill. Active has no extra treatment beyond the browser’s press.
  • Locked rows stay focusable with aria-disabled="true" so keyboard and screen-reader users reach the goal, which is in the name. The mocks use disabled, which drops them from the tab order (see Sources).
  • Disabled: never. A map is open or locked; there is no third state.
  • Earned is not a state of the card but of its stars: three gold stars on Prairie Paceline.

Theming

Cards read --color-raised (thumb well), --color-panel (row hover and the next row), --color-volt (open dot, 50% path line), --color-gold (next dot, 40% ring) and --color-gold-fg (the next goal). The path line under locked maps is --color-line. In light the next row is a white panel on paper with a pale gold ring; the goal text switches to deep gold so it keeps 4.5:1.

The lock icon on a greyed photo now sits on a small glass disc (--color-scrim-strong, game.css): on paper the greyed photo is pale and a bare bone lock was nearly invisible.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

The path is an <ol> because the order is the unlock order. Open cards are links (APG Link pattern); locked rows are <button type="button" aria-disabled="true"> (APG Button pattern) whose name ends with “, locked.” or “, locked, your next unlock.” and then reads the goal. The dots, line, thumb and chevron are decorative.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves through every map in unlock order, locked ones included.
EnterOpen card: opens Race Setup for that map. Locked row: nothing happens; the goal has already been read.
SpaceSame as Enter on a locked row (no action); scrolls the page on a link, as links do.

Focus

The shared ring on the row or tile box (--radius-xl rows, --radius-lg tiles), 2px offset, so it clears the gold ring of the next row.

Labelling

An open card’s name is its visible text (name, facts, “1 of 3 stars”, best). A locked Home tile uses aria-label="Neon Docklands, locked: see how to open it" because it leads to Map Select. Stars inside are role="img" with their count.

Motion

Hover colour and fill use --duration-fast; none under reduced motion. When a map opens, Home shows the gold unlock banner and the tile simply appears open; the card itself does not animate.

Touch

Rows are at least 70px tall (54px thumb + 16px padding) and full width; tiles are about 156 × 150px on a phone.

Content

  • Facts: “14.2 km · 140 m climbing · Easy”: one decimal for km under 100, whole metres, difficulty one of Easy, Moderate, Hard, Brutal.
  • Best: “Best 27:10”, m:ss; omit when the rider has not finished the map.
  • Goal: one sentence without a full stop, starting with a verb: “Finish top 3 vs Bots on Old Town Criterium”, “Earn 2★ on Cliffside Coast”, “Reach rider level 10”.
  • Map names are proper names and keep their capitals; never shorten them (not “Old Town”).
