Radio group

A radio group asks for exactly one answer from a short set. Telo Ride's are mostly cards, each with a title and a sentence ("Bots — 9 computer riders. Starts the moment you're ready."); plain radios cover compact forms.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Use a radio group when the rider must pick one of two to five options and needs to see them all to choose. Use cards when each option needs a sentence of explanation: Race Setup's "Race against" (.mode-option), Custom Setup's training mode (.training-mode), Class Start's "How do you want to ride?" (.ride-option). Use plain radios (.radio) in compact forms. Short labels with no explanation read better as a segmented control; long lists as a select.

Anatomy

  1. Icon20px, muted; volt when checked.
  2. Title16px semibold: the option's name.
  3. Body14px muted: what picking it means.
  4. Card--radius-xl, 1px --color-line, 16px padding; checked: volt border and a 10% volt tint. role="radio" + aria-checked.

Sizes

One size for each: cards fill their column (16px padding), plain radios are a 16px circle with a 14px label.

States

Active on a radio checks it on press, so it looks like Checked. Disabled cards say why ("Opens at level 3") and show a lock, as Race Setup does for a new rider. Invalid only happens in a plain group with no default (rare: pre-pick the safest option instead).

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

WidthRule
360 (base)Cards stack full width, 8px apart (12px for training modes). Body text wraps; the icon stays top-aligned. Horizontal plain groups wrap.
640 (sm)Training modes go two-up (.training-modes). Mode and ride options stay stacked: their sentences need the width.
1024 (lg)Cards sit in the 420px setup aside.

Theming

Cards read --color-line (border), --color-faint (hover border), --color-volt (checked border and dot) and a 10% volt mix for the checked fill (.training-mode uses --color-raised instead). Plain radios share the checkbox's --choice-* tokens: an ink circle with a 3:1 border and an 8px volt dot. Volt is identical in both themes; the tint lands on paper or ink.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain radios: native <input type="radio"> sharing a name, in a <fieldset> with a <legend>. Cards: role="radiogroup" labelled by its heading, buttons with role="radio" and aria-checked, roving tabindex, per the APG Radio Group pattern. A card is never a link with role="radio".

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabEnters the group on the checked option; the next Tab leaves the group.
↓ → / ↑ ←Moves to and checks the next / previous option, wrapping; disabled options are skipped.
SpaceChecks the focused option if it is not checked.

Focus

Cards take the shared ring at a 2px offset outside the rounded card; plain radios ring the 16px circle.

Labelling

The group is named by its visible heading ("Race against") through aria-labelledby. Each card's name is its title; its body is read as part of the name (title + sentence), so keep bodies to one sentence. Icons and the drawn dot are aria-hidden. A locked card adds its reason to the visible text.

The unchecked card border (--color-line) is decorative: the title and body identify the card. The checked state is the volt border plus the tint plus the volt icon (or dot), announced as "checked".

Motion

Border and fill ease over --duration-fast; the dot scales in. All instant under reduced motion.

Touch

Whole cards are targets (at least 72px tall). Plain radios: the label row is the target.

Content

  • Titles are the option's name, two or three words: "Your best time", "Bots", "Riders online".
  • Bodies are one sentence on what happens: "Solo. A ghost of your fastest ride sets the pace."
  • Live numbers belong in the body ("14 riders queuing now"); update them politely, never by re-rendering the group.
  • Pre-select the option most riders want (Bots on Race Setup; "Ride with a group" when a session is open).
