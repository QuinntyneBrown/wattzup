Leaderboard

Who is where: the start grid, the live race and class boards and the final standings, with your row always in volt.

Overview

A leaderboard ranks riders in a race or a group class. The rider’s own row is always filled volt (“you”), bots carry a bot icon, and every rider has a jersey dot in their colour. Four boards share the rules:

  • Start grid (.start-grid, Race Lobby): the seats before the start, with level, country and a Ready tick; open seats are dashed. You are in volt text, not a fill.
  • Race board (.race-board, Race HUD): live standings on HUD glass with the time gap to you, from 768px.
  • Class board (.class-board, Class Ride): the group ranked by output in kJ, with your place in the head.
  • Standings (.standings, Results): the final order with finish times or output.
  • For a ranked table with sortable columns use a table; boards are lists with one ranking.

Anatomy

  1. PositionTabular numeral, right-aligned in a fixed column, faint (inherits on your row).
  2. Jersey dot10px circle, --jersey from the rider’s colour.
  3. NameTruncates with an ellipsis; “You” for the rider, semibold.
  4. Bot icon16px, faint, role="img" aria-label="Bot".
  5. ValueFinish time, gap (“+0:03”) or output (“65 kJ”), tabular.
  6. You rowVolt fill, ink text, aria-current="true".

Sizes

Row heights come from the context: start grid 48px (12px padding), race board 32px, class board 36px, standings 44px. The race board is 260px wide (--race-board-width), the class board 280px (--classes-board-width).

Rows are not interactive: no hover, focus, active or disabled. “Selected” is replaced by current (aria-current="true") on your row. In the lobby, ready is a tick with role="img" aria-label="Ready"; your own readiness is the “I’m ready” toggle above the grid.

Rows sliding

When places swap, the two rows trade places by translating over --duration-fast (150ms) with --ease-out. The DOM order changes at once; the translate only animates the move (FLIP). Reduced motion: the rows swap instantly.

Theming

Your row reads --color-volt with --color-on-fill text in both themes. Positions, bot icons and units are --color-faint; values --color-muted. The race board sits on .race-glass (ink at 70% with a 12px blur; paper at 70% in light). Jersey colours (--jersey-*) are content data and theme-independent.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Every board is an <ol> (its order is the ranking) inside a labelled region: aria-labelledby the board title, or aria-label="Final standings". Your row has aria-current="true". Jersey dots are decorative; the bot icon is role="img" aria-label="Bot". (APG Structural roles)

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabBoards contain no focusable elements; Tab passes them. Screen-reader users read them with list navigation (L / I).

Focus

None: rows are never focusable. Do not add tabindex to make a board “reachable”; list navigation already does.

Labelling

Live regions. Never make a live board a live region: it changes every second and would talk over the ride. Instead keep one visually hidden role="status" near the HUD that announces only your place, when it changes and at most once every 10 seconds: “You moved up to 4th.” The lobby count “6 / 10” and the class board head are not live either; the start clock is role="timer", which is silent by default. Results standings are static and need no live region.

Content

  • You are always “You”, never the rider’s name. People keep their handles as typed (“lena.k”, “Ollie R.”); bots are “Pacer Nova” … “Pacer Echo”.
  • Gaps: “+0:03” behind you, “−0:02” ahead (true minus sign); your row has no gap.
  • Places: “5th / 9” in the class board head; “2nd of 10” in prose.
  • Lobby meta: country code · level, “DE · Lv 4”; your seat shows only “Lv 7”.
  • Output: whole kJ with the unit in a smaller faint span: “65 kJ”.
