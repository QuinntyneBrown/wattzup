Pagination

Splits a long list into pages and moves between them. Telo Ride's lists are short today; pagination is ready for the first one that is not, such as a veteran's 146 recent rides.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Use pagination when a list is too long to load or scan at once and people want to land on a stable place in it ("page 3 of my rides"). Profile shows a handful of recent rides; a veteran has 146, which is where it would start.

  • Numbered when people jump around (rides by date).
  • Previous / next only, when they read in order and the total does not matter.
  • Do not paginate leaderboards mid-race or lists under about 25 items; show them whole.
  • Prefer "Show more" (a button that appends) on phones where a list is browsed rather than searched.

Anatomy

  1. Summary"21–30 of 146 rides": muted 14px with Barlow numerals.
  2. StepPrevious / Next: chevron and label (label hidden below 640px), 36px tall.
  3. Page36px square, Barlow numeral; the current one is bone with ink text, aria-current="page".
  4. GapAn ellipsis in --color-faint where pages are skipped; hidden from screen readers.

Sizes

One size: 36px controls (--control-height-sm), matching the header's links and the segmented control used for page size.

States

PartDefaultHoverFocusActiveCurrentDisabled
Page 4 4 4 4 3 never
Step Next Next Next Next — Next
  • Current is bone with ink text, the same inverse pair as the selected tier tab; one per nav, aria-current="page".
  • Disabled is Previous on the first page and Next on the last: an <a> with no href and aria-disabled="true", at 40% opacity. It stays in place so the row does not jump.
  • Loading: when the next page loads in place, keep the old rows with aria-busy="true" on the list and announce "Page 4 of 15" in a polite live region once it arrives.

Responsive behaviour

  • Under 640px: the summary sits on its own line; the controls become ‹ "Page 3 of 15" › with icon-only steps. No row of small numbers for thumbs.
  • From 640px (--layout-breakpoint-sm): summary left, numbers right, steps with their labels.
  • Show at most seven slots (first, last, current ± 1, two gaps) so the row never wraps.

Theming

Steps and pages read --pagination-fg (--color-muted), hover --pagination-bg-hover (--color-raised); the current page --pagination-bg-current / --pagination-fg-current (--color-fg on --color-ink, flipping to ink on paper in light). No volt: moving through a list is not the view's primary action.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <nav> landmark named for its list ("Recent rides pages") around a list of links; the current page link has aria-current="page". There is no APG widget; it follows the landmark and link conventions (and the consistent navigation criterion).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabPrevious → page links → Next; disabled steps (no href) are skipped.
EnterGoes to that page; focus moves to the list's heading so the new rows are read from the top.

Focus

Shared ring around the 36px square. After a page change, never leave focus on a link that moved or vanished.

Labelling

Page links are named "Page 3" (the numeral alone is ambiguous out of context); steps "Previous page" / "Next page", with their visible label aria-hidden so the name does not double. Name the nav after its list when a page has two.

Contrast

Page numeral and steps on a panel

Motion

Fill and colour over --duration-fast. Do not animate the list sliding between pages.

Touch

36px squares with 4px gaps above 640px; below, only two 36px steps either side of the text, well apart.

Content

  • Summary: "21–30 of 146 rides" with an en dash and the list's noun.
  • Compact: "Page 3 of 15".
  • Steps: "Previous" and "Next"; never "Older" / "Newer" unless the list is strictly by date and says so.
  • Page size label names the thing: "Rides per page".
