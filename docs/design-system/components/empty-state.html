Empty state

Where content will be, say how to get it: icon, title, one sentence, one action.

Overview

An empty state fills the space where content will be, and says how to get it. Telo Ride has few true empties because most collections ship with the app (the manifest marks empty as not applicable on most screens: "The catalogue ships with the app; every shelf always holds classes."). The ones it has are first-run and setup moments:

  • No bike on Custom ride: the dashboard asks for one ("Connect your bike").
  • Bluetooth off: the browser has no radio to ask with ("Turn on Bluetooth").
  • New rider on Profile: no rides yet ("Nothing yet. Your first race shows up here, with the XP it earned.").
  • Nothing sent in the custom ride's command log before a bike is connected.

The formula: icon, title, one sentence, one action. A hint may follow when riders often get stuck. The first-run Home and Class Library are not empties: Home shows two open maps and the next ride, and Class Library shows locked classes with "Opens at level 5". Show what there is, and what opens next, before an empty message.

Anatomy

  1. Icon32px, the tone colour (volt for "connect yours", warn for a blocked device, muted otherwise); aria-hidden.
  2. Title20px semibold (24px for the dashboard's "Connect your bike"); the state as a task.
  3. One sentence14px muted, relaxed leading; what to do, with the real names.
  4. One actionOne button: primary when it is the next step, raised when it re-checks.
  5. Hint (optional)12px faint; the common snag.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: full empties keep 24px side padding; the action button goes full width inside the card.
  • 640px: the card caps at 28rem (--layout-panel-narrow) and centres.
  • 1024px: on the custom ride the empty sits over the video area only; the resistance panel stays visible (disabled) beside it.
  • Compact empties stay left-aligned at every width.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A <section> with a heading at the right level for the page. When it replaces content after something changes (Bluetooth turned off mid-setup), put it in role="status"; a lost connection mid-ride is an error and uses role="alert". No APG widget.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches the one action.
EnterRuns it.

Focus

When a screen opens empty, focus starts as normal (skip link, then header). When an empty appears in place of content the rider was using, move focus to its heading (tabindex="-1").

Labelling

The title says the state as a task ("Connect your bike"), the sentence the next step. The icon is decoration. The action is named for what it does ("Connect bike", "Check again").

Content

  • Title: the task, not the absence ("Connect your bike", not "No bike").
  • One sentence with real names: the bike model, the setting, the map.
  • One action. Primary when it is the next step; raised when it re-checks.
  • Profile's "Nothing yet." is the one place an absence leads, because it then says what will fill it.
  • No illustrations; the photo and dashboard behind already carry the mood.

Drift fixed during extraction
