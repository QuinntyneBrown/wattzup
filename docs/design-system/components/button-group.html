Button group

A button group joins related buttons into one control. The segmented control picks one of two to four options on a shared track; the toolbar keeps the ride controls together and lets arrow keys move between them.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Use a segmented control (.segmented) when the rider picks exactly one of two to four short, mutually exclusive options and the choice applies at once: class length on Class Library (All, 20 min, 30 min, 60 min), pack strength on Race Setup (easy, medium, hard), feedback kind (Bug, Improvement, Kudos). Use a toolbar (.toolbar) for a row of separate actions that belong together, like the ride controls (Pause, End, Full screen).

  • More than four options, or options with a sentence of explanation: use a radio group of cards ("Race against").
  • Options that toggle independently: use filter chips (Custom Setup's plan presets).
  • Options that show different content in place: use tabs (the ride view's Video / Power vs. plan).

Anatomy

  1. Selected optionRaised fill (or bone with --strong), fg label; aria-pressed="true" or aria-checked="true".
  2. Option36px tall (40px with --md), 12px padding, --radius-md, Inter Tight 14px medium, muted label.
  3. Track--color-panel on the page or --color-raised on a panel, 4px padding and gap, --radius-lg.

Sizes

Small: options 36px (--control-height-sm), track 44px. Medium (--md): options 40px, track 48px. Toolbars take the size of the buttons inside.

A segmented option has no separate pressed look: selection applies on press, so active shows the new selection. Focus on a selected option keeps both the fill and the ring.

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

WidthRule
360 (base)A segmented control never wraps. Four short options (All, 20 min, 30 min, 60 min) fit 328px; in a narrow column use --block so options share the width. The toolbar wraps as a unit with the ride header; its buttons grow to 48px under 640px.
640 (sm)Class Library's header puts the filter beside the title (.library-head wraps it under the lead below this).
1024 (lg)No change; block controls are capped by their 420px aside.

If the labels do not fit at 360px, there are too many options: switch to a select or a radio group.

Theming

The track reads --color-panel (raised with --raised), options read --color-muted and turn --color-fg on hover; the selected option reads --color-raised (panel on a raised track; bone --color-fg with an ink label under --strong). In light, bone becomes ink and ink becomes paper, so the strong selection inverts cleanly.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

  • Single choice (pack strength, feedback kind): role="radiogroup" with a label, options role="radio" + aria-checked, roving tabindex per the APG Radio Group pattern.
  • Filter (class length): role="group" with aria-label, toggle buttons with aria-pressed per the APG Button pattern; every option is a tab stop.
  • Toolbar: role="toolbar" with aria-label, roving tabindex per the APG Toolbar pattern.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabRadiogroup and toolbar: one stop (the checked option or the last focused button). Filter: each option.
→ ↓ / ← ↑Radiogroup: moves focus and checks the next / previous option, wrapping. Toolbar: moves focus only (← →).
Home / EndToolbar: first / last control.
Space / EnterFilter: toggles the pressed option. Radiogroup: checks the focused option. Toolbar: activates the focused button.

Focus

The shared 2px ring sits inside the track's 4px padding, so it is never clipped. The selected option keeps its fill under focus.

Labelling

The group needs a name: a visible heading via aria-labelledby ("Pack strength") or aria-label ("Class length"). Option labels are their text; stacked icons are aria-hidden.

The selected option differs from the rest by fill and label colour (muted to fg) and is announced as pressed or checked; colour is not the only cue.

Motion

Colour and fill ease over --duration-fast; nothing slides. Reduced motion makes the change instant.

Touch

Options are 36px tall and at least 44px wide in every product use; the 4px track padding sits between neighbours.

Content

  • One or two words per option, parallel in form: "20 min / 30 min / 60 min", "Easy / Medium / Hard".
  • Sentence case; the mock's lowercase "easy / medium / hard" (CSS capitalize) is written as text instead.
  • Put what the choice means beneath the control, not inside it: "Around 190 W. A top 3 here earns the second star."
  • "All" comes first in a filter.
