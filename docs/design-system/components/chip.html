Chip

Overview

Chips are small capsules that pick, show or remove a value. Custom workout's plan picker is the one in the mocks: .preset-chips of .preset-chip buttons ("GCN 30-min HIIT", "Tabata", "40 / 20", "Minute on", "Long climbs"), raised and muted, turning bone on ink when pressed. This page documents them as filter chips and adds .chip for static chips (a labelled value) and removable chips (an active filter with a remove button).

  • Filter chip: a toggle button with aria-pressed; one or many may be on.
  • Removable chip: a value already applied; its only control is the 24px remove button.
  • Static chip: a value with no action (a segment kind with its swatch). For status, use a badge.
  • When exactly one of two to four options must be on, use a segmented control instead.

Anatomy

  1. Label14px medium (12px small), sentence case, one line.
  2. Remove button (removable)24px circular .chip__remove with an × icon and "Remove … filter" as its name.
  3. Capsule36px tall (--control-height-sm), --radius-full, 16px padding (filter) or 14px (chip); raised fill; pressed filter chips fill bone with ink text.

Both themes

30 minHIIT
30 minHIIT
  • Pressed: aria-pressed="true", bone fill (--color-fg) and ink label: the strongest contrast on the panel, so the choice reads at a glance.
  • Hover: the muted label brightens to --color-fg; the fill stays.
  • Disabled: 40% opacity, used while the ride is starting; prefer hiding presets that do not apply.

Responsive behaviour

  • Chip groups wrap onto more lines (flex-wrap, 8px gaps); they never scroll sideways and never truncate a label.
  • At 360px the five presets take two lines; no change at 640 or 1024px.

Theming

.chip { --chip-bg: var(--color-raised); --chip-fg: var(--color-fg); --chip-height: var(--control-height-sm); }
.preset-chip[aria-pressed="true"] { background: var(--color-fg); color: var(--color-ink); }

Pressed is an inversion (bone on ink in dark, ink on paper in light), so it needs no extra token and always passes.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Filter chips are toggle buttons (<button aria-pressed>) inside role="group" with a label, following the APG button pattern. Removable chips are text plus a real <button>. Static chips are spans.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves chip to chip (each is its own tab stop).
Enter, SpaceToggles a filter chip; activates a remove button.
Backspace, DeleteOn a remove button: removes that chip (same as activating it).

Focus

Shared ring on the chip (filter) or the remove button (removable). After a removal, focus moves to the next chip, or the previous one, or the group's heading when none are left.

Labelling

The chip label names a filter chip; the group label says what they choose ("Plans"). Remove buttons say the whole action ("Remove HIIT filter"). Announce removals politely ("HIIT filter removed").

Motion

Colour transitions over --duration-fast; active scales to 0.98. Both stop under reduced motion.

Touch

Chips are 36px tall with 8px between them; remove buttons are 24px with the chip's padding around them.

Content

  • Labels are names, not verbs ("Tabata", "Long climbs"), and stay one to three words.
  • Numbers keep their spacing ("40 / 20").
  • Remove buttons are labelled "Remove {label} filter".
