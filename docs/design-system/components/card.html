Card

The surfaces that hold everything else. Telo Ride uses two depths, the panel on the page and the well inside a panel, and builds every tile, option and dashboard block from them.

Overview

Telo Ride has no single "card" in the mocks; it has many hand-made ones that share two surfaces. This page names them so new screens stop re-deriving them:

  • .panel: the page-level surface (--color-panel, 24px radius). Connect Bike's device cards, the dashboard's side column, Results' summary.
  • .well: a recessed block inside a panel (--color-raised, 16px radius). The Next ride reward, the diagnostics packet count.
  • .card: a panel with structure (media, body, footer), optionally interactive (the whole card follows one link, like a map tile) or selectable (a real radio, like the race mode options).

Do not use a card just to draw a box. Plain content on the page reads better; a card says "this is one thing you can act on or compare".

Anatomy

  1. Media (optional)16:9 photo with descriptive alt (or empty alt when the title says it all), an optional .photo-tag.
  2. Eyebrow14px muted: kind or category.
  3. Title18px semibold; in an interactive card it holds the one link, stretched over the card.
  4. Meta / footer14px muted facts; a footer holds secondary actions above the stretched link.

Selectable
  • Hover: interactive cards lift the title to --color-volt-fg and scale the photo 3% (the map tiles and class cards); selectable cards darken the border to --color-faint.
  • Focus: the whole card draws the shared ring (:has(:focus-visible)), not just the title link.
  • Selected: a checked radio inside: volt border, --color-volt-subtle fill, volt icon.
  • Disabled / locked: a selectable card with a disabled radio fades to 50%; a locked map is a static card that says why (pages/map-select), not a disabled link.
  • Loading: use skeleton shapes inside the card, never a spinner over it.

Responsive behaviour

  • Cards fill their grid track; .grid--auto fits as many 16rem columns as the row holds, so the same markup is one column at 360px, two at 640px and three or four at 1024px.
  • Padding steps from 20px to 24px at 640px.
  • Media keeps 16:9; text never overlays the photo except a .photo-tag.
  • The Next ride card switches from stacked to photo-left at 1024px; that is a layout (.split-like), not a card variant.

Theming

Override the block tokens: --card-bg, --card-border, --card-radius, --card-pad, --card-title-hover. Variants only change these. In light the panel is paper on a slightly darker page, so outline cards matter more there: on a light panel prefer .card--outline over a well when the step in fill is too faint.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A card is not a widget: use <article> for a self-contained item (a map, a class), <section aria-labelledby> for a block of a page. Interactive cards follow the "one link, stretched" pattern: the title's <a> is the only link, its ::after covers the card, other buttons sit above it. Selectable cards are native radios (APG Radio Group behaviour for free) inside a <fieldset> with a legend.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabOne stop per interactive card (its link), then its footer buttons; one stop for a whole selectable group.
EnterFollows the card's link.
↑ ↓ / ← →Moves the selection between selectable cards.
SpaceSelects the focused selectable card.

Focus

The ring is drawn on the card (24px or 16px radius) via :has(.card__link:focus-visible) or :has(.card__input:focus-visible), so it outlines the whole hit area.

Labelling

The link's name is the title only ("Prairie Paceline"); the rest of the card is read as context, not crammed into the link. Never wrap a whole card in <a>: the name becomes every word in it. Photo alt describes the place, or is empty when it adds nothing.

Motion

Title colour over --duration-fast; photo scale over --duration-base; press scale 0.99. With reduced motion only the colour changes.

Touch

The whole card is the target (well over 44px). Footer buttons are 36px and separated from the stretched link by the rule.

Content

  • Title: the thing's own name ("Old Town Criterium", "30 Minute HIIT Cardio").
  • Eyebrow: kind and difficulty, two words at most ("Race · Easy").
  • Meta: facts with units, middle-dot separated ("140 m climbing · 1 of 3 stars").
  • Selectable title + one sentence on what choosing it means.
