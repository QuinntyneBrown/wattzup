Progress bar

How far through: XP to the next level, minutes through the ride, a file being saved.

Overview

A progress bar shows how far through something you are. Telo Ride has two in the mocks: the gold XP bar (.xp-bar: in the header beside the level badge, and large on Profile, "1,840 / 2,600 XP") and the ride progress strip (.ride-progress, an SVG coloured by segment kind with a bright head at "6:52 of 30:00"). .progress is the generic block: a labelled track and fill with tones, sizes and an indeterminate bar for waits with no known length.

  • Determinate: you know the total (XP to the next level, ride time, a file being saved).
  • Indeterminate: you do not (looking for bikes). Prefer a spinner inside a button or row; use the bar when it is the main thing on screen.
  • The interval countdown and level-up meters live in Ride readout.

Anatomy

  1. Label14px --color-muted; what is filling ("Level 7").
  2. ValueBarlow numeral; the amount in words people use ("1,840 / 2,600 XP"); hidden from screen readers, which read aria-valuetext.
  3. Track and fill6px (8px large, 4px small), fully rounded; fill width is --value.
  4. Foot (optional)What is left ("760 XP to level 8") or what is wrong.

States

Progress bars are read-only. Hover and focus belong to whatever wraps them (the header XP is inside a link). "Disabled" is a paused bar; "loading" is indeterminate; "invalid" is a stopped bar in heart with a reason in the foot.

The gold fill on raised is about 1.3:1 in light. That is acceptable: the value is also text beside the bar, and the bar is not the only cue (WCAG 1.4.11 applies to the parts needed to understand it).

Responsive behaviour

  • Bars fill their container; labels truncate before the value does.
  • 360px: the header hides the XP label and keeps the 6px bar beside the level badge; Profile's bar goes full width.
  • 640px: Profile's bar caps at 28rem.
  • 1024px: the ride strip spans the video width; its height stays 8px.

Theming

.progress { --progress-track: var(--color-raised); --progress-fill: var(--color-volt-fg); --progress-height: var(--space-1-5); }
.progress--gold { --progress-fill: var(--color-gold); }  /* XP only */

Fills use the -fg tokens (--color-volt-fg, --color-rest-fg, --color-ok-fg, --color-heart-fg): on paper the base volt (2.8:1) and green (1.6:1) are too light against the raised track, while the deeper -fg values pass 3:1 in both themes. Gold stays --color-gold because the XP value is always written beside it. Ride progress takes its colours from the segment kind (--kind).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" on the track with aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax, aria-valuenow and an aria-valuetext people would say ("1,840 of 2,600 XP"). Indeterminate bars leave out aria-valuenow. Named by its visible label (aria-labelledby). See the ARIA progressbar role and the APG meter pattern for values that are levels rather than progress.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips the bar; it is not focusable.

Focus

None on the bar. When it sits inside a link (the header XP), the link draws the ring.

Labelling

Label what fills, value in words. Do not announce every change: update aria-valuenow silently and announce only the end ("Packet log saved.") in a status region.

Motion

The fill eases over --duration-base. The indeterminate bar sweeps over --duration-pulse; under reduced motion it stops at the middle of the track, still showing that something is happening through its label.

Touch

Not a target.

Content

  • Label: what is filling ("Level 7", "Saving packet log").
  • Value: the unit people use ("1,840 / 2,600 XP", "6:52 of 30:00", "62%").
  • Foot: what is left ("760 XP to level 8") or why it stopped, with the fix.
  • Indeterminate labels end in an ellipsis ("Looking for bikes nearby…").
