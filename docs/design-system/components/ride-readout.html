Component · Ride

Ride readout

Readouts are the numbers a rider glances at mid-effort: watts against the target, cadence, heart rate, speed. They are huge, tabular, unit-labelled, coloured only where the colour means something, and they never talk over the rider.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

Every ride screen has one primary number, power, and a handful of secondary ones. Three families draw them:

  • Race power panel (.race-power): a glass panel over the world. Position and gaps on top, watts and target in the middle, cadence, heart and speed underneath.
  • Dashboard readouts (.ride-readouts, .ride-readout__*): the custom ride's telemetry row. Power at 96–128px, cadence at 60px, each with a foot line (target and delta, or the cadence to aim for).
  • Secondary metrics (.ride-metrics, .ride-metric__*): a ruled row of five smaller figures: heart rate, average power, speed, distance, energy.

The class ride uses tiles for the same numbers (.class-stat): see Stat. Times that count down (an interval's "0:29") belong to the HUD panel; elapsed clocks to the bar headers. Use a readout only for a live measurement; a number that does not change during the ride is a fact (description list).

Anatomy

  1. Position"5th" in volt Barlow 48px, "/ 10" muted 20px. Solo shows "Ahead" or "Behind" your best.
  2. GapsAhead and behind in m:ss with a sign, 14px figures under 12px muted labels.
  3. ValueThe measurement: Barlow Condensed bold, tabular figures ('tnum'), line height 0.9 so the digits sit on the unit's baseline.
  4. UnitMuted, a third the size of the value, baseline-aligned: W, rpm, bpm, km/h.
  5. Target"Target 418 W", muted 14px.
  6. Delta"On target" in volt, or the shortfall ("−47 W") in amber. Words first, colour second.
  7. StatsA <dl> of three: label 12px muted, value 24px, unit 12px faint; heart in heart red.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: the race panel pins top left under the bar (top 64px, left 12px); watts at 64px. Dashboard power is 96px; the readouts row wraps so cadence and the interval drop under power. Metrics are two per row.
  • 640px: race watts grow to 88px and the panel moves to 20px from the edge; dashboard power to 128px; metrics go five across.
  • 1024px: the race panel drops to 72px from the top and gets roomier padding (16px 20px). Dashboard readouts sit in one row with the interval pushed right (max 24rem).
  • Values never truncate or wrap: a four-digit watt value ("1250") fits because the figures are condensed and tabular. Units never wrap away from the value (white-space: nowrap on the stat).

Theming

Readouts read --color-fg for values, --color-muted for labels and big units, --color-faint for small units, and three meaning colours: --color-volt-fg (position, on target), --color-warn-fg (under target) and --color-heart-fg (heart rate). On the world they sit on .race-glass; on the dashboard on the ink page; class tiles on --color-panel. The numeral face is --font-family-display with font-feature-settings: 'tnum' 1, which every value class sets (or .numeral).

/* A partner build that wants heart rate in the foreground colour */
.ride-metric__number--heart, .race-power__stat-value--heart { color: var(--color-fg); }

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Readouts are static text in a labelled region: the race panel is a <section aria-label="Power and position">, the dashboard labels each readout with a heading ("Power", "Cadence"), stats and metrics are <dl> pairs. There is no widget pattern; the APG guidance that applies is landmark regions, and live updates follow WCAG 4.1.3 Status messages.

Live updates: what is announced

  • Never announced: anything that changes every second: watts, cadence, heart, speed, distance, gaps, clocks. No aria-live on readouts. Clocks are role="timer", whose implicit live setting is off.
  • Announced, politely, through one .ride-announcer (role="status"): a new interval ("Work, 30 seconds at level 24"), a place gained or lost that holds for 5 seconds ("Up to 4th"), 10 seconds left in an effort, paused and resumed, and the bike coming back.
  • Announced assertively: only "Lost the bike" (role="alert" on its overlay) and the 3-2-1 countdown.
  • At most one polite message every 5 seconds; a newer one replaces a queued one. A rider who wants a number moves to the region and reads it: the text is always current.

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabSkips readouts: nothing in them is focusable.
Screen reader heading or region keys (H, D in NVDA and JAWS)Jump to "Power" or the "Power and position" region and read the current values.

Focus

Readouts never take focus, and nothing steals focus to them when values change.

Labelling

Every value has a visible label and a unit in text. Units are spoken as written ("371 W"); where an abbreviation would be read badly, wrap it: <abbr title="revolutions per minute">rpm</abbr>. Deltas are words or signed numbers, never colour alone: "On target", "−47 W". No data is an em dash with the unit kept ("— W"), so the layout does not jump when the bike connects.

Motion

Values change in place once a second with no animation; tabular figures stop the digits shifting sideways. Nothing counts up or flashes.

Touch

Readouts are not targets. They never sit under a control a rider might reach for mid-effort.

Content

  • Units: W, rpm, bpm, km/h, km, kcal, kJ; always after the value, never in the label ("Power", not "Power (W)").
  • Precision: watts, cadence, heart and energy whole; speed one decimal ("26.5"); distance two ("2.24").
  • Signs use the true minus (U+2212): "−47 W", "−0:01". Gaps always carry a sign; deltas only when off target.
  • Labels are nouns in sentence case: "Heart rate", "Avg power". The aim reads "Aim for 72".
  • Position is ordinal with the field size: "5th / 10"; solo says "Ahead" or "Behind" with "Best +0:01".
