Countdown

Countdowns tell a rider how long until they ride: the lobby clock while the grid fills, a full-screen 3-2-1 in volt at the line, and a plain "starts in 9 min" for group classes. Each speaks at the moments that matter and stays quiet in between.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

  • Lobby clock (.race-lobby__clock): the race lobby's big m:ss clock with a status line above ("Waiting for riders", "On the start line"), a hint below and the I'm ready toggle. 96px on phones, 120px from 640px (--race-lobby-clock-size).
  • 3-2-1 (.race-countdown): at 0:03 the lobby is covered by ink at 85% and one 200px volt numeral (--race-countdown-size) ticks 3, 2, 1, Go. On Go the race opens.
  • Starts in: Class Start's group option and its button say when the next session begins, to the minute: "7:39 PM · starts in 9 min · 6 of 10 riders", "Join · starts in 9 min".

Time left inside a ride (an interval's "0:29") is a countdown too, but it lives in the HUD panel; elapsed time is the clock in the bar headers. Never use a countdown to create urgency that is not real: there is no "offer ends in".

Anatomy

  1. Status14px muted: "Waiting for riders", then "On the start line" once bots have filled the grid.
  2. ClockBarlow bold, tabular, m:ss; role="timer" with a sentence label ("Starts in 24 seconds").
  3. HintWhat happens next, max 20rem: "At 0:10 bots fill the rest so the race starts full."
  4. Ready togglearia-pressed: volt "I'm ready", then raised "You're ready" with a volt tick. 56px tall, full width.

Responsive behaviour

  • 360px: the clock section stacks above the start grid; the clock is 96px, the ready toggle full width. The 3-2-1 numeral stays 200px: it is the only thing on screen.
  • 640px: the clock grows to 120px; the start grid goes two columns.
  • 1024px: the clock section becomes a 320px column left of the grid (--race-lobby-aside-width). Class Start's Join button pins to the bottom of its column.

Theming

The 3-2-1 reads --color-volt-fg on --color-ink at 85%; the lobby clock is --color-fg; the ready toggle swaps --color-volt for --color-raised when pressed. In light theme the overlay is paper at 85% and the numeral turns the deep signal orange (5:1 on paper).

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Clocks are role="timer": a live region whose default is aria-live="off", so the per-second tick is never read. The 3-2-1 is the one countdown that is announced: aria-live="assertive", aria-atomic="true", with the words in the region ("Race starts in 3", then "2", "1", "Go"). The ready button is an APG toggle button. See WCAG 2.2.1 Timing adjustable: the lobby clock is a real-time event, an allowed exception.

What is announced

  • Each 3-2-1 step, assertively.
  • Lobby changes politely through the page's role="status": someone joining or leaving ("MarcoVelo left. Their seat is open again."), bots filling the seats at 0:10, and 10 seconds to go.
  • Never the clock's seconds. A rider can read the clock at any time: its accessible name is a sentence ("Starts in 24 seconds").

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabReaches I'm ready, then the start grid's controls. During the 3-2-1 nothing behind the overlay is reachable.
Enter, SpaceToggles ready.
EscDoes not cancel the countdown; Leave in the lobby header does, until 0:03.

Focus

Focus stays on I'm ready when it is pressed (the label changes, the button does not move). When the race opens, focus goes to the race's first control, not the page top.

Labelling

The toggle's label changes with its state ("I'm ready" / "You're ready") and aria-pressed carries the state, so a screen reader says "You're ready, toggle button, pressed". The tick icon is aria-hidden.

Motion

Each step fades and settles over 200ms (--duration-base, --ease-enter). Under prefers-reduced-motion the scale is dropped and the zeroed duration makes the step a cut; the one-second tick (--duration-countdown) is a timer and keeps its pace. Nothing pulses or flashes.

Touch

The ready toggle is 56px tall and full width. Nothing in the 3-2-1 is a target.

Content

  • Clocks are m:ss ("0:24"), never "24s".
  • Steps are "3", "2", "1", "Go": no exclamation mark.
  • Starts-in times round to the minute and pair with the clock time: "7:39 PM · starts in 9 min". Under a minute: "Join now · starting".
  • Hints say what happens next in a sentence: "At 0:10 bots fill the rest so the race starts full." "Keep pedalling easy. Resistance is set for the first stretch."
