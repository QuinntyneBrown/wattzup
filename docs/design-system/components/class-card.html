Class card

A follow-along class as a door: cover, length, the shape of the plan and when the next group rides.

Overview

A class card offers one follow-along class: its cover, length, the shape of its plan, its title and channel, and when the next group session starts. The whole card is one link to Class Start, where the rider chooses to ride alone or join the group.

  • Class card (.class-card) in a shelf (.class-shelf): the Class Library groups classes by length into tiers (20 min, 30 min, 60 min endurance).
  • Class teaser (.class-teaser): Home’s Classes door shows the next group class with two actions, Join group and Ride alone.
  • Seats and starts-in: group sessions start every 15 minutes and seat 8–10; the card says “Group in 9 min · 6/10”, the teaser “6 of 10 riders · 7:39 PM”.
  • Tiers: classes open at levels 1, 5, 10 and 20. A locked card (--locked) says “Opens at level 10” in gold on its cover.

Anatomy

  1. Cover16:9 photo in a raised well, --radius-lg; scales 1.03 on hover.
  2. Group tagGlass label top left: users icon (volt) + “Group in 9 min · 6/10”. Only when a group session is open.
  3. Time tagGlass label bottom right, tabular “30:00”.
  4. Plan profile20px bar chart of the resistance plan: volt work, blue rest, slate warm-up and cool-down.
  5. Title16px semibold, turns volt on hover.
  6. MetaChannel · focus, 14px muted.

Theming

Reads --color-raised for the cover well, --color-scrim-strong and --color-on-photo for the tags, --color-volt for the users icon, --color-gold for the lock label, and the plan colours (--color-volt, --color-rest, --classes-segment-easy). Tags are theme-independent; only the title and meta change with the theme.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

One <a> per card (APG Link pattern); its content is the name. The plan profile is aria-hidden on the card (Class Start shows it in full with a text summary). A locked card is <button aria-disabled="true"> (APG Button pattern).

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabMoves card to card in shelf order.
EnterOpens Class Start. On a locked card: nothing.
SpaceLocked card: nothing. On a link it scrolls, as links do.

Focus

The ring sits on .class-card__cover (2px, 2px offset, --radius-lg); the card itself sets outline: none.

Labelling

Because the cover tags come first in the DOM, the link reads “Group in 9 min · 6/10 30:00 30 Minute HIIT Cardio GCN Training · HIIT”. Give the title an id and set aria-labelledby to title then group tag so the class name is heard first. A locked card’s name must include “Opens at level 10”.

Motion

The cover photo scales over --duration-base with --ease-panel; reduced motion zeroes it.

Touch

The whole card is the target (at least 300 × 230px on a phone). The teaser’s buttons are 40px tall with 8px between them.

Content

  • Group tag: “Group in 9 min · 6/10”: minutes until the session and taken/total seats.
  • Teaser line: “6 of 10 riders · 7:39 PM”, the clock time in the rider’s locale.
  • Lock: “Opens at level 10”, never “Locked” alone.
  • Meta: channel · focus, “GCN Training · HIIT”. Titles are the video’s own.
  • Shelf blurbs: one short sentence, e.g. “Short and sharp — fits a lunch break.”
