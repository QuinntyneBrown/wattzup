Stars

Up to three gold stars score each map. They open other maps, so a rider must always be able to read how many they have and how to earn the rest.

Overview

Stars score a map: up to three, one for each rule. They are the second currency after XP and open maps (Dolomites opens at 2★ on Cliffside Coast, Col du Granit at 3★ on Dolomites). Gold fill means earned; a faint outline means still to earn. A star row is a single image with a text alternative, “1 of 3 stars”.

  • .stars: the row on map tiles (Home), map rows (Map Select), the Profile maps table and Results.
  • .star-rules: the three rules, in the Map Select aside.
  • .setup-stars: the rules for one map with what you have earned (Race Setup).
  • .map-stars: the map’s stars after a race, with “+1 new star” (Results).
  • Not for ratings, difficulty or reviews: the map’s difficulty is a word (Easy, Moderate, Hard, Brutal).

Anatomy

  1. Earned starLucide star, filled and stroked --color-gold, class is-earned.
  2. Unearned starSame path, outline only, --color-faint.
  3. Row.stars: flex, 2px gap (6px on Results); role="img" with the count as its name.

Theming

Earned stars read --color-gold-fg for fill and stroke; unearned read --color-faint. In the dark theme that is the design's gold (11:1 on ink). On paper the design's gold read 1.6:1 against white panels and failed 1.4.11, so light uses the deep gold (--palette-gold-deep), the same step the XP bar and the “+1 new star” text use. Keep the count in text nearby as well, as Home (“5 of 21 stars”) and Profile do.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A star row is role="img" with its count as the name; each SVG is aria-hidden. Setup stars are a <ul> of rules where the star is decorative and the earned state is text (“(earned)”). (APG Names and descriptions)

Keyboard

KeyAction
TabStars are never focusable; focus lands on the link or button that contains them.

Focus

None of their own. In a map tile the ring wraps the whole tile (--radius-lg).

Labelling

Say the count and the total: “1 of 3 stars”. For a rule that is worth a fixed number, say “2 stars”, not “2 of 2 stars” (the Map Select mock does; see Sources). For a change on Results, the visible “+1 new star” is the announcement.

Motion

Stars do not animate. A newly earned star on Results appears with the page; the unlock card carries the motion.

Touch

No targets of their own; see the parent card for target sizes.

Content

  • Counts: “5 of 21 stars” for a total, “1 of 3 stars” for a map, “+1 new star” / “+2 new stars” after a race.
  • The three rules are fixed and always in this order: “Finish the race”, “Top 3 against the medium pack”, “Beat your best time”.
  • In goals, “2★” is allowed inside a short phrase (“Earn 2★ on Cliffside Coast”); elsewhere write “stars”.
  • Never use stars to rate a map or a class.
