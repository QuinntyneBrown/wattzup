Pattern

Dialogs and overlays

Three things in Telo Ride open on top of a screen. Diagnostics is a centred dialog; Send feedback is a popover under its button that becomes a bottom sheet on phones; Leave this ride? is a confirmation anchored to Library. Lost the bike, Paused and the 3-2-1 are overlays, not dialogs: they cover the ride but ask nothing.

Overview

A layer interrupts. Telo Ride keeps interruptions small and close to what caused them: a popover opens under the button that opened it, so the rider's eyes don't travel and the ride stays visible around it. Only Diagnostics, which has nothing to do with any spot on the screen, opens in the middle. Any modal layer opened mid-ride pauses the ride first, and the copy says so.

Components used: Dialog, Popover, HUD panel, Countdown, Switch, Description list, Button and Tooltip.

  • One modal at a time. Opening Library while Feedback is open closes Feedback first; Diagnostics never opens over another dialog.
  • A modal beats an overlay. If the bike drops while Feedback is open, Feedback stays on top and Lost the bike waits underneath; closing Feedback reveals it.
  • Toasts float above modals but never cover the dialog's primary action.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Diagnostics, Send feedback and Leave this ride? follow the WAI-ARIA dialog (modal) pattern: role="dialog", aria-modal="true", aria-labelledby pointing at the visible title, a trigger with aria-haspopup="dialog" and aria-expanded. Overlays are not dialogs: Lost the bike is role="alert", Paused is role="status", the 3-2-1 is role="timer" with aria-live="assertive".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycle through the dialog's controls only; focus can't reach the inert page.
EscClose Diagnostics or Send feedback; in Leave this ride? it means Keep riding. Does nothing on an overlay.
Enter / SpacePress the focused button.

Focus

  • On open, focus goes to the first thing the rider will want: Send feedback → the message box; Leave this ride? → Save ride and open library (never the destructive choice); Diagnostics → its close button.
  • On close, focus returns to the trigger (Feedback, Library, the bike status).
  • When an overlay appears mid-ride, focus moves to its action if it has one (Reconnect) and returns when it lifts.

Labelling

Close buttons name their dialog: “Close diagnostics”, “Close feedback”. Each dialog has exactly one visible title, which is its accessible name.

Contrast

Dialog title on panel
Fact labels and body on panel
Privacy note and file name on panel

Panels are told apart from the page by their border and --shadow-4, not by colour alone, so they hold up in forced colours.

Motion

Panels open over --duration-base (200ms) with --ease-panel; under reduced motion they appear at once. Overlays never animate in. The 3-2-1 keeps its one-second steps under reduced motion (it is a timer) but drops the scale.

Touch

Close buttons are 32px (above the 24px minimum). The sheet's footer button is 36px; Leave this ride?'s actions are 40px and stack full width.

Content

  • Titles name the thing or ask the question: “Diagnostics”, “Send feedback”, “Leave this ride?”. A confirmation title is a question ending in “?”.
  • The body states the stakes and what is happening meanwhile: “You’re 6:52 in with 2.24 km ridden. The ride is paused while you decide.”
  • Actions name outcomes: “Save ride and open library”, “Discard and open library”, “Keep riding”. Never OK and Cancel.
  • A switch row explains its cost: “Turn on while reproducing a problem — it uses memory.”
  • Privacy is stated where data is shown: “Everything here is read in your browser and stays on this device until you download it.”
