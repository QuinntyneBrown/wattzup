Pattern

Empty and error states

Telo Ride is never blank: a new rider always has two maps to ride. What it does have are the gaps of a first run and the failures of the outside world (Bluetooth, the browser, the bike itself). Each one gets a sentence that says what is wrong in the rider's terms and one action that fixes it.

Overview

Empty means nothing has happened yet. The mocks treat the new rider (level 1, no rides, no stars) as every screen's empty state, and keep each screen useful: Home shows the first unlock to chase, Map Select shows two open maps, Class Library shows the starter classes, Profile says where the first race will appear.

Error means something outside Telo Ride is in the way. The app's own data is local and can't fail to load, so the errors the mocks draw are all about reaching the bike: Bluetooth is off, the browser can't do Web Bluetooth, the page is on plain http, no bike is picked, or the bike stopped answering. The one addition is the 404 page.

Components used: Empty state, Error page, Alert and banner, HUD panel, Button, App header and Table.

When to use

Pick the form by how much of the screen stops working. Block only what can't work; leave the rest usable.

Accessibility

  • Announce failures, not emptiness. Blockers, notices, panels and Lost the bike are role="alert". An empty line is ordinary text: there is nothing to announce.
  • Name the region. A blocker is a section with an h2 title and aria-labelledby, so it shows up in the headings list.
  • Say what a symbol means. The dash is aria-hidden beside “No best yet”; the red bike icon sits beside “not connected”; locked map names add “, locked”.
  • Move focus only mid-task. When Lost the bike appears during a ride, focus goes to Reconnect and comes back when the overlay lifts. A blocker present on page load doesn't take focus.
  • Leave everything else usable. Disabled controls behind an overlay are disabled, not hidden; full screen and the header stay reachable.

Motion

Overlays appear without animation. Nothing in an error state moves except the spinner of a retry in progress, which stops under reduced motion.

Touch

Recovery actions are 44px tall (Reconnect, Check again); Connect bike is 48px. They are often pressed with a hand off the bars.

Content

  • Name the problem in the rider's world: “Bluetooth is off”, “Lost the bike”, “This browser can’t reach the bike”. Never an exception name or a code.
  • Say where it works: “Chrome or Edge on a laptop or Android tablet”.
  • Say what is safe: “Your rides and progress are safe.”, “your ride is saved at 6:52”, “Your rides and settings carry over”.
  • Say what keeps going: “The class keeps going.”, “Your rider coasts for 15 seconds”.
  • One action, a verb: Try again, Reconnect, Copy link, Open over HTTPS, Connect bike, Back to Home.
  • Empty lines are hopeful and specific: “Nothing yet. Your first race shows up here, with the XP it earned.” Not “No data”.
  • Hints answer the next question: “Not in the list? Close the Echelon app on your phone — the bike talks to one device at a time.”
