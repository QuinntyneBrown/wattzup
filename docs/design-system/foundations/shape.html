Foundation

Shape

Controls 8px, wells 12px, cards 16px, pills round. Radius grows with size and steps down as shapes nest; borders are a 1px hairline.

Overview

Shape in Telo Ride is soft but not bubbly: controls at 8px, wells at 12px, cards at 16px, and pills fully round. Radius grows with the size of the thing, so a button inside a card is visibly tighter than the card around it. Borders are almost always a 1px hairline; colour and lightness do the separating, not line weight.

Which radius for which size

Pick by the element's size and role, not by taste. Nested shapes step down: a 16px card holds 12px wells, which hold 8px controls or 6px thumbnails, so the gaps between curves stay even.

Border widths

--border-width-hairline1px
--border-width-thick2px
TokenWidthUsed for
--border-width-hairline1pxEvery border: cards, dividers, inputs, banners, the header rule
--border-width-thick2pxSelection marks: the current tab and side-nav bar, current table row, avatar ring, checkbox tick; the focus ring is the same width (--focus-ring-width)

A hairline border's colour decides its job: --color-line for decoration (card edges, dividers, the header rule), --color-line-strong where the border is the only thing marking a control (inputs), which must reach 3:1.

Why six radii plus full

The extraction method keeps at most five radii plus full. Telo Ride keeps six plus full, and this is a deliberate deviation, logged as such:

  • The design is written in Tailwind and uses rounded, rounded-md, rounded-lg, rounded-xl and rounded-2xl each in many places with a clear size logic (above). Collapsing any two would change dozens of components the design draws deliberately differently (an 8px button inside a 12px well inside a 16px card).
  • --radius-xs (2px) is the sixth. It exists only for chart marks and ticks, where 4px visibly blobs a 6px-wide bar. It is not a UI radius: never put it on a control, card or tag.

So the UI itself uses five radii plus full (sm, md, lg, xl, 2xl); xs belongs to data visualisation.

Usage

  • Inner radius is smaller than outer: a control inside a well inside a card steps down lg → xl → 2xl from the inside out.
  • A bottom sheet rounds only its top corners (--radius-2xl); at 640px it becomes a centred dialog with all four.
  • Images in cards are clipped by the card (overflow: hidden), not rounded separately.
  • Never use --radius-full on a rectangular button: pill buttons are not part of Telo Ride.
  • Borders are 1px. A 2px border means "selected" or "ring", never emphasis on a resting card.
