Foundation

Motion

The 3D world is the motion. The interface around it stays quiet: colour changes on hover, standings rows slide, panels open, and one gold moment celebrates an unlock.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stableSince v1.0

Overview

During a ride the rider watches a photo-real world roll past at their own speed. Anything else that moves competes with it, so interface motion in Telo Ride is short, small and functional. It confirms that something happened (a hover, a place gained, a panel opened), and it celebrates exactly one thing: a map unlocking.

  • Confirm, never decorate. Nothing loops or bounces for attention. The spinner and the skeleton pulse are the only repeating motion, and both mean “waiting”.
  • Under a quarter of a second. Interface motion uses 150, 200 or 250 ms. Longer durations belong to timers (the 3-2-1, the interval bar), not to animation.
  • Timers are not motion. A clock that ticks once a second keeps ticking with reduced motion. The transitions around it stop.
  • Tokens only. Every transition reads --duration-* and --ease-*, so prefers-reduced-motion switches the whole product off in one place (see Accessibility).

Choreography

  • One thing at a time. Only the unlock staggers (card, then photo). Everything else moves as a single piece.
  • Exits are faster than entrances. Open on --ease-spring or --ease-enter; close on --ease-exit, a step shorter (base in, fast out).
  • Small distances. Panels scale from 96%, buttons press to 98%, steppers to 96%. Nothing flies in from off screen; dialogs on phones rise as a sheet from the bottom edge they are anchored to.
  • Numbers do not tween. Watts, cadence, heart rate, speed, gaps and the clock change once a second with the bike's notifications. Counting animations would lie about the reading in between.
  • Layout never animates mid-ride. The HUD panels stay where they are; only their contents change. A slide in the standings is a row trading places, not the list resizing.
  • Gold motion is rare. The unlock is the one moment that animates in gold. XP and stars on Results appear with the page, without their own animation.

Usage

Write every transition from the tokens. List the properties you animate; never transition: all.

/* hover colour */
.app-header__nav-link { transition: color var(--duration-fast) var(--ease-standard); }

/* rows trading places */
.race-board__row { transition: translate var(--duration-fast) var(--ease-out); }

/* a panel opening */
.feedback-context__chevron { transition: rotate var(--duration-base) var(--ease-panel); }

/* a timer: linear, one second per step, survives reduced motion */
.race-interval__fill { transition: width var(--duration-countdown) linear; }
  • Animate opacity, transform, translate, scale, rotate and colours. Avoid animating width, height or top, except the interval fill, which is a timer.
  • No raw milliseconds in components.css or on a page; the checker and review reject them.
  • No motion longer than --duration-slow unless it is a timer step.

Accessibility: reduced motion

When the rider asks for reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce, WCAG 2.3.3), tokens.css sets --duration-fast, --duration-base, --duration-slow, --duration-loop and --duration-pulse to 0ms. Components need no media queries of their own. --duration-deliberate (and so --duration-countdown) is left alone, because the 3-2-1 and the interval bar are timers: their pace is information, not decoration.

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  :root { --duration-loop: 0ms; --duration-pulse: 0ms; --duration-fast: 0ms; --duration-base: 0ms; --duration-slow: 0ms; }
}
MomentWith reduced motion
Hover, press, switchesThe new colour or position appears at once; there is no press scale.
Standings rowsThe list re-renders in its new order without sliding.
Panels, popovers, chevronsThey appear and disappear at once, already in place.
The unlockThe card appears at its end state, in colour, with no scale.
The 3-2-1Still one digit per second (a timer). Each digit appears without fading or scaling.
Interval barStill fills once a second; the fill jumps rather than glides.
Spinner, skeletonThey stand still. The visible label (“Searching…”, “Pairing…”) or the empty block carries the meaning.
The 3D worldFrozen: the camera holds the frame the mocks show, and distance, clock and standings keep updating. Class and custom-ride videos keep playing, because the video is the class.
  • Nothing flashes more than three times a second (2.3.1). The 3-2-1 changes once a second.
  • Pause, stop, hide (2.2.2): the spinner and skeleton stop as soon as the wait ends. The world and the class video are the activity itself; a solo ride can be paused, a race or a group class cannot (see Accessibility).
  • components.css also carries a blanket prefers-reduced-motion rule that shortens every animation and transition to 0.01 ms as a safety net. That rule also flattens the timer transitions; the timers themselves keep ticking because the clock, not CSS, drives them.
