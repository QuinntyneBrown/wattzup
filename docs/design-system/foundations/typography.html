Overview

Three families, each with one job. Inter Tight sets everything you read: titles, labels, body. Barlow Condensed sets everything you watch change: watts, cadence, times, positions, and in black italic capitals the wordmark. JetBrains Mono appears only where a rider reads machine output: the diagnostics panel, the packet log and the render backend tag. The scale is Tailwind's, which is what the design was drawn with, and every step below 5xl carries its paired line height.

Measure

Prose stops at --layout-measure (65ch), documented on Layout. In the product most text is short; the places that run long are the Connect lead, the lost-bike notice, dialog bodies and the diagnostics privacy note. Cards narrower than the measure simply wrap. Long names (maps, classes, riders) truncate with an ellipsis on one line inside tiles and boards and wrap everywhere else.

Pedal once to wake it up. Telo Ride reads your power and sets resistance for every hill and interval. Everything here is read in your browser and stays on this device until you download it.

65ch at 16px is about 600px of Inter Tight.

Usage

  • Anything that changes during a ride or is a measured value (watts, rpm, bpm, km/h, times, positions, XP counts) is Barlow with tabular figures, so digits do not jump as they tick.
  • Units sit beside the numeral in Inter Tight at a smaller size and muted colour ("87 rpm"), never inside the numeral.
  • Inter Tight 14px medium is the UI's working size for labels and buttons; 16px is body.
  • Sentence case everywhere. Uppercase only for the wordmark, .wide and overlines.
  • Mono only for machine output a rider might read back to support (IDs, URLs, timings).
  • Use a role shorthand when the whole decision fits; never mix a shorthand with a hand-set family.

Accessibility

The 11px exception

Body text is never below 16px and interface text never below 12px, with one exception: --font-size-2xs (11px) for text that repeats information available another way or that labels a chart. It is used for the XP label under the header bar (the link's accessible name already says "Level 7, 1840 of 2600 XP"), chart axes and scales (.plan-preview__axis, .level-slider__scale, .ride-ticks), the race bar's backend tag and the ride log link. Never use it for anything a rider must read to act.

  • All sizes are in rem, so the whole scale follows the browser's font size; layouts are tested at 200% zoom.
  • Text spacing overrides (1.4.12) do not clip: line heights are set, heights are not, except on numerals whose box is sized to them.
  • Numerals that update live are announced through the component's live region at a sensible rate, never every tick; the type itself carries no semantics.
  • Fonts are bundled with font-display: swap; the fallbacks (ui-sans-serif, ui-monospace) keep the layout readable if a file fails.
