Foundation

Elevation

On a dark product lightness does the lifting: ink, panel, raised. Shadows only for what floats, glass for the ride HUD, and ten named layers.

Overview

Telo Ride is dark, and shadows barely show on near-black, so elevation is carried mostly by surface lightness: ink, then panel, then raised, each a step lighter. Shadows are kept for things that truly float over other content (tooltips, toasts, dialogs, menus), and on the ride screens the HUD floats as glass: translucent ink with a blur, so the world stays visible behind the numbers. Every layer has a z-index token; no component writes a raw z-index.

Shadows

Four levels plus focus. The light theme mixes them from a cool slate at low opacity; dark uses black at much higher opacity, because a soft shadow on ink is otherwise invisible. Both are needed: the shadow separates an overlay from what is under it, the panel lightness says it is a surface.

The source used a fifth popover shadow (0 24px 60px -12px); it was folded into --shadow-4 (0 25px 50px -12px), which is visually the same. --shadow-diagnostics and --feedback-shadow are area aliases of --shadow-4.

Surface hierarchy

ink < panel < raised. On dark each step is lighter: the higher a surface, the more light it catches. On paper the canvas is warm off-white, panels are pure white and lift by contrast with the canvas, and raised wells are a warm grey that reads as recessed into the white card. The meaning is the same in both: a well sits inside a card, a card sits on the page.

HUD glass over photos

On the race the 3D world is the page, so panels cannot be opaque. HUD glass is --color-glass, ink at 80% (the design's bg-ink/70 backdrop-blur-md, deepened for contrast), with a 12px backdrop blur. It is the same in both themes: the world behind it does not change with the theme, so neither does the glass, and a HUD panel sets data-theme="dark" so its text, muted labels and accents resolve to their dark values on paper pages too.

Contrast on glass. Measured on --color-glass with nothing but pure white behind it (the worst case), bone text (--color-fg, dark) reads 9.7:1 and muted 4.3:1. Over the race's own sky (--race-sky) muted reads 5.7:1. So values and anything a rider must read are --color-fg; muted is for units and captions that repeat what the number says. .race-glass in components.css still mixes 70% of the theme's ink (muted 2.9:1 worst case, and paper on light pages); it should read --color-glass.

Glass is for the ride HUD only. Off the ride, floating things are opaque panels with a shadow; never put glass over text, where the blur makes both layers unreadable. Where backdrop-filter is unsupported, the 80% ink alone still carries the contrast.

Scrims

A scrim dims the page behind something modal and catches the outside click. --color-scrim is ink at 60% on dark (45% on paper) behind dialogs and sheets. --color-scrim-strong (ink 85%) sits behind text on photos (photo tags) and the full-screen covers that must hide the world: the 3-2-1 countdown, Lost the bike, paused. The "Leave this ride?" popover uses a transparent scrim at --z-hud: it catches the click but keeps the ride visible.

Z-order

Ten layers, lowest first. Components set z-index: var(--z-*) and nothing else; a stacking problem is solved by choosing the right layer, never by a bigger number.

Why toasts sit above modals: "Feedback sent" and reconnect results arrive while a dialog may be open and must not hide behind it. Why the HUD is low: everything the rider opens mid-ride (menus, dialogs, diagnostics) must cover it.

Elevation in the dark theme

  • Lightness, not shadow, separates levels: panel is a step above ink, raised a step above panel. Never put a raised well directly on ink without a panel between; the step is too small to read.
  • Floating surfaces add a hairline (--color-line) as well as the shadow, because a black shadow on near-black is a faint halo at best.
  • Dark shadows are 0.4–0.65 opacity against light's 0.08–0.25 so they still register at the edges of a dialog.
  • Hover on a raised fill moves one step up (--color-raised-hover = line on dark), never darker.

Usage

  • Cards in the product carry no shadow: panel on ink is enough. Add --shadow-1 only where a card sits on paper and needs to lift.
  • Pair each shadow with its layer: --shadow-2 tooltip, --shadow-3 toast, --shadow-4 anything modal or anchored.
  • Glass only on the ride, only over the world.
  • Every modal layer has a scrim; every scrim closes its layer on click unless the layer is a confirmation that must be answered.
